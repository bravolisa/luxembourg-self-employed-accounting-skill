## Rules people get wrong most often

- A car kept as a business asset is sold with VAT on the **whole** price, even when it was bought without VAT from a private person; and its private use is paid for once – full deduction with yearly VAT on the private km, or the business share only. [C · S66, S69]
- Equipment contributed to your own company as share capital is **taxed like a sale for VAT** (it stays a contribution – nothing has to be sold): 17% on the value in the deed, with an invoice – unless the whole business passes, and then no VAT may be shown at all. [C · S89, S24 for the rule; I for equipment contributed by a sole trader]
- **3% for courses** only when the course is a short « manifestation » with people in the room (generally a week at most); weekly or term-long courses and online participation are at 17%, whatever the advertising or the invoice wording (rates.md). [C · S59, S24]
- **VAT shown is VAT owed**: anyone who shows VAT on an invoice owes it, even for an exempt supply or under the franchise (art. 61(8); invoicing.md). [C · S24, S57]
- The €50,000 franchise threshold counts **supplies located in Luxembourg**, not total income. Services to business customers abroad do not count; most services to **private** customers do, even outside the EU – except listed services such as consulting, legal, accounting, advertising or data processing for private customers outside the EU, and electronic or streamed services taxed where the customer lives. Goods exports count. [C · S1, S24 arts 17, 57; details in profiles-and-thresholds.md]
- Turnover counts when the supply is made, not when it is paid: a client paying late does not keep someone under the threshold. [I · S6]
- Between €50,000 and €55,000 the franchise holds until 31 December; above €55,000 it ends the day after the sale that crosses it. Either way the next year is in the normal regime. [C · S1]
- A franchise user who buys **services** from a foreign business owes Luxembourg VAT on them under reverse charge from the first euro, cannot deduct it, and must file the simplified return. [I · S1, S6]
- In the first year after leaving the franchise, VAT on purchases is deductible from the switch date for taxed activity; it is **not** a pro-rata share of the year. [I · S6]
- On the normal annual return, services to non-EU **business** customers (and the listed services to private customers outside the EU) go in **box 019** ("other supplies carried out abroad"), not box 014 (exports of goods). On the simplified return there is **no box** for them. [C · S2, S3]
- Franchise turnover in a year that also had normal-regime months goes in **box 481** of the annual return (footnote 17). [C · S3]
- A VAT number does not mean the person charges VAT: franchise users have one too: every taxable person established in Luxembourg declares the start of its activity and gets a VAT number (art. 62(1) 1°, 62(5)(a)), and the franchise users' exemption from registering was deleted in 2013. Guichet's VAT-registration page (23 Jan 2025) still lists them under optional registration – out of date. [C · S24 art. 62; S48 art. 16]
- An invoice without VAT is not automatically reverse charge, and a foreign hotel, restaurant or event bill is not reverse charge at all: it is taxed where it takes place, and that foreign VAT is never Luxembourg input tax. [C · S17; see purchases.md]
- Reverse charge is reported on **both** sides even when fully deductible (e.g. 741/742 and 461). With a pro-rata, the full VAT goes in the input box and the non-deductible part in 095 – not just the net. [C · S3, S14 boxes; I method]
- An embassy's or EU body's request is not enough to drop VAT: the exemption needs the certificate **before** the supply (for missions in Luxembourg: visa'd by the Foreign Ministry and the AED, at least €120 excl. VAT per supply, a set invoice mention); then box 015 with deduction. A seller already exempt under art. 44 stays art. 44 – box 016, no deduction. [C · S61; I · S62]
- A franchise user with an EU **business** client files the recapitulative statements and the simplified annual return itself – nobody sends them (franchise.md). A customer without a VAT number, an international organisation included, is not a business customer: the service stays in Luxembourg under the franchise and counts towards €50,000. [C · S1, S24 arts 17(1)(a), 57, 57bis(4)]
- Franchise users owe VAT on EU goods purchases only once those exceed €10,000 in the current or previous year (art. 2(2)(b)); this has nothing to do with the €50,000 threshold. [C · S15]

## Answering cross-border questions

The place of supply decides which country's VAT applies; lu-vat then says what the Luxembourg return and invoice must show. Typical split:

- eu-vat: "Is this service taxed in Luxembourg, in the client's country, or nowhere in the EU?"
- lu-vat: "Then which box, which invoice wording, which return?"
