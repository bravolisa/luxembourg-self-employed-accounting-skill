# Activities exempt by nature (art. 44)

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

These businesses charge no VAT at any turnover. They are not "franchise" users, and no turnover threshold applies to them. [C · S6]

## Typical cases (Luxembourg self-employed)

- **Health**: doctors, dentists, physiotherapists, midwives, nurses, psychotherapists, speech therapists, dieticians; dental technicians. Since the 2022 reform only care with a **therapeutic purpose** is exempt: cosmetic procedures, wellness and expert reports are taxable, so one doctor can be partly taxable. [C · S6]
- **Education**: independent teachers giving private lessons at **school or university level** are exempt. A business coach or driving instructor is taxable. [C · S6] Adult language courses not tied to school or university curricula: probably taxable. [I] The AED limits « leçons » to the transmission of school or university-level knowledge, and the exemption for professional training to bodies authorised and supervised by the authorities or created by law. [C · S59] Taxable courses: which rate – rates.md.
- **Insurance**: agents and brokers on commissions. **Finance**: credit intermediaries. [C · S6]
- **Property**: residential letting (not a self-employed activity in itself). Short-term furnished letting is a different case (3% accommodation rate, possibly commercial). [C · S6]
- **Not exempt despite common confusion**: IT freelancers, consultants, architects, lawyers, accountants, coaches, artists. They are taxable (franchise if small). [C · S6]

## Duties

- Dispensed from VAT registration by default: no VAT number, nothing to file. [C · S6]
- Must register **before** the first: purchase of services from a foreign business (reverse charge, first euro); intra-EU goods purchases above €10,000; or B2B services supplied to EU customers. A business wholly exempt under art. 44 without any right to deduct is released from the start-of-activity declaration (RGD of 1 December 2009, art. 2 d)) but must ask for a VAT number before such operations (art. 3(1)). [C · S53] Then file the annual return and, for EU B2B services, recapitulative statements. [C · S6] Where the exempt turnover goes on that return is unclear: its box 012 may only hold 481 + 482 (VAT-O-15). [C · S47; O]
- Mixed taxable and exempt activity: input VAT deduction is restricted (pro rata). [I]
