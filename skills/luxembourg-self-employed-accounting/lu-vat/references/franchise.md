# The VAT franchise (art. 57bis)

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

The franchise exempts small businesses from charging Luxembourg VAT on their Luxembourg supplies. It does not take them out of the VAT system: they keep a VAT number, can owe VAT on purchases from abroad, and may have to file a simplified return.

Not open to VAT groups, flat-rate farmers for their farm supplies, a business that opted to tax lettings (art. 45), or occasional operations (art. 4(5)); sales of new means of transport to another EU country are never covered. [C · S24 art. 57bis(2), (3)]

## 1. What a franchise user cannot do

- Show VAT on invoices. Simplified invoices are allowed from 2025, but without VAT [C · S1 p. 10] – except for goods sent to another EU country and for supplies where the customer abroad owes the VAT (reverse charge), which need a full invoice. [C · S24 art. 63(10) 2°]
- Recover VAT paid on purchases. [C · S1 p. 3] VAT paid is part of the cost of the expense or asset. A car: cars.md, section 10.

## 2. Buying from abroad – allowed, with obligations

| Purchase | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Services from a business in another EU country or outside the EU (software, ads, platforms, freelancers) | Reverse charge: the franchise user owes Luxembourg VAT on the service from the first euro (boxes 741/742 for EU suppliers, 751/752 for non-EU suppliers at 17%), cannot deduct it, and must file the simplified return | [C · S1 p. 9, S2 section D] |
| Goods from another EU country, total ≤ €10,000 in the current and the previous year | The supplier charges its own VAT (or Luxembourg VAT via OSS if it is a large webshop); nothing to declare in Luxembourg | [C · S8; I · S7] |
| Goods from another EU country above €10,000 | Once EU goods purchases exceed €10,000 in the current year, or exceeded it in the previous year, they are intra-Community acquisitions: Luxembourg VAT self-assessed (boxes 711/712 at 17%), not deductible. So crossing the threshold also taxes all purchases of the **next** year. The user must inform the AED and give suppliers the LU VAT number. The €10,000 relief never covers new means of transport, or excise goods (energy products, alcohol and alcoholic drinks, manufactured tobacco) on which excise duty is due in Luxembourg: those are always intra-Community acquisitions taxed here | Rule [C · S15 art. 2(2)(b)]; vehicles and excise goods [C · S24 arts 2(1)(b), 2(2)(b), 2bis]; treatment of the crossing purchase itself and the practical steps [I · S7] |
| Opting in below €10,000 | Possible; the option covers two calendar years. Worth it only when the supplier country's VAT is higher than Luxembourg's rate for the goods | Option [C · S15 art. 2(2)]; advice [I · S7] |
| Goods from an EU supplier who is under its own country's small-business franchise (invoice without VAT) | Not an intra-Community acquisition in Luxembourg: nothing to declare | [C · S24 art. 2(1)(b)(i)] |
| Goods from outside the EU | Import VAT (at customs, or boxes 721/722 when declared on the return); not deductible | [I] |
| Hotel, restaurant, conference or fuel abroad, with that country's VAT | Taxed abroad; not a Luxembourg reverse charge. The foreign VAT is part of the cost: it cannot be deducted in Luxembourg, and franchise users are excluded from the EU refund procedure | [C · S16, S1]; place of supply of the services [C · S17] |

Practical point for users: "I don't charge VAT, so VAT doesn't concern me" is wrong as soon as they buy services from a foreign business. Many common subscriptions (video calls, design tools, online ads) are invoiced from another EU country.

## 3. Selling abroad

| Sale | Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Services to a business in another EU country | Taxed in the customer's country; the customer pays VAT (reverse charge). Report in box 423 of the simplified return and in the recapitulative statements | [C · S1, S2] |
| Services to a business outside the EU (UK, US, UAE…) | Outside the scope of Luxembourg VAT; no box on the simplified return; do not count towards €50,000 | [C · S2; I · S1] |
| Services to an international organisation, EU body or other non-business customer in another EU country **without a VAT number** | Not a business customer for place of supply: only a non-taxable legal person « identifiée à la TVA » counts as one (art. 17(1)(a)). So the general private-customer rule applies – taxed in Luxembourg, covered by the franchise, no reverse charge and no recapitulative statement. It counts towards €50,000, also when it is exempt under art. 43(1)(k) (art. 57, point 2 b)). Invoice with the art. 57bis mention; simplified return box 481 | [C · S24 arts 17(1)(a), 17(1)(c), 57]; box 481 [I · S2] |
| The same customer **with** a VAT number | A business customer: taxed in its country; if it is exempt there (e.g. an international organisation), box 424 of the simplified return, not 423, and no recapitulative statement | [C · S24 art. 17(1)(a); S2]; not on the statement [I · eu-vat S6] |
| Most services to private customers in the EU | Taxed in Luxembourg (supplier's country) under the general rule → covered by the franchise and count towards €50,000 | [C · S24 art. 17(1)(c)] |
| Services to private customers outside the EU | Taxed in Luxembourg and count towards €50,000 – unless listed in art. 17(2) 8° (consultants, engineers, lawyers, accountants, advertising, data processing and information, rights and licences, staff hire, rental of movable goods), which are taxed outside the EU and do not count | [C · S24 art. 17] |
| B2C electronic services above the EU-wide threshold (eu-vat, oss.md); online events and education streamed to private customers since 2025 (no threshold for these) | Taxed in the customer's country → not covered by the Luxembourg franchise. Use the EU SME scheme (EX) where the other country offers a franchise, or OSS | [C · S24 art. 17(2) 4° b), 7bis, art. 19quater; see eu-vat] |
| Goods to private customers in the EU above the EU-wide threshold (eu-vat, oss.md) | Taxed in the customer's country → OSS or local registration | [C · S6] |
| Goods exported outside the EU | Count towards the €50,000 | [C · S6] |
| Goods sent to a business in another EU country (buyer with a VAT number) | **Not** an exempt intra-Community supply: that exemption does not apply to franchise users (art. 43(1)(d)). The sale is a Luxembourg supply covered by the franchise; the buyer has no intra-Community acquisition to declare. Full invoice (section 1) with the art. 57bis mention. Whether it counts towards €50,000 and goes on a recapitulative statement: open point VAT-O-12 – count it to be safe | Exemption excluded [C · S24 art. 43(1)(d)]; the rest [I · S24 arts 2(1)(b)(i), 57bis(1), (5)] |

Franchise at home and OSS abroad can be combined. [C · S6] Splitting one activity between a sole trader and his own SARL-S so that each stays under the franchise limit: risky, open point VAT-O-17.

### Typical case: a coach selling to private clients in Luxembourg, the EU and beyond

| How the coaching is given | Client in Luxembourg | Client in another EU country | Client outside the EU |
|---|---|---|---|
| In person, in Luxembourg | Franchise – no VAT | Franchise – no VAT (general rule) | No VAT: general rule (Luxembourg) or, if coaching counts as « conseillers … et autres prestations similaires » (art. 17(2) 8° c)), outside the EU – only the €50,000 count differs; count it to be safe |
| One-to-one by video | Franchise – no VAT | Franchise – no VAT on the general rule, with the caveat of eu-vat EUVAT-O-03 | As in person |
| Online group sessions or webinars | Franchise – no VAT | Client's country from the first euro (art. 17(2) 4° b)) – not the Luxembourg franchise; usually still no VAT through the EX scheme while under that country's limit and €100,000 EU-wide (eu-vat sme-cross-border.md), otherwise that country's VAT via OSS | No Luxembourg VAT; local rules of the client's country may apply |
| Recorded courses, downloads | Franchise – no VAT | Luxembourg while cross-border electronic services (plus distance sales of goods) stay under €10,000 this year and last → franchise; above, the client's country | Client's country (art. 17(2) 7bis) – no Luxembourg VAT |

A session organised for people in the room in Luxembourg stays a Luxembourg supply even if a few join by video; a workshop held abroad is taxed where it takes place (eu-vat place-of-supply.md). Keep, per client, the country and how the session was given. Before selling online group sessions or recorded courses to EU clients, review the VAT treatment first. [I · S24 art. 17; eu-vat S19, S21]

## 4. Invoices

- Supplies located in Luxembourg: the mention « TVA non applicable – Article 57bis de la loi modifiée du 12 février 1979 ». Quote it exactly. [C · S1; S24 art. 57bis(5)]
- Private customers: the VAT law does not require an invoice to be issued at all for services to private individuals – the duty to issue one covers supplies to another business or to a « personne morale non assujettie » (art. 63(4) 1°), so an international organisation or other non-business legal person does get one – but an invoice that is issued for a supply taxed in Luxembourg – most services to private customers in Luxembourg, elsewhere in the EU and many outside it (section 3) – must carry the same art. 57bis mention, and the amount goes in box 481. "Private customer, so VAT does not concern me" is wrong: the supply is under the franchise and counts towards €50,000. [C · S24 arts 57bis(5), 63(4) 1°; S2]
- EU business customers: a full invoice with the mention « Autoliquidation » (or the term of another language version of the VAT Directive, e.g. "Reverse charge") and the customer's VAT number, besides one's own. [C · S24 art. 63(8) 3°, 4°, 14°]
- Non-EU customers: a short reason such as "Not subject to Luxembourg VAT – place of supply outside Luxembourg" (good practice, not an official phrase). [I]
More: invoicing.md.

## 5. Returns and other duties

- **Turnover notice**: a franchise user informs the AED in writing of the previous year's turnover before 1 March [C · S4; S48 art. 5bis(2)] – by e-mail to the competent VAT office or by post. [C · S39] The law asks it of every holder « soumis au seul régime de franchise national », also one who files the simplified return. [C · S24 art. 57bis(4)]
- **Simplified annual return** (eCDF, before 1 March of the following year) is **mandatory** when the user supplies intra-Community services, or owes VAT as a recipient (art. 61), e.g. reverse charge on foreign services. [C · S1 p. 9; S24 art. 57bis(4)] Nobody sends it: the user files it in eCDF for every year with such a service.
- **Recapitulative statements** for intra-Community services: the user files them in eCDF, monthly or quarterly, before the 25th of the following month, one line per EU business customer with its VAT number; only for periods with such services; the AED does not send them (eu-vat, reverse-charge-and-reporting.md). [C · S1 p. 9; eu-vat S6]
- **Declarations to the AED** (art. 62(2)): a franchise user declares that it makes taxable EU goods purchases (above the €10,000 relief, or after opting in), supplies services in another EU country where the customer owes the VAT, or receives services on which it owes the VAT (art. 61(5)). [C · S24 art. 62(2)]
- Box 423 of the simplified return must equal the total of the recapitulative statements. [C · S2]
- Boxes: forms/2025/vat-returns.md (sections 1, 4 and 9). Assessing a single purchase invoice: purchases.md.

## Joining the franchise

The AED asks for the franchise to be requested: a business that starts asks for it in its initial declaration (« déclaration initiale »); a normal-regime business that qualifies asks its tax office, and the switch takes effect on 1 January of the next year. [C · S1] (The law itself words the exemption as applying by law – « Sont exonérées », art. 57bis(1). [C · S24])

## 6. Leaving the franchise voluntarily

On request to the tax office, with the AED's form; it takes effect on the first day of the month after the AED receives it – for a business that has just started, from the start of the activity if the request is filed within one month. The user stays in the normal regime for at least one calendar year. [C · S1 p. 13; S24 art. 57bis(8)] The AED releases the user from that minimum year when the conditions of the activity change essentially during it. [C · S24 art. 57bis(8)] Joining the franchise from the normal regime: switching-regime.md. Worth considering for heavy exporters of goods or businesses with large VAT-bearing purchases, who would then recover input VAT. [I]
