# Which VAT rate – the cases settled so far

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

The four rates and their annexes: 17% standard; 8% (annex A), 3% (annex B), 14% (annex C) (art. 39(3), art. 40). Anything not listed in an annex is at 17%. [C · S24] An exempt supply is not a 0% supply (exempt-art44.md). The full catalogue is still open: VAT-O-06. This file collects the cases settled one by one.

## Courses, conferences, seminars, events – the 3% rate (annex B, point 12°)

The law: 3% for « Octroi du droit d'accéder aux concerts, représentations théâtrales, chorégraphiques et cinématographiques, aux spectacles, divertissements, conférences, cours et autres manifestations à caractère scientifique, culturel, éducatif, économique ou professionnel » and to museums, archives, botanical or zoological gardens, nature parks and circuses. [C · S24 annex B, point 12°]

The AED reads it narrowly (circular n° 808, after the EU Court's Srf konsulterna judgment) [C · S59]:
- a « cours » counts only if it is a **« manifestation »**: planned in advance, one indivisible block, in one place, over a **short** period – as a rule not more than **one week** – on a set subject;
- the participants must be **physically present** (« ne vise donc que les activités impliquant la présence physique des participants »);
- a course spread over weeks or months, in separate sessions, especially with preparation or a test between sessions, is ongoing training, **not** a manifestation – that alone rules out 3%;
- the subject must be scientific, cultural, educational, economic or professional: a sports course never gets this 3% (sports facilities are point 13°);
- the AED's own example: a language school, courses open to anyone who signs up and advertised on its own website, groups of 4–8 or one-to-one, 2 hours a week for 3–6 months → **not** a manifestation → **17%**.

What follows [I · S59]:
- Open to the public and advertised on a website does **not** make a course 3%; the AED's example had both.
- Calling the price « droits de participation » changes nothing: what is supplied decides, not the word on the invoice.
- A one-day or few-day seminar, workshop or conference with a set programme and people in the room: 3%.
- Weekly classes, coaching programmes, language courses over a term, one-to-one lessons: 17% – unless exempt as school or university-level lessons (exempt-art44.md).

**Online**: Directive 2022/542 let member states apply the reduced rate to « l'accès à la diffusion en direct » of such events from 2025; Luxembourg did not take that option – annex B, point 12° is unchanged in the 2026 law – so online participation is at 17%, even for an event whose in-room participants pay 3%. [C · S24 annex B; S60 for the option not taken]

A franchise user charges no VAT at all; the rate matters once the business leaves the franchise (switching-regime.md). [C · S24 art. 57]

## Typical case: a weekend workshop and a weekly course

A trainer under the normal regime runs (invented figures):
- a two-day workshop in a rented room in Luxembourg, programme published, €300 a place → a manifestation → **3%**;
- the same content as a 10-week evening course, one evening a week → not a manifestation → **17%**;
- the two-day workshop streamed live to people at home → **17%** for the online places (and, for private customers in other EU countries, their country's VAT – eu-vat place-of-supply.md).
