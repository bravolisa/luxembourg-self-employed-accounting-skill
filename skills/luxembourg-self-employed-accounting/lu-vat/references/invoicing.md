## When an invoice is required, and by when

- Required for supplies to another business or to a non-taxable legal person, for goods sent to another EU country, for distance sales of goods, and for advances received before such supplies. The VAT law does not require one for services to private individuals. [C · S24 art. 63(4) 1°]
- Issue it **at the latest on the 15th of the month after** the supply; for an advance on a supply not yet made, at the latest when the advance is received. Where an invoice is required, VAT becomes due when it is issued in that time, or when the time runs out. [C · S24 arts 63(5), 24(1)]

Careful with private customers abroad: "no VAT" is clear for business customers. For private individuals, online services may be taxable in the customer's country (eu-vat).

## Numbering

- Each invoice needs « un numéro séquentiel, basé sur une ou plusieurs séries, qui identifie la facture de façon unique » (a sequential number, based on one or more series, which uniquely identifies the invoice). [C · S24 art. 63(8) 2°; same wording as EU VAT Directive art. 226(2), S5]
- Several series are allowed; the choice "lies with the business", for example "for each branch, or for each type of supply or for each customer". Letters may be part of the number. This also covers self-billed invoices and invoices issued by third parties (e.g. a platform invoicing in the user's name). [C · S5 – Commission guidance, not law]
- Separate series for invoices with and without VAT: allowed, not required. [C · S5]
- Several invoicing systems (software, platform, manual): give each its own clearly different series (2026-001, PL-2026-001, M-2026-001) so no number can repeat, and keep one overview list. [I]
- Never reuse a number. The law asks for a unique sequential number, not in so many words for "no gaps"; still avoid gaps, because a gap invites questions in an audit. One series is usually simplest. [I · S24 art. 63(8) 2°]

## VAT shown is VAT owed

- Whoever shows VAT on an invoice owes it: « La taxe est due par toute personne qui mentionne la taxe sur la valeur ajoutée sur une facture » (art. 61(8)) – also when the supply was exempt, outside VAT or under the franchise. [C · S24]
- It can only be undone by correcting the invoice, and only if the issuer acted in good faith or removed in time every risk that the customer deducts it. The Cour d'appel applied this in 2015 to a building contributed to a company: the contribution deed showed VAT, so the VAT was owed whether or not the contribution was taxable. [C · S57]
- So: never show VAT on an invoice or deed unless it is due; a franchise user shows the art. 57bis mention instead (franchise.md).
