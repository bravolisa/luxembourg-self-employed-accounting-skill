## 1. The short answer

- **Normal regime:** the VAT on the car and on its costs can be recovered as far as the car serves the taxed business. Luxembourg has no special exclusion and no price cap for cars; the only limit is luxury spending (section 7). [C · S24 arts 48–54]
- **Private use is paid for once:** either deduct everything and pay VAT every year on the private share, or deduct only the business share from the start. Never both. [C · S69; S24 art. 16(a)]
- **Franchise:** nothing is recovered and nothing is due on private use (section 10). [C · S24 art. 57bis(5)]
- **One kilometre record** serves VAT and income tax: total km, private km, business km.

## 2. Three ways to treat a mixed-use car

The person chooses when buying the car. The Court of Justice: « l'intéressé a le choix, pour les besoins de la TVA, soit d'affecter ce bien en totalité au patrimoine de son entreprise, soit de le conserver en totalité dans son patrimoine privé », or to bring it in only for the business share. [C · S69; S66 for a car]

| Route | VAT on the purchase | Private use | A later sale | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) The whole car in the business | Deducted in full, at once | VAT every year on the private share (section 6) | VAT on the whole price | [C · S63, S69, S66; S24 art. 16(a)] |
| (b) Only the business share in the business | Only that share is deducted | Nothing to declare for the car | VAT only on that share of the price | [C · S64, S66; I for Luxembourg – no text names it] |
| (c) The car stays private | Nothing deducted | No VAT | Outside VAT | [C · S66] |

- The annual return is built for route (a): appendix B carries the private use to box 010. Luxembourg wrote the « business share only » rule into the law for buildings only (art. 48(1bis)), not for cars. [C · S24, S3; I for the conclusion]
- **No minimum business share:** the right to deduct exists « si réduite que soit la proportion de l'utilisation à des fins professionnelles ». [C · S63] The Luxembourg law has no threshold. [I – arts 48–54 read]
- **Running costs are a separate question from the car itself:** under every route the VAT on fuel, repairs and the like is deductible as far as the cost serves the taxed business. [C · S66; S24 art. 48(1)]
- **Showing the choice:** no form exists. Indications: the invoice in the business's name, the VAT deducted in the return of the purchase period, the car in the books as a fixed asset, appendix B of the annual return. A Member State may refuse the deduction when the allocation was not visible by the deadline of the annual return. [C · S71; I for the list]
- **Changing later:** a car bought privately gives no deduction afterwards – the adjustment rules cannot create one. [C · S63] Moving a business car to private property is possible at any time, as a withdrawal (section 8). [C · S24 art. 13(a)]

## 3. Buying the car

A car is **new** for VAT when it is delivered six months or less after its first entry into service, or has driven 6,000 km or less – one of the two is enough. [C · S24 art. 4(4)(b); S85]

| Case | Who pays which VAT | How | Deductible (normal regime, business share) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Luxembourg dealer, VAT on the invoice | The dealer charges 17% | Nothing as buyer | Yes – annual return box 081 | [C · S24 art. 48(1)(a); S3] |
| **New** car from another EU country – normal-regime business | The seller invoices without VAT; the buyer owes Luxembourg VAT of 17% | Own VAT return, boxes 711 / 712; no payment at customs | Yes – box 082 (periodic 459) | [C · S24 arts 2(1)(b), 61(6); S48 art. 18; S78] |
| New car from another EU country – franchise user | Always taxed in Luxembourg: the €10,000 relief never covers a new car | Through a VAT return (simplified return, boxes 711 / 712) – not confirmed for the franchise | No | [C · S24 art. 2(2)(b); I for the route – VAT-O-22] |
| New car from another EU country – private person | 17% in Luxembourg | Declaration 446-L at a customs centre within 15 days | No | [C · S48 arts 16–17; S77; S78] |
| **Used** car from a dealer under the margin scheme (Luxembourg or EU) | No VAT is shown; the invoice says « Régime particulier – Biens d'occasion » | Nothing on the return | No – nothing to deduct | [C · S24 arts 56ter-1, 56ter-3, 63(8) 17°; S78] |
| Used car from an EU dealer who invoices without VAT on the buyer's VAT number | The buyer owes Luxembourg VAT of 17% | Boxes 711 / 712 | Yes – box 082 | [C · S78; S24 art. 61(6)] |
| Used car from a private person | No VAT | Nothing | Nothing to deduct | [C · S24 art. 2(1)(a); S78] |
| Car imported from outside the EU | Customs duty of 10%, and import VAT of 17% on value plus duty and costs | A taxable person pays the import VAT through the return (boxes 721 / 722), not at customs | Yes – box 083 (periodic 460), with the import document in the business's name | [C · S79; S24 arts 34, 48(3)(c), 61(7)] |

- A car coming from abroad is registered only with the customs document **vignette 705**, also when no Luxembourg VAT is due; a VAT-identified business need not present the car if it hands in the signed undertaking. [C · S78, S83, S84]
- A franchise user buying a used car from an EU dealer: the €10,000 rule of franchise.md applies; one car usually exceeds it. [C · S24 art. 2(2)(b); I]
- A car bought without deductible VAT can still be kept as a business asset – but then its sale is taxed on the whole price (section 8). Decide before buying. [C · S66]

## 4. Leasing

| Contract | For VAT | VAT | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hire-purchase (« location-vente », « vente à tempérament »): ownership passes at the latest with the last instalment | A supply of goods at hand-over | 17% on the whole price at delivery; deducted like a purchase | [C · S24 arts 12(a), 22(1)] |
| Ordinary lease (operating lease, long-term rental) | A service | 17% on each rent, period by period | [C · S24 arts 15, 22(1); S81] |
| Lease with a purchase option only | Not named in the law: a service until the option is used; the option price is then a sale of the car | 17% on each rent, then on the option price | [I – medium · VAT-O-23] |
| Higher first rent, advance | Part of the price of the hire | 17% when paid | [I · S24 arts 23, 29] |

- **Deduction and private use:** the two routes of section 2 apply to the rents. The AED treats a leased car as an asset allocated to the business, and private use is declared when the VAT on the leasing was deducted; appendix B has its own line, « Leasing / renting » (boxes 378 / 379). [C · S36, S46 – written for cars given to employees; S3; I for the owner's own use]
- **Lessor in another country, lease of more than 30 days:** taxed in Luxembourg, where the customer is established; the lessor invoices without VAT and the customer owes the 17% (reverse charge) – boxes 741 / 742 (EU lessor) or 751 / 752 (non-EU), deducted for the business share. A franchise user owes the same VAT without deducting it and must file the simplified return. [C · S24 arts 17(1)(b), 61(5), 57bis(4)]
- **Short hire (30 days or less):** taxed where the car is handed over. A rental car abroad carries that country's VAT (refund procedure, section 5). [C · S24 art. 17(2) 7°]

## 5. Running costs and charging

| Cost | Luxembourg VAT | Deductible (business share) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Petrol, diesel | 17% | Yes | Rate [I – not in the reduced-rate annexes]; deduction [C · S24 art. 48] |
| LPG | 8% | Yes | [C · S24 annex A] |
| Charging at a public charge point in Luxembourg | 8% | Yes | [C · S86; S24 annex A] |
| Repairs, servicing, tyres, car wash | 17% | Yes | Rate [I]; deduction [C · S24 art. 48] |
| Car park, rented parking space | Taxed | Yes | [C · S24 art. 44(1)(g); I for the rate] |
| On-street parking paid to the commune, road tax | No VAT | – | [I] |
| Insurance | No VAT (exempt) | – | [C · S24 art. 44(1)(i)] |

- **Invoice:** up to €100 including VAT a simplified invoice (a fuel or parking ticket) is enough; above, a full invoice in the business's name (normal-regime.md). [C · S24 art. 63(10)]
- **Costs paid abroad** (fuel, a repair, a rental car, tolls) carry foreign VAT, which is not deductible on the Luxembourg return. A normal-regime business asks for it back through the EU refund procedure: electronic claim in the AED's VAT Refund portal by 30 September of the following year, above the minimum amounts given in eu-vat reverse-charge-and-reporting.md. The other country decides under its own rules. [C · S82] Belgium caps the refund on cars, fuel and repairs at 50%; Germany lists no car exclusion. [C · S87, S88 – Commission guides of 2016: check before relying on them] A franchise user deducts nothing and gets no refund (eu-vat reverse-charge-and-reporting.md). [C · S24 art. 57bis(5)]
- **Electric car, charging at home:** the household's electricity bill carries 8% VAT; the share that went into the car for business km is deductible. Evidence: the kWh charged (wallbox or separate meter, or the car's charging log) × the business-km share, and the electricity invoices. [I – medium; no official text on the proof · VAT-O-24]
- **Charging in Luxembourg billed by a provider not established here** (a foreign charge card): by the text of the law the VAT-identified customer owes the Luxembourg VAT himself (reverse charge). Check such invoices. [I – medium · S24 arts 14(1)(f), 61(4) · VAT-O-24]
- **Wallbox:** 17%; fixed to the private home, deduct only the business share from the start. [I]

## 6. The yearly VAT on private use (route a)

Declared once a year in appendix B of the annual return and carried to box 010 (boxes: forms/2025/vat-returns.md §7). [C · S3]

- **Base:** the car's costs on which VAT was deductible × the private share of the km. The form lists fuel, LPG, repairs, tyres, leasing and depreciation; insurance, road tax and garage rent are not lines of appendix B. Depreciation counts « only if » the VAT on the purchase was recoverable. [C · S24 art. 28(1)(c); S3 comments 10–11; S69; S46 for cars given to employees]
- **The purchase price** enters year by year as the depreciation booked for the year (the form takes it from the car's book values). No text fixes a number of years; the AED uses five years for cars rented to employees, and five years is also the adjustment period. A book period much longer than five years lowers the yearly VAT and may be questioned. [C · S3, S76; S36 for the five years; I – medium · VAT-O-21]
- **Rate:** 17%. [C · S24 arts 39–40; I]
- **Home-to-office km:** no Luxembourg text says whether the person's own trips between home and a fixed office are private or business km for VAT. The Court of Justice ruled only for employees: home to a fixed workplace normally serves private needs. Count them as private km until the AED answers – it cannot understate the VAT, and income tax also keeps these km out of the business share. Home office as the only place of business: trips to clients are business km. [O · VAT-O-20; C · S65 for employees; I]
- A leased car: the rents are the cost line; there is no depreciation. [C · S3]
- This VAT is not a deductible cost for income tax (art. 12 n° 3 LIR; lu-income-tax-business vat-in-income-tax.md). [C · lu-income-tax-business S3]

## 7. Expensive cars

No price ceiling exists. The limit is art. 54(1): no deduction for expenses « qui n'ont pas un caractère strictement professionnel, telles que les dépenses somptuaires ». [C · S24 art. 54] The courts apply it to luxury cars: the Court of Appeal refused the deduction on two prestige cars of a one-person consulting company – the taxable person must prove the strictly business character – and the Court of Cassation left that assessment to the trial judges. [C · S73, S74; S75] The decisions concern companies; the article applies to every taxable person. [I] A car out of proportion to the business can lose the whole deduction, on the purchase or lease and on its costs. If the deduction was refused under art. 54, the later sale is exempt. [C · S24 art. 44(1)(x)] The regulation the article announces was not found. [I – Legilux titles searched 19 November 2026]

## 8. Selling the car, taking it private, stopping

| Situation | VAT | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Car wholly in the business (route a) is sold | 17% on the whole price – annual return box 005 | [C · S66; S24 arts 9, 28(1)(a); I for the box] |
| Only the business share was in the business (route b) | VAT on that share of the price | [C · S66] |
| Car kept private (route c) | Outside VAT | [C · S66] |
| Business-asset car that was bought **without** deductible VAT (private seller, margin scheme) | Still 17% on the whole price: that nothing was deducted « est sans pertinence » | [C · S66; I – no Luxembourg exemption covers it] |
| The same car first taken out of the business | The withdrawal is not taxed (nothing was deducted); the later sale is private | [C · S24 art. 13(a); S66] |
| Car on which VAT was deducted is taken into private property, or kept when the business stops | Treated as a sale: 17% on the price of a similar car on that day (its residual value), box 008 | [C · S24 arts 13(a), 13(c), 28(1)(b); S67; I for the box] |
| Seller under the franchise | No VAT; the sale does not count towards the franchise threshold | [C · S24 arts 57, 57bis] |

The charge on stopping the business applies even after the five years of section 9 have run out. [C · S70] Example (invented): car bought for €40,000 plus €6,800 VAT, all deducted; taken private when a similar car costs €18,000 without VAT → VAT due €3,060. [I]

## 9. The five-year adjustment

For a depreciable capital good on which the VAT exceeded €125, the deduction is corrected when the right to deduct changes within five years, counted from 1 January of the year of purchase; each year concerns one fifth. [C · S49; S50; S24 art. 53(2)]

- It matters when the right to deduct itself changes: a business with exempt activity (pro-rata), or a move to the franchise – the remaining fifths are repaid in the last normal-regime return (box 098, minus sign). Example (invented): €6,800 deducted in 2025, franchise from 1 January 2028 → two fifths, €2,720, to repay. [C · S24 art. 57bis(7); S49; I for the example]
- It does **not** follow the private km: under route (a) the yearly private-use VAT does that. [I – high]
- A sale or withdrawal that is taxed needs no adjustment when the VAT was deducted in full. [I – high]

## 10. Under the franchise

- No deduction on the car or its costs; the VAT is part of the cost. [C · S24 art. 57bis(5)]
- No VAT on private use and none on a withdrawal: both need a deduction first. [C · S24 arts 13(a), 16(a); S46]
- A sale under the franchise carries no VAT. [C · S24 art. 57bis(1)]
- A **new** car from another EU country is always taxed in Luxembourg (section 3); a lease from a foreign lessor is reverse-charged (section 4). Both make the simplified return compulsory. [C · S24 arts 2(2)(b), 57bis(4)]
- A car bought under the franchise when the business later moves to the normal regime: whether part of the VAT can be recovered is open (VAT-O-01). Once in the normal regime the car is a business asset whose sale is taxed on the whole price, unless it is taken out of the business first. [O · VAT-O-01; I · S66]
