Never use this file for a 2025 or 2026 answer. Each line says whether it is **adopted law** [C] or a **bill** [O]. Status of each bill: lu-tax-core watch list.

## 2028 and later

| Change | From | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Bill 8815** (watch list W-02), B2B e-invoicing between businesses established in Luxembourg, for supplies taxed in Luxembourg: every business must be able to **receive** and process e-invoices | 1 Jan 2028 | [O · S28, S29] |
| – Receiving: until 30 Jun 2028 (recipients above 2 of 3 criteria at the 2026 balance-sheet date) or 31 Dec 2028 (others), transitional alternative solutions may replace the common delivery network | 1 Jan 2028 | [O · S28 art. 5 new] |
| – Mandatory **issuing** for issuers above 2 of 3 criteria at the 2026 balance-sheet date (balance sheet €7.5m, net turnover €15m, 50 full-time staff) | 1 Jul 2028 | [O · S28] |
| – Mandatory issuing for all other issuers | 1 Jan 2029 | [O · S28] |
| – An e-invoice compliant with the 2019 law would not need the customer's acceptance (art. 63 changes) | 1 Jan 2028 | [O · S28] |
| EU single VAT registration, including a mandatory reverse charge for non-established suppliers | 1 Jul 2028 | [C · eu-vat S14] |
| EU digital reporting for cross-border B2B transactions | 1 Jul 2030 | [C · eu-vat S14] |

The bill covers invoices issued under the VAT law between businesses established in Luxembourg for supplies taxed there that must be invoiced; it excludes only occasional sellers of new means of transport, invoices to people taxable only under the housing-VAT rules, and distance sales to consumers. Franchise users are not excluded and must invoice business customers, so in our reading they would issue e-invoices to Luxembourg businesses (small issuers from 1 January 2029); art. 44 supplies for which no invoice is required stay outside.
