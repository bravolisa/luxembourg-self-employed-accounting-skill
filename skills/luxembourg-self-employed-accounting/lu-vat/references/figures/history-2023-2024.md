# VAT history – 2023 and 2024

**Purpose:** only for old invoices, assets bought in 2023 or 2024 (cost and VAT for depreciation), and corrections of those years. For current questions use figures/2025.md or 2026.md.

## Rates

| Period | Standard | Intermediate | Reduced | Super-reduced | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Jan – 31 Dec 2023 | **16%** | **13%** | **7%** | 3% | [C · S19, S21] |
| From 1 Jan 2024 | 17% | 14% | 8% | 3% | [C · S20] |
| Until 31 Dec 2022 | 17% | 14% | 8% | 3% | Standard rate [C · S19 example]; others [I] |

The 2023 rates come from the law of 26 October 2022 (Mémorial A n° 534), a temporary derogation from art. 39(3). [C · S21]

### Which rate applies to an invoice around a change (1 Jan 2023 and 1 Jan 2024)

- The rate in force when the supply is made (chargeable event) applies. Where VAT becomes due at another moment, the rate in force when it becomes due applies. [C · S19]
- Accrual basis: VAT becomes due when the invoice is issued, at the latest on the 15th of the month after the supply. Example from the circular: works finished in December 2022, invoiced 20 December 2022 → 17%; invoiced 20 January 2023 → 16% (VAT due 15 January 2023). [C · S19]
- Advance payments: VAT is due when the advance is received, on the amount received, at the rate then in force. [C · S19]
- Receipts basis (art. 25): VAT is due on payment, but at the rate in force when the supply was made. [C · S19]
- Continuous services with periodic statements: each period counts as supplied when it ends. [C · S19]
- The same rules apply in reverse to the return to 17/14/8% on 1 January 2024. [C · S20]
- Imports and credit notes are not covered by the circulars. [O]

### For assets bought in 2023 or 2024

- Read the VAT on the invoice at the rate shown; check it against the table above.
- Whether that VAT was deductible depends on the buyer's VAT regime **at the time** (franchise below), not today's regime. Non-deductible VAT is part of the asset's cost and is depreciated with it. [I · see lu-accounting vat-entries.md]

## Franchise before the 2025 reform (old art. 57)

| Point | Rule until 31 Dec 2024 | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold | Turnover excluding VAT of the **previous** calendar year ≤ **€35,000**. Without a previous year: the expected turnover of the current year | [C · S22] |
| Leaving during the year | Franchise ends from the first day of the month after the month in which current-year turnover passes €35,000 | [C · S22] |
| Turnover counted | Excluding VAT, minus most art. 44 exempt supplies and ancillary sales of capital goods; a part year is scaled up to a full year | [C · S22] |
| Not covered | Supplies taxed by option (art. 45), new means of transport, taxable persons not established in Luxembourg | [C · S22] |
| Deduction and invoices | No right to deduct input VAT; no VAT on invoices | [C · S22] |
| Option | The person could opt for normal VAT (rules in a grand-ducal regulation, not opened) | [C · S22; O for the details] |
| Annual notice | Previous year's turnover to the AED in writing before 1 March | [C · S23] |

The €35,000 for 2023 rests on the text in force until 2024 and the EU authorisation of 2019; no 2023-dated page states it. [C · S22, S35]

## Other 2023–2024 points

- Receipts-basis VAT ceiling: €500,000 (2023). [C · S34]
- From 1 January 2024 the domestic reverse charge (art. 61(3)) was rewritten: the customer pays the VAT on emission allowances and gas and electricity certificates, and, above €10,000 excluding VAT, on mobile phones, chips, consoles, tablets, laptops and certain metals. [C · S33]
