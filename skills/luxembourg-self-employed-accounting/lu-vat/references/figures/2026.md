# VAT figures – tax year 2026

No amendment to the VAT law took effect during 2025: the AED's consolidated text at 1 January 2026 still names the law of 20 December 2024 as the last change. The 2026 budget law does not amend the VAT law either. [C · S24; I · S30 for the budget law]

| Item | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Standard / intermediate / reduced / super-reduced rate | 17% / 14% / 8% / 3% | [C · S24] |
| Franchise threshold (Luxembourg turnover) | €50,000 in the current year and in the previous year | [C · S25] |
| Franchise tolerance | Up to 10% above (€55,000): franchise continues for the year. More than 10%: it stops from the day after the threshold is passed | [C · S25] |
| Franchise invoice mention | « TVA non applicable – Article 57bis de la loi modifiée du 12 février 1979 » (the law's wording; circular 814 leaves out « modifiée ») | [C · S24 art. 57bis(5); S25] |
| EU SME scheme (EX), EU-wide turnover ceiling | Set by EU law – eu-vat, sme-cross-border.md | – |
| Intra-EU goods purchases, franchise and art. 44 users (current or previous year) | €10,000 | [C · S15, unchanged per S24] |
| EU B2C distance sales and e-services (OSS) threshold | Set by EU law – eu-vat, oss.md | – |
| Filing frequency (prior-year turnover) | Not above €112,000 annual; above €112,000 up to €620,000 quarterly; above €620,000 monthly | [C · S48 arts 5, 5bis – regulation unchanged since 2013] |
| Receipts-basis VAT ceiling | €500,000 | [C · S24 art. 25(1)] |
| Simplified invoice | ≤ €100 including VAT, or any invoice issued by a franchise user – never for goods sent to another EU country or supplies where the customer abroad owes the VAT | [C · S24, S38 art. 63(10)] |

## Deadlines for 2026 returns

| Return | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Simplified annual return (franchise; art. 44 with self-assessed VAT) | Before 1 March 2027 |
| Franchise turnover notice to the AED | Before 1 March 2027 [C · S4] |
| Annual return, annual-only filers | Before 1 March 2027 |
| Annual return, monthly / quarterly filers | Before 1 May 2027 |
| Monthly / quarterly returns | Before the 15th of the month after the period [C · S14] |
| Recapitulative statements | Before the 25th after the period |
| OSS | End of the month after the quarter |
| EX report | 30 Apr / 31 Jul / 31 Oct / 31 Jan |

Deadline rules [C · S6, S3 comment 2, S14 comment 2]; the 2027 dates are those rules applied to 2026. [I]

## Return forms

The Q1 2026 quarterly return uses exactly the same field codes as the 2025 forms, including box 481 and the purchase boxes (741/742, 751/752, 461…): forms/2025/vat-returns.md applies to 2026 too. [C · S14] The 2026 annual and simplified field lists are identical to 2025 (forms/2025/vat-returns.md). [C · S47]

## Other 2026 points

- Circular 807-1 (21 October 2025): providing a company car to employees is always a supply of services; when the employee pays for it, it is a taxable car rental. Repeals circular 807bis. [C · S36]
- Circular 814 (1 June 2026) covers photovoltaic installations and restates the franchise rules above. [C · S25]
