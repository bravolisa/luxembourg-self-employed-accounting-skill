# Leaving or joining the franchise

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

## When normal VAT starts

| Luxembourg turnover this year | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|
| €50,001 – €55,000 | Franchise continues until 31 December; charge VAT from 1 January | [C · S1] |
| Over €55,000 | Charge VAT from the day after the sale that crosses €55,000 | [C · S1] |
| Either case | Out of the franchise for the following calendar year | [C · S1] |
| Voluntary switch | First day of the month after the AED receives the request (a new business: from the start if filed within one month); at least one calendar year | [C · S1 p. 13; S24 art. 57bis(8)] |

AED example: a sale on 8 September takes turnover to €56,000 → normal regime from 9 September. [C · S1] If the AED's registration letter gives a different date, the letter wins. [I · S6]

## VAT deduction in the first year – not a fraction of the year

- VAT on purchases made **from the switch date** for taxed activity is deductible. [I]
- Purchases made under the franchise are generally not deductible, even if used later. [I · S1]
- Capital goods bought earlier: a partial adjustment of deduction may be possible when the deduction right starts. The law lets the AED take the measures needed so that a business moving between the normal regime and a special regime neither gains nor loses unjustly; the general adjustment for capital goods runs in fifths over five years (tenths over ten years for buildings). The regulation of 3 March 1980 adjusts an initial non-deduction too, year by year for the rest of the period, when the right to deduct changes; there is no adjustment when the VAT on the asset was €125 or less, nor when the change is under 10 points unless the yearly amount exceeds €125. Until 2024 the franchise regulation applied this expressly to moves between the franchise and the normal regime; it was repealed from 2025. [C · S49, S50; I for leaving the franchise – open point VAT-O-01]

So a laptop bought in November after the switch: VAT deductible. Books bought in March under the franchise: not deductible. Explain why "2/12 of the year" is the wrong way to think about it.

## Returns for the switching year

- File the normal annual VAT return for the year. [C · S3]
- Enter the franchise-period turnover in **box 481** ("Supplies carried out within the scope of the domestic SME scheme of article 57bis"), section I.B line 6. Footnote 17: where during one calendar year the taxable person was subject to both the standard scheme and the domestic SME scheme, box 481 shows the turnover generated under the SME scheme. [C · S3]
- During the year, monthly or quarterly returns may also be due, as the AED sets. They also have box 481, with the same footnote. [C · S14]
- On the normal return, input VAT on purchases used for franchise-period supplies is not deductible: enter it in its origin box and exclude it in **box 094** (« relating to supplies exempt pursuant to Art. 44, 56quater, 57bis… »). [C · S3 box wording; I application]

## Joining the franchise

- **A new business** below the threshold asks for the franchise when it registers for VAT (registration form). [C · S1]
- **A business in the normal regime** (or on the cash basis) that qualifies can ask to join, with a written request on the AED's form. The franchise starts on **1 January of the year after** the AED receives the request. [C · S24 art. 57bis(7)]
- On joining, the business is treated as stopping for its VAT returns: it files a final return and **repays part of the VAT it deducted on capital goods** still within their adjustment period (five years, ten for buildings); a cash-basis business also settles the VAT on unpaid invoices. [C · S24 arts 57bis(7), 53(2), 25(4)]

## Check before switching (flag, do not conclude)

- **Is the activity taxable at all?** Some education is exempt (art. 44): private lessons by teachers at school or university level. Private language lessons for adults or companies are often taxable. [I · S6; see exempt-art44.md]
- **Private customers abroad**: since 2025, educational activities streamed or made available online to private individuals are taxed in the customer's country from the first euro, with no EU-wide threshold. They then fall outside the Luxembourg threshold count and need OSS or a foreign registration. Whether live one-to-one lessons are such an activity is open (eu-vat EUVAT-O-03). [C · S24 art. 17(2) 4° b), art. 19quater; I for one-to-one lessons]
