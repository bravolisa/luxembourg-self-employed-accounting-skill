# Open points – eu-vat

Answer an open point by saying it is not settled, giving the working answer with its label, and naming who can settle it.

## EUVAT-O-03 · Live one-to-one online teaching or coaching
- Question: General B2C rule, electronically supplied service, or the 2025 virtual education rule? The same question for one-to-one coaching by video.
- Why it matters: Place of supply of online lessons.
- Working answer: Not an electronically supplied service (too much human input); probably the 2025 rule for educational activities « diffusées ou mises à disposition virtuellement » to private customers, which taxes them where the customer lives from the first euro. The rule itself is law [C · S19 art. 17(2) 4° b)]; whether a live one-to-one lesson is such an "activity" is our reading. [I] The AED reads « activités éducatives » in that rule widely – wider than events and not limited to admission (lu-vat S59, circular 808 §3.2) – which supports this reading for lessons. Coaching is less clearly « éducative », but « ou similaires » may reach it; until settled, treat one-to-one video coaching for EU private clients as taxed in Luxembourg only with this caveat stated. [I]
- Who can settle: AED; EU VAT Committee guidance.
- Status: open

## EUVAT-O-04 · OSS on the Luxembourg annual return
- Question: What exactly goes in box 226 each year?
- Why it matters: Filing for OSS users.
- Working answer: OSS sales in 226, never taxed in Luxembourg. [C · S3; I detail]
- Who can settle: AED; eCDF notice.
- Status: open
