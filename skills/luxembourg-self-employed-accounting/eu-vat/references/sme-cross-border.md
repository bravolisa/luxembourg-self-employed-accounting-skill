# EU cross-border SME scheme (EX number)

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files of the domain skills (lu-vat, lu-income-tax-business, lu-social-security) win if they differ._

Since 2025 a small business can be VAT-exempt in other EU countries that offer a franchise, without registering there. [C · S1]

| Point | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Condition | EU-wide turnover ≤ €100,000 (all member states added up), and the turnover in each other country under that country's own national threshold | [C · S1] |
| How | Register for the cross-border scheme in the home country; an EX-suffix identification is issued | [C · S1 p. 7] |
| EX number | The Luxembourg VAT number with the suffix « -EX » (LU, 8 digits, -EX); the AED sends it with the list of countries that accepted, generally within 35 working days of the prior notification; the exemption applies from the date the number is communicated | [C · S1 p. 18; S19 art. 57quater(2), (4)] |
| Reporting | Quarterly EU-turnover report: 30 April, 31 July, 31 October, 31 January, via the certified professional space on MyGuichet | [C · S6; S1 pp. 17–20] |
| Exceeding €100,000 | The exemption in the other countries ends from the day after the threshold is crossed; tell the AED and file a final declaration within 15 working days; the EX number is deactivated. A year above €100,000 also excludes the next calendar year | [C · S19 art. 57quater(6), (9); S6] |
| Home regime | EX does not require the franchise at home; a normal-regime business under €100,000 EU turnover can hold it | [C · S6] |
| Business customers | EX status covers all supplies in that country, also those to other businesses: the sale is exempt there and the customer does **not** reverse-charge (no « autoliquidation » on the invoice). Without EX the customer reverse-charges as usual, so for B2B services alone the scheme is not needed | [C · S1 p. 25] |
| Operations not covered | For operations the franchise does not cover (e.g. intra-Community acquisitions), the business « doit se conformer aux obligations fiscales de cet État membre » | [C · S1 p. 14] |
