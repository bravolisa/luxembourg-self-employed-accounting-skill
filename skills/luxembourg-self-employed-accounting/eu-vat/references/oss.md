# Selling to private customers in other EU countries – OSS

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files of the domain skills (lu-vat, lu-income-tax-business, lu-social-security) win if they differ._

| Point | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold | €10,000 per year, EU-wide, for distance sales of goods and telecom/broadcasting/electronic services to private persons in other EU countries – only for a business established in one member state; the sales must stay under it in the current year **and** have stayed under it the previous year; once crossed, destination VAT applies from that moment; the business may opt for destination VAT earlier | [C · S6; S4 for services; lu-vat S24 art. 19quater] |
| Above the threshold | VAT of the customer's country applies | [C · S6] |
| How to pay it | One-Stop-Shop (OSS) registration in the home country; one quarterly OSS return for all EU countries, due by the end of the month after the quarter | [C · S6] |
| On the Luxembourg return | OSS sales are reported in box 226 of the annual return (annual-return section I.B line 8) and never taxed in Luxembourg | [C · S3; I for the reporting detail] |
| Franchise users | The Luxembourg franchise covers only Luxembourg supplies. Above the threshold (row above), destination VAT is due anyway – via OSS – unless the EX scheme exempts the sale in that country | [C · S6] |
| Online events and education (from 2025) | Taxed in the customer's country from the first euro – the €10,000 threshold does not apply (place-of-supply.md) | [C · S19 art. 17(2) 4° b), art. 19quater] |
