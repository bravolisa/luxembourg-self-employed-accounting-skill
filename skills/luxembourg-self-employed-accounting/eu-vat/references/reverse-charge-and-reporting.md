# Reverse charge, VIES and recapitulative statements

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files of the domain skills (lu-vat, lu-income-tax-business, lu-social-security) win if they differ._

## Reverse charge

- For a service to a business in another EU country, the **customer** accounts for the VAT in its own country. The supplier charges no VAT and mentions the reverse charge on the invoice. [C · S2: "for which the customer is liable for VAT"]
- Mirror image: a Luxembourg business that **buys** services from a foreign business self-assesses Luxembourg VAT, whether the supplier is in the EU or outside it (US, UK, UAE, India…); the supplier's region only changes the box. Normal-regime users deduct it in the same return; franchise and art. 44 users cannot deduct it, so it is a cost. [C · S2 section D; S9 art. 17(1)(b); details in lu-vat purchases.md]
- Exceptions where the customer's country does **not** decide: property (incl. hotels), passenger transport, admission to events, restaurants and catering, short-term vehicle hire. A foreign hotel bill carries that country's VAT; it is not reverse-charged in Luxembourg. [C · S10; hotel I]
- VAT wrongly charged by a supplier where the reverse charge applied: the customer cannot deduct it and should ask the supplier to correct the invoice and repay; the tax authority must refund directly only if the supplier cannot (e.g. insolvency). [I · S13]
- Invoice wording: "Reverse charge – VAT due by the customer" (autoliquidation), with both VAT numbers. [I]

## Checking VAT numbers (VIES)

- Check the customer's VAT number in VIES before invoicing without VAT, and keep a dated record. [I · S8]
- VIES shows whether a number is valid now; it does not show registration dates. [I · S8]
- No valid VAT number → treat the customer as a private person until proven otherwise. [I] For **goods**, the exemption needs the customer to be VAT-identified in another member state and to have given its number to the supplier, and it is lost if the recapitulative statement is late or wrong, unless the supplier justifies it. [C · lu-vat S24 art. 43(1)(d)] A VIES failure noticed only when filing means re-checking how the invoice was treated, not dropping the line from the statement. [I]

| Exception | Supplies covered by the supplier's EX exemption in the customer's country (no reverse charge there) are not listed | [C · S6, S1 pp. 24–25] |
| Frequency | Services: monthly or quarterly at the filer's choice. Goods: monthly, quarterly allowed if intra-EU goods supplies ≤ €50,000 in the quarter and each of the previous four | [C · S6] |
| Deadline | Before the 25th of the month after the period, via eCDF | [C · S6] |

| No supplies | A period without qualifying supplies needs no statement | [I · lu-vat S24 art. 64bis(1)] |
| Data rules | Country = the VAT prefix (EL for Greece), the number without prefix; one line per customer with the amounts grouped; for call-off stock a replacement customer must differ from the original | [C · lu-vat S47; I for EL from BravoLisa uploads] |
| Own goods moved abroad | Moving the business's own goods to another EU country is listed too, like a sale (art. 64bis(1) with art. 43(1)(f)); call-off stock: the intended customer's VAT number and any change (art. 64bis(1bis)) | [C · lu-vat S24] |
| Consistency | In Luxembourg, box 423 of the VAT return must equal the statements' total | [C · S2] |

## Foreign VAT paid abroad – refunds

- VAT charged by a supplier in another EU country (hotel, fuel, conference) is reclaimed from that country through the EU refund procedure (Directive 2008/9/EC), filed via the home country's portal – for a business with no taxable supplies there. A Luxembourg business files through the AED's VAT Refund portal (eTVA access with a professional LuxTrust certificate) « au plus tard le 30 septembre de l'année civile qui suit la période du remboursement » (VAT law art. 55bis). A claim covers 3 months to a calendar year; minimum €400 for a period shorter than a year, €50 for a year or its remainder. The AED does not forward claims of franchise users or of businesses with only art. 44 exempt supplies. [C · S19 art. 55bis; S12 arts 15, 17; S24] A business VAT-registered in that country deducts it on that country's return instead. It is never Luxembourg input tax. [C · S12 art. 3; I for the registered case]
- The refund is limited to VAT the business could deduct at home. Businesses under a small-business exemption are excluded: SME-exempt persons have no right to deduct or to a refund under arts 167–171 of the VAT Directive. [C · S11]
- So for a franchise user, foreign VAT is simply part of the cost. [C · S11]

## Moving your own goods to another EU country

- Sending your own goods to your stock or a marketplace warehouse in another member state is a deemed supply, exempt like an intra-Community supply (art. 12 g), art. 43(1)(f)); call-off stock (art. 12bis) is the exception. [C · lu-vat S24]
- Report it in box 013 (annual) or 457 (periodic) – both name art. 43(1)(f) – and on the recapitulative statement under **your own VAT number in the arrival country**: the regulation asks for « le numéro par lequel l'assujetti est identifié … dans l'État membre d'arrivée ». So a VAT number there is needed; the arrival is an intra-Community acquisition in that country. Franchise users cannot use this exemption. [C · S3; S22 art. 6; S17]
- Sales from that warehouse follow the rules of that country.
