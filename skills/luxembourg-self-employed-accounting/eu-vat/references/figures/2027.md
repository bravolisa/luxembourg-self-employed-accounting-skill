## Luxembourg transposition for 2027 – bill 8812 (watch list W-01)

The bill amends the VAT law to transpose the 2027 part of the directive; as filed it states « La présente loi entre en vigueur le 1er janvier 2027 ». [O · S15] Cross-border points, **as proposed**: [O · S15]

- The franchise and the IOSS scheme would be mutually exclusive: a franchise user must give up the franchise to use IOSS.
- EU cross-border SME scheme (EX): authorities would act within up to 35 working days after notification and must promptly tell the business about changes to its franchise information.
- The non-EU OSS would cover services to all non-taxable customers in the EU.
- Distance-selling €10,000 threshold: only sales from stock in the seller's own member state would count.
