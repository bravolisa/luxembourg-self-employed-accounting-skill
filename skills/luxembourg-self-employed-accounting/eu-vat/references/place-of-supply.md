# Place of supply

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files of the domain skills (lu-vat, lu-income-tax-business, lu-social-security) win if they differ._

The place of supply decides which country's VAT applies. Get it right before thinking about rates, boxes or invoices.

## Services

| Customer | Type of service | Taxed in | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business (VAT-identified) | General rule (consulting, editing, design, IT, marketing…) | Country where the customer is established; customer pays under reverse charge | [C · S9 art. 17(1)(b), S10; S2, S3 for the boxes] |
| Business outside the EU | General rule | Outside EU VAT; local tax in the customer's country may apply | [I] |
| Private person | General rule | Country where the supplier is established – also for private customers outside the EU | [C · S19 art. 17(1)(c)] |
| Private person outside the EU | Rights and licences, advertising, consultants, engineers, lawyers, accountants and similar, data processing and information, banking and insurance, staff hire, rental of movable goods other than vehicles | Where the customer lives (outside the EU) | [C · S19 art. 17(2) 8°] |
| Private person in another EU country | Telecom, broadcasting, electronically supplied services (automated, little human input) | Customer's country, unless the supplier's EU-wide sales of these services stay under €10,000 | [C · S4; S19 art. 17(2) 7bis, art. 19quater] |
| Private person outside the EU | Telecom, broadcasting, electronically supplied services | Where the customer lives (no threshold) | [C · S19 art. 17(2) 7bis] |
| Private person | Online events and online education streamed or made virtually available (from 1 January 2025) | Country where the customer is established or lives, from the first euro: the €10,000 threshold does not apply to these | [C · S19 art. 17(2) 4° b), art. 19quater] |
| Private person | Cultural, artistic, sporting, scientific, educational, entertainment or similar activities attended in person | Where the activities actually take place – a workshop held abroad is taxed abroad | [C · S19 art. 17(2) 4° b)] |
| Any | Services linked to a building or land | Where the property is | [C · S10, S19 art. 17(2) 2°] |
| Any | Hotel accommodation | Where the hotel is (property-related) | [C · S19 art. 17(2) 2°: « la fourniture de logements dans le cadre du secteur hôtelier »] |
| Business | Passenger transport | Each country crossed, by distance | [C · S10, S19 art. 17(2) 3°] |
| Business | Admission to cultural, sporting, educational or entertainment events | Where the event takes place; not when attendance is virtual | [C · S10, S19 art. 17(2) 4° a)] |
| Any | Restaurant and catering (not on board trains, ships or planes) | Where physically performed | [C · S10, S19 art. 17(2) 6°] |
| Any | Short-term hire of a means of transport (under 30 days; 90 for boats) | Where it is made available | [C · S10, S19 art. 17(2) 7°] |

Notes:
- Teaching given live by a teacher over the internet is **not** an electronically supplied service: Regulation 282/2011, art. 7(3)(j), excludes « les services d'enseignement, lorsque le contenu des cours est fourni par un enseignant sur l'internet ». [C · S20] Since 2025, educational activities « diffusées ou mises à disposition virtuellement » to private customers are taxed where the customer lives, and the EU VAT Committee reads streamed or virtually available activities into that rule. [C · S26; S21] Whether a one-to-one lesson counts as such an "activity" is open (EUVAT-O-03). [I]
- **Hybrid sessions**: an activity organised for people attending in person stays taxed where it takes place even if it is sometimes also streamed; when the customer can choose in-person or online and the supplier does not know the choice when invoicing, the in-person rule applies unless the facts show the customer took part online – then the invoice is corrected. [C · S21 – EU VAT Committee guideline, not binding]
- **« Activities » is wider than « events »**: the AED reads the private-customer rule (art. 17(2) 4° b)) as covering « divers types d'« activités éducatives » », not only events and not only admission – unlike the business-customer rule for access to events (4° a)). [C · lu-vat S59, circular n° 808 §3.2] Whether a one-to-one lesson or coaching session is such an activity: EUVAT-O-03.
- For a Luxembourg franchise user, services taxed outside Luxembourg under these rules do not count towards the franchise threshold (lu-vat profiles-and-thresholds.md). [C · S19 art. 17; lu-vat S24 art. 57]
- Keep proof of the customer's status and location: a valid VAT number (VIES check, dated), address, contract. [I · S8]

## Goods

| Situation | Treatment | Luxembourg box (2025 annual return) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sold to a VAT-identified business in another EU country, goods leave Luxembourg | Exempt intra-Community supply; buyer self-assesses (not for a seller under the Luxembourg franchise: excluded from the exemption, a Luxembourg supply under the franchise – lu-vat franchise.md) | 013 | [C · S3] |
| Sold to private persons in other EU countries (distance sales), EU-wide total above €10,000 | Taxable in the customer's country | 203 (or via OSS, 226) | [C · S3, S6] |
| Exported outside the EU | Exempt export | 014 | [C · S3] |
| Bought from a business in another EU country | Intra-Community acquisition taxed in Luxembourg (franchise and art. 44 users: only once EU goods purchases exceed the Luxembourg threshold in the current or previous year, or with an option – lu-vat, franchise.md) | see lu-vat | [C · S9 art. 2(2)(b)] |
| Bought from a seller outside the EU | An import only if the goods enter the EU and the buyer is the importer on the customs papers; a non-EU seller shipping from EU stock is not an import | see lu-vat purchases.md | [I] |

## EU basis of these rules

The B2B general rule and its exceptions come from the VAT Directive as replaced by Directive 2008/8 (arts 44, 45, 47, 53, 55; reverse charge art. 196) [C · S25]; virtual events and education from Directive 2022/542 (arts 53, 54(1), from 2025) [C · S26]; B2C telecom, broadcasting and electronic services from art. 58 and the €10,000 threshold of art. 59c (Directive 2017/2455) [C · S27]; the SME scheme from Directive 2020/285 (art. 284) [C · S28]. The Luxembourg law carries the same rules (S19).
