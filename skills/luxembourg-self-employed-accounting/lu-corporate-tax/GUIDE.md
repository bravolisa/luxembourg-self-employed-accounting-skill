## Rules people get wrong most often

- The 2025–2026 rates are **14% / 16%**, plus 7% of the tax as solidarity surcharge – not the 15% / 17% still shown on Guichet's page. [C · S8, S5]
- A SARL always pays municipal business tax, even for a liberal-profession activity that would be exempt for a sole trader. [C · S12]
- There is no minimum income tax, but there is a **minimum net wealth tax**: a dormant SARL or SARL-S still pays at least €535 a year. [C · S1, S10; I · S2]
- Company taxes (IRC, ICC, NWT) are not deductible from the company's profit. [C · S8]
- Directors' fees are not deductible for the company and bear 20% withholding; a manager's salary for day-to-day management is a normal deductible wage. [C · S24, S8]
- Without a tax card the salary is taxed at class 1, at least 33% of the semi-net pay; a manager insured as self-employed may not get a card automatically – request it with form 164 R. [C · S45, S44; I for the manager]
- Dividend withholding is **15%** (not 20%), paid within 8 days; the resident shareholder gets a 50% exemption plus €1,500. [C · S8]
- Corporate returns must be filed **electronically** for a SARL and SARL-S, signed with LuxTrust or a Luxembourg eID, by 31 December of the following year. [C · S29, S30, S20]
- An exempt ASBL is still taxed on a commercial activity such as a bar. [C · S8]
