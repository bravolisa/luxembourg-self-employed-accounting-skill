# Company tax – known changes from 2027

Nothing below is law yet. Bill 8800 (2027 budget) was deposited on 7 October 2026; status on the watch list W-03. Never use this file for a 2025 or 2026 answer.

| Proposal | Now (2025–2026) | Bill 8800, from tax year 2027 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| IRC lower rate | 14% up to €175,000 | **13%** up to **€200,000** | [O · S31] |
| Sliding band | €24,500 + 30% between €175,000 and €200,001 | **€26,000 + 23%** of the part above €200,000, between €200,000 and €250,001 | [O · S31] |
| IRC standard rate | 16% above €200,000 | **15%** above **€250,000** | [O · S31] |
| Loss carry-forward (IRC, via art. 114 LIR) | 17 years | No time limit, also for losses since 2017 | [O · S31] |
| Loss carry-forward (ICC) | 17 years | No time limit | [O · S31] |

The art. 114 change also covers the business losses of sole traders (lu-income-tax-business figures/2027.md). [O · S31]
