# Company tax figures – tax year 2026

The 2026 budget law (law of 19 December 2025) changed only revaluation coefficients and the CO2 tax credit in direct tax: no change to the IRC, the ICC allowance or the net wealth tax. The LIR in force on 1 January 2026 still has 14% / 16%. [I · S11; C · S8]

| Item | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| IRC | Same as 2025: 14% up to €175,000; sliding band; 16% above €200,000 | [C · S8] |
| Solidarity surcharge | 7% of the IRC | [C · S5] |
| ICC allowance (companies) | €17,500 | [C · S4] |
| Communal multipliers 2026 | Luxembourg City 225% (6.75%); range 225%–350% | [C · S7; I range] |
| Minimum net wealth tax | €535 / €1,605 / €4,815, unchanged | [C · S10] |

## Deadlines for tax year 2026

| What | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Form 500 (IRC, ICC 2026; NWT at 1 January 2027) | 31 December 2027 | [C · S20 rule] |
| Advances | As in 2025 | [C · S23] |
