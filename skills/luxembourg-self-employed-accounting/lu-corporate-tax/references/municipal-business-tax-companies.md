# Municipal business tax (ICC) – companies

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

Sole traders: lu-income-tax-business, municipal-business-tax.md. This file covers SARLs and SARL-Ss.

- A capital company is **always** a commercial enterprise for all its activities, so it pays municipal business tax (*impôt commercial communal*, ICC; *Gewerbesteuer*) **even when its activity would be a liberal profession** for a sole trader – an architect or consultant who moves into a SARL starts paying ICC. [C · S12]
- **Allowance**: €17,500 on the adjusted operating profit for companies subject to IRC (sole traders and partnerships have a higher allowance – lu-income-tax-business). [C · S4]
- **Rate**: 3% of the adjusted profit after the allowance, multiplied by the municipality's multiplier (*taux communal*). [C · S4]
- **Luxembourg City 2026**: 225%, so 3% × 225% = 6.75%. [C · S7] The 2026 multipliers range from 225% (e.g. Luxembourg, Hesperange, Contern, Leudelange) to 350% (e.g. Bettembourg, Differdange, Dudelange) – 6.75% to 10.5%: the company's municipality matters. [I · S7]
- For a company, ICC is **not deductible** from its profit; for sole traders and partnerships it is. [C · S4]
- **Advances**: 10 February, 10 May, 10 August, 10 November. [C · S23]
- Declared on Form 500 together with the IRC (form-500-and-deadlines.md). [C · S29]

Not covered yet: rounding of the base, a short first financial year, splitting the base between municipalities when a company has premises in several.
