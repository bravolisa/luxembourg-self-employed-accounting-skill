# ASBL taxation

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

The ASBL as a legal form (founding, members, accounts): lu-entity-profiles, asbl.md. VAT of an ASBL: lu-vat (exempt activities, art. 44).

## Income tax (IRC)

- ASBLs (*association sans but lucratif*; *Vereinigung ohne Gewinnzweck*) are among the bodies subject to IRC (art. 159(1) A n° 6 LIR). [C · S8]
- An ASBL is **exempt** if its statutes and its actual activity pursue directly and exclusively religious, charitable or public-interest purposes (art. 161(1) n° 1). [C · S8]
- Even an exempt ASBL is **taxable on its industrial or commercial activity** – for example a bar at events, a shop or paid services sold like a business. [C · S8]
- ASBLs may file on MyGuichet or with the PDF Form 500. [C · S6] Whether a fully exempt ASBL must file every year: open point CT-O-04.

## Municipal business tax and net wealth tax

- Net wealth tax: a body with cultural, charitable or public-interest purposes (§ 3(1) n° 6 VStG) is exempt « seulement … dans la mesure où ils n'exercent pas une activité économique »; if it runs one, the assets used in it are taxed. [C · S32 point 5]
- Municipal business tax: the same kind of exemption (GewStG § 3 n° 6), except for an economic business beyond managing their own assets – original 1936 text, no consolidated text published. [I · S14]
- A fully exempt ASBL (no economic activity) pays no net wealth tax and so no minimum: resident private-law bodies owe the minimum « pour autant qu'ils ne bénéficient pas d'une exemption personnelle » (point 8). [C · S32; I that an ASBL is among the « autres personnes morales de droit privé »] Whether the minimum applies to an exempt ASBL with an economic activity: open point CT-O-03.

## Donations to an ASBL (the donor's side)

- Only gifts to bodies **recognised as being of public utility** (by special law or under the ASBL and foundations law), approved development NGOs, listed public institutions, approved social-impact companies and similar EU/EFTA bodies are deductible for the donor (art. 112(1) n° 1). An ordinary ASBL that is not recognised does not qualify. [C · S8; S28]
- Donors deduct their gifts within yearly limits – a minimum total, a maximum share of income and an absolute cap – and carry the excess over two years; amounts: lu-income-tax-personal, special-expenses.md. [C · S28] The ACD publishes the lists of approved bodies. [C · S28]

## Worked example (invented)

A sports club ASBL runs a bar at its events: the club's sports activity is exempt; the bar's profit is taxable as commercial activity; sponsors can deduct gifts only if the club is recognised of public utility. [I · S8]
