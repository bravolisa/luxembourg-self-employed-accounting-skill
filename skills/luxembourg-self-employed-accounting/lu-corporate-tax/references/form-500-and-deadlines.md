# Form 500, deadlines and payments

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

- **One return for three taxes**: Form 500 covers the IRC and ICC of tax year N and the net wealth tax at 1 January N+1 (the 2025 form covers NWT at 1 January 2026). [C · S29]
- **Electronic only for companies**: an SA, SAS, SCA, SARL, SARL-S or SE cannot use the PDF form; it files on MyGuichet.lu. [C · S29] Signing needs a LuxTrust product or a Luxembourg eID card; a foreign eIDAS means cannot sign the corporate return. [C · S30]
- ASBLs may use either MyGuichet or the PDF form (asbl-taxation.md). [C · S6]
- **Deadline**: 31 December of the year after the tax year (from tax year 2022): the 2025 return by 31 December 2026, the 2026 return by 31 December 2027. [C · S20]
- **Contents of the tax package** (*liasse fiscale*): at least the balance sheet, the profit and loss account, the table of fixed assets and depreciation, and a statement of certain overheads. [C · S6] Preparing the accounts: lu-accounting, companies-and-asbl.md.

## Payments

| What | When | Status |
|---|---|---|
| IRC advances | 10 March, 10 June, 10 September, 10 December | [C · S23] |
| ICC and NWT advances | 10 February, 10 May, 10 August, 10 November | [C · S23] |
| Balance of IRC, ICC and NWT | At the latest one month after receiving the assessment notice (bulletin) | [C · S22] |
| Late filing | The tax office may add up to 10% of the tax | [C · S21] |
| Late payment | Interest of 0.6% a month, also on unpaid advances | [C · S21] |

A new company receives its first advance notices from the ACD; until then nothing is due in advance. [I]

## Withholding returns the company files

- Dividends: the 15% withheld must be declared and paid within 8 days of the dividend being made available (dividends-and-withholding.md). [C · S8 art. 149]
- Directors' fees: the withholding return (form 510bis) is filed electronically on MyGuichet with LuxTrust since 1 January 2025 (owner-manager-pay.md). [C · S24]
- Salaries paid to the manager or staff: wage withholding through the payroll – outside this skill.
