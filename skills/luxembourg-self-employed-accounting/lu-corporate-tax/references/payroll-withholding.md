# Paying a salary: wage withholding tax (RTS)

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

A company that pays a salary – also to its own manager – withholds income tax from it (« retenue d'impôt sur les traitements et salaires », RTS; *Lohnsteuer*) and passes it on to the ACD.

## Does the manager's salary bear RTS?

- Yes. Pay to a SARL manager for day-to-day management is employment income (art. 95(6) LIR), and all employment income bears the withholding (art. 136(1)). [C · S8]
- This holds also when the manager is insured as self-employed at the CCSS (lu-social-security, company-managers.md): the tax law sets no social-security condition for the withholding. [I · S8] Whether the employee tax credits apply to such a manager is a separate question (lu-income-tax-personal, tax-credits.md).

## Before the first payslip

- Register as an employer with the CCSS: the « déclaration d'exploitation » is due within **8 days** of the first employee starting. [C · S43]
- The ACD puts each employee's tax card (« fiche de retenue d'impôt ») on MyGuichet; the employer must look at the cards at least once a month (« au moins une fois par mois de calendrier », art. 143(4) LIR) and withhold according to them. [C · S8; S42]
- Keep a salary account for each employee (art. 2 RGD of 27 December 1974). [C · S38]

## The tax card – and a manager insured as self-employed

- The ACD issues a resident's tax card by itself « suite à toute affiliation d'un salarié par un employeur auprès du CCSS » and sends it by post, on average within 30 working days; the employer downloads it on MyGuichet (PDF or XML). [C · S44; S42]
- **No card yet**: the employer withholds at the dearest rate – the class 1 table, « sans que cette retenue d'impôt puisse être inférieure à 33% de la rémunération semi-nette ». Once the card is there, the employer corrects the withholding from the card's validity date; otherwise the ACD corrects it in the annual adjustment (modèle 163) or the tax return. [C · S45]
- The automatic card does not free the employee from getting one when none comes (« ne dégage pas ce dernier de l'obligation de se procurer, le cas échéant, une fiche de retenue d'impôt »): request it with form **164 R**, handled by the RTS office of the place of residence. [C · S45; S44]
- **A manager insured as self-employed** may get no card automatically, because the trigger is the affiliation of an employee: check soon after the first payslip, and request the card with form 164 R if none comes. [I · S44]
- **The manager's own contributions**: for an employee, the employer deducts the social contributions before working out the tax. A manager who pays CCSS contributions personally as self-employed can ask for them to be written on the tax card – form 164 R, box 540 « Prélèvements et cotisations à payer par le contribuable en raison de l'affiliation obligatoire des non-salariés » – which lowers the monthly withholding. Without it, the withholding runs on the full salary and the excess comes back only after the year (annual adjustment or tax return). [C · S44 for the box; I that it fits a manager]
- **Two offices**: the employee's card is handled by the RTS office of the commune of residence; the employer's withholding, salary-account extracts and card consultations are checked by the RTS office of the commune where the employer is established. [C · S46]

## Withholding, return and payment

- The tax is **withheld** each time pay is attributed (« lors de chaque attribution de rémunérations », art. 136(3) LIR), and the return covers « l'impôt retenu durant la période de déclaration » (RGD art. 14(2)). [C · S8; S38] So pay for September that is only paid in October belongs to the period that contains October. [I · S8; S38]
- It is **declared and paid** for a period – the month, the quarter or the year – **within 10 days after the period ends**, not on payday: « Dans les dix jours qui suivent la fin de chaque période de versement, l'employeur … est tenu de verser au bureau de recette l'impôt retenu » (RGD art. 15(1); return: art. 14(1)). A return is due for every period, also when nothing was withheld – it then says so (art. 14(3)). [C · S38; S40]
- The period depends on the **month's withholding after the tax credits paid out** to employees (art. 13(2)):

| Month's withholding, net of tax credits | Return and payment | Due |
|---|---|---|
| €750 or more | Monthly | 10th of the next month |
| €75 to under €750 | Quarterly | 10 April, 10 July, 10 October, 10 January |
| Under €75 | Yearly | 10 January |

[C · S38 art. 13(2); S40]

- The company may always choose a shorter period. When a month reaches a higher band, the shorter period takes over for the tax still owed. [C · S38 art. 13(2)]
- **The return**: « déclaration de la retenue d'impôt sur rémunérations et des crédits d'impôt bonifiés » (modèle 950), **electronic only on MyGuichet since 1 January 2025** (LuxTrust); an agent such as a fiduciary may file it. It is filled in online in the MyGuichet assistant, which can be pre-filled from an XML file exported by payroll software (« peut être pré-remplie à l'aide d'un fichier XML »). Payment can be made separately from the return, with the tax number, the period and the return's reference. [C · S39; S41; S40]
- **Late return**: a surcharge of up to 10% of the tax (§ 168(2) AO) and penalty payments (§ 202 AO). [C · S38 art. 14(5)]
- **Late payment**: interest – « Le défaut de payement de l'impôt à son échéance rend exigible un intérêt de retard » (art. 155(1) LIR: 1% a month in the law; what the ACD charges in practice: lu-income-tax-personal, filing-and-assessment.md). The instructions of the RTS return name this article for late payment. [C · S8 art. 155(1); I that the practice rate applies to RTS]
- **Missing return or payment**: the ACD may fix the tax itself; the employer is personally liable for tax withheld or that should have been withheld (art. 136(4), (7) LIR), and withheld tax not paid over is never remitted (RGD art. 15(3)). [C · S8; S38]

## After the year

- Send the electronic salary-account extract (ECSP) for each employee to the ACD via MyGuichet **before 1 March** of the next year, and give each employee an annual salary certificate on paper. [C · S42]
- The employee asks the ACD for the annual adjustment or files a return; the employer does not do it. [I · S42]

## Example (invented figures)

A one-person SARL withholds €147 a month from its manager's salary, after tax credits: that is between €75 and €750, so it files and pays **quarterly** – €441 by 10 April, 10 July, 10 October and 10 January. If the €147 is before the tax credits, work out the net amount first: a net below €75 a month means one yearly return by 10 January. If the manager has the own CCSS contributions written on the tax card (box 540), the withholding can drop sharply – possibly below €75 a month. [I · arithmetic on S38; S44]
