# Special sectors

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

| Sector | Extra requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Bars, restaurants, accommodation, discotheques (HORECA) | An accelerated training on food hygiene and safety, human rights and the protection of minors – at least **8 hours**, by the Chambre de Commerce (House of Training) | [C · S1; S17] |
| Bars and restaurants – alternative | One year of professional experience (CCSS certificate or EC certificate) can replace the training | [C · S9] |
| HORECA – also | Registration as a food business with the Food Safety Division | [C · S9] |
| **Short-term rentals** (Airbnb-type) | Renting furnished units to passing guests for **90 nights or more** a year (all units added) makes you an accommodation operator needing a permit; pass the accelerated training within **6 months** of reaching the threshold | [C · S1] |
| Real estate agents, business introducers, property managers, syndics, developers | Accelerated real-estate training of at least **50 hours** (Chambre de Commerce) – business introducers too since 2023 (art. 10(1)); professional liability insurance at all times for agents, property managers, syndics and developers – **not** required of business introducers (art. 10(2)) | [C · S1 art. 10; S17] |
| National road haulage under 3.5 tonnes | General commercial activity: no qualification, no financial guarantee | [C · S10] |
| International haulage over 3.5 tonnes | Professional-competence course and exam (House of Training); bank guarantee of €9,000 for the first vehicle and €5,000 per extra vehicle | [C · S28] |
| Taxi | Permit (list C craft, no qualification) **plus** a taxi operating licence from the Service des taxis (Ministry of Mobility); licences are limited in number | [C · S3; S27] |
| Private security and guarding | Commercial permit plus a special approval from the Ministry of Justice | [C · S26] |
| Package travel | The insolvency guarantee of the Consumer Code | [C · S1] |
| Architects | Specific professional liability insurance and registration with the OAI after the permit | [C · S33] |
| **Tattoo** (also permanent make-up), **piercing**, branding, cutting | Everyone who does it – employee or self-employed – needs a hygiene training of at least **21 hours** from a training body authorised in Luxembourg (or recognised in the EU country where it was taken), on a set programme: rules, skin and healing, hygiene, infection risks, sterilisation, worker protection, waste, and a practical module on sterile technique. The business declares the activity to the Ministry of Health (Direction de la santé, on MyGuichet, free) **one month before** starting, naming the employer and each practitioner with their training certificates; changes and the end of the activity within one month. Working without the declaration or the training is a criminal offence (8 days to 3 months in prison and/or €251–5,000). | [C · S48 arts 3, 8; S49 arts 1–2, annex A; S50] |
| – who is exempt | Health professionals performing healthcare acts; a jeweller-goldsmith (*bijoutier-orfèvre* permit) piercing ear lobes with a piercing gun. No diploma is listed as equivalent to the training: health, nursing or hygiene studies do not replace it by the text – only the Ministry of Health can accept them. | [C · S48 arts 1, 3; S49 art. 2; I · S49] |
| – customers | Not on minors, except ear-lobe piercing; tattoo and piercing from 16 with a parent's prior informed consent, in writing, kept 5 years; for under-18s the parent attends the prior consultation | [C · S48 arts 6, 7] |
| – which permit | Tattooist: list C craft, no qualification (tattoos and permanent make-up). Piercing alone is not named in any craft list – BP-O-09 | [C · S1, S3; O] |
| Public UV tanning | Whoever supervises the sunbeds: at least **8 hours** of training; declaration to the Ministry of Health one month before | [C · S48 art. 11; S50] |

Workshops and other classified establishments may also need an operating licence (commodo), which can take months – lu-business-start. [C · lu-business-start S9]
