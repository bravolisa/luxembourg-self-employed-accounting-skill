# Professions under their own laws

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

- **Lawyers, doctors, dentists, vets and statutory auditors (réviseurs d'entreprises)** are governed by their own laws, not by the business-permit law. [C · S2]
- **Lawyers** need an authorisation linked to the Ministry of Justice instead of a business permit, also when practising through a company. [C · S34]
- **Medical professions** (doctor, dentist, vet, pharmacist) need an authorisation from the Ministry of Health. [C · S34]
- An **expert-comptable** needs **both** the business permit and registration on the roll of the Ordre des Experts-Comptables. [C · S15]
- Because these professions are outside the 2011 law, they cannot use a SARL-S, whose object must fall under that law (lu-entity-profiles, sarl-s.md). [I · lu-entity-profiles S6]
- Notaries, bailiffs, insurance intermediaries and other professions with their own authorisation routes are not covered yet: refer to the professional body.
