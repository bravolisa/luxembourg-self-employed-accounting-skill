# The conditions: fixed place, integrity, qualification, dirigeant

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

The Minister issues the permit when the conditions of establishment (a fixed place), professional integrity and qualification (arts 4 to 27) are met. [C · S1] Qualifications: qualifications.md.

## The dirigeant

- The **dirigeant** must actually and permanently run the day-to-day business **through physical presence** at the establishment (wording added in 2023). [C · S1]
- He or she must have a **real link** with the business: the owner for a sole trader, or a mandataire registered in the RCS for a company. [C · S1]
- He or she must not have evaded social and tax charges, withholding taxes included, personally or through a company he or she runs or ran. [C · S1]
- One person may be dirigeant of at most **two craft businesses** (lists A and B) outside a group, unless holding at least 25% of the shares in each. [C · S1]

## A non-EU national as partner or dirigeant

- **Owning shares** is not restricted. The immigration law regulates activities: a self-employed activity is any paid economic activity not carried out for and under the direction of another person. [C · S51 art. 3] Holding shares is not an activity, and the SARL rules set no nationality or residence condition for partners. [I · S51; lu-entity-profiles S6]
- **Being the manager on whom the permit rests** is a self-employed activity for the immigration law: a corporate officer who is registered as a beneficial owner and holds the business permit for the company is treated as a self-employed worker. [C · S51 art. 51(2)]
- A non-EU national who lives in Luxembourg for that needs the residence permit « travailleur indépendant »; one condition is presence in the country for effective day-to-day management. [C · S51 arts 51(1), 52] Procedure: procedure-and-fees.md.
- **EU Blue Card holder:** the law authorises a **subsidiary** self-employed activity alongside the main highly qualified job – no second permit is asked for. [C · S51 art. 45-2(3)] The law does not define « subsidiary », and Guichet's Blue Card page does not mention the side activity. [C · S51, S52; O · BP-O-10]
- Our reading for a Blue Card holder: being a partner is fine. Being the dirigeant on whom the permit rests is doubtful – the permit law wants the dirigeant to run the day-to-day business actually and permanently through physical presence (above), which sits badly with « subsidiary » beside a full-time job. Safer: another founder holds the permit and manages; confirm with the Direction générale de l'immigration before the deed is signed. [I · BP-O-10]
- Other residence permits (salaried worker, family member, long-term resident) are not covered here – send the user to the Direction générale de l'immigration.
- Social security follows its own test: a partner with more than 25% of the shares on whom the permit rests is self-employed for the CCSS (lu-social-security company-managers.md).

## Fixed place of business (lieu d'exploitation fixe)

- The business must have a fixed place of operation in Luxembourg, defined by five elements: a **material installation** suited to the nature and size of the activity; administrative and technical **equipment**; **effective and permanent management**; **regular presence** of the dirigeant; **documents** kept accessible there. [C · S1]
- A **domiciliation address** (law of 31 May 1999) is **not** an establishment. [C · S1]
- The form asks for the operating address; a lease or other proof may be supplied in a second step, after the file is examined. [C · S4]
- Whether a **private home** qualifies is existing open point IT-O-05 (lu-income-tax-business): answer with its working answer and say it is not settled.
- **Coworking:** read the contract first. A flexible-desk ("nomad") contract seen in October 2026 expressly excluded any use of the address as registered office, establishment or administrative address – such a desk cannot be named as the fixed place. [P · S47] Whether a coworking contract that allows it meets the five elements is our reading case by case. [I · S1]

## Professional integrity (honorabilité)

- Required of the dirigeant, the majority shareholder and anyone able to influence the management significantly. [C · S1]
- Judged on past conduct and the administrative enquiry, counting only the **last ten years**. [C · S1]
- Not filing direct-tax (including withholding) or indirect-tax returns for **two consecutive years within three** is a breach of integrity. [C · S1]
- Proof: residents of more than 10 years – a recent Luxembourg criminal-record extract (bulletin n° 3); the application form asks for one less than 6 months old. [C · S5, S4] Non-residents and residents of less than 10 years add the extracts of every country of residence in the last 10 years and a sworn non-bankruptcy declaration before a notary. [C · S13]
