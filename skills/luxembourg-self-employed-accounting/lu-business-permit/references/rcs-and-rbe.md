# RCS and RBE for sole traders

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

Companies and ASBLs: lu-entity-profiles, rcs-rbe-and-publication.md.

## Trade and companies register (RCS)

- Every individual trading as a **merchant** (*commerçant personne physique*) registers with the RCS. [C · S18] The law names only merchants: a craft sole trader registers when the business counts as commercial – most do, because they also sell goods; for a pure-service craft, check with the LBR. [I · S18]
- Only the persons listed in art. 1 of the RCS law are registered; **liberal professionals** working in their own name are not on that list, so they do not register. [I · S18]
- The permit must be in hand first; the RCS entry records the permit number. [C · S18]
- A **trade name** (*enseigne commerciale*) must include the trader's own surname and first names, and nothing that suggests a company; the LBR checks that it is not already registered (arts 16–17). Checking a name beforehand: lu-entity-profiles rcs-rbe-and-publication.md. [C · S18 arts 16, 17]
- Entries and changes within **one month** of the event; the LBR registers an accepted filing within three working days. [C · S18] For the first registration the event is the start of trading: the duty falls on « tout particulier faisant le commerce » (art. 3) and the entry gives « la date de création du commerce » – a permit held without trading does not start the month. [I · S18 arts 3, 15]
- A natural person registered in the RCS gives their national identification number; a sole trader's own registration shows it (art. 3, 8°, since 1 February 2025). Someone without one is allocated a number at registration. [C · S18 arts 3, 12bis]
- Late filings of entries cost a surcharge (figures). If a file is still not updated 30 days after the LBR's registered request (art. 19-6, in force since 1 February 2025): a public "not up to date" notice from the 2nd month, the failures shown on certificates from the 3rd, a daily penalty of **€40** in months 7 to 9 – at most 90 days, so at most €3,600 – and strike-off from the 12th month, without dissolving the entity. The LBR applies these sanctions progressively from 21 September 2026; associations and foundations are not concerned for now. [C · S18 art. 19-6; S36; S19] (The "€3,500 plus €500 a day" of some secondary sources is not in the law: €3,600 is the cap of the daily penalty, €500 the highest one-off late-filing surcharge.)
- The €12 registration duty is charged on each private deed filed with the RCS; a sole trader's registration request is not a deed, so normally no €12 is due – not confirmed (open point BP-O-03). [I · S20]
- Whether the RCS number must appear on a sole trader's invoices: open point BP-O-06 (the permit number must – procedure-and-fees.md).

## Annual accounts

Commercial sole traders whose previous year's turnover exceeded the bookkeeping threshold (lu-income-tax-business, figures) file annual accounts with the RCS at the latest **seven months** after the calendar year end (not published); at or below it they may keep simplified books and skip the standard chart of accounts – lu-accounting, bookkeeping-duties.md. [I · S18; C · lu-accounting S12]

## Register of beneficial owners (RBE)

Sole traders are **outside the RBE**: the RBE law covers RCS-registered entities from point 2° onwards, and sole traders are point 1°. [C · S21]
