# Applying, fees, changes and sanctions

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

## Applying

- Online on **MyGuichet** (professional space) – which needs a LuxTrust certificate and a MyGuichet account – or by post to the Ministry of the Economy (DG Classes moyennes, Service des autorisations d'établissement). [C · S2]
- The permit is needed **before** starting the activity. [C · S1]
- Third-country nationals who want to be self-employed file one combined file with the residence-permit application to the Minister of Immigration. [C · S2] Guichet (October 2026) describes two steps: a long-stay visa D applied for before entering Luxembourg (valid 6 months), then the first residence permit after arrival (€80 fee). [C · S37, S38] If no decision comes within 3 months, the applicant may treat the request as refused and appeal – it is not an automatic refusal. [C · S39 art. 4]
- **Fee**: a chancery duty (*droit de chancellerie*) of **€50** per permit, paid online in MyGuichet, or by tax stamp or transfer for postal applications; the official form states the same. [C · S2, S4] The law itself only sets a range of €24 to €2,500 for a grand-ducal regulation, which was not found – open point BP-O-02. [O · S1]
- A **duplicate** of a valid permit is free, electronically through MyGuichet. [C · S14]

## The form, step by step

- **Declaration of honour:** the application form contains the applicant's own declaration on the **three years** before the application – paid management positions (formal or de facto), majority holdings (alone or with a spouse, partner or third party) or significant influence in companies in Luxembourg or abroad, listed with name, registered office and dates – or a declaration that there were none. [C · S4] The separate « déclaration sur l'honneur supplémentaire » is **only** for beneficial owners and/or managers who are not the manager applying; they also provide a criminal-record extract (bulletin n° 3) and an ID. [C · S45] A founder who is sole partner and the gérant applying does not need it.
- **Activity:** for "commercial activities and services" the form asks for a detailed description of the activity. [C · S4]
- **« Start Your Business » (ADEM)?** – a compulsory yes/no question. Answer yes only for a project inside ADEM's StartYourBusiness programme, which is open only to jobseekers registered with ADEM and admitted to it (lu-subsidies start-up-aid.md). [C · S4, S46]
- **A company being created:** draft articles are enough for an agreement in principle (« accord de principe »); the articles registered with the RCS are asked for before the permit is issued. Order of steps: lu-entity-profiles sarl-s.md. [C · S4]
- **Attachments named in the form:** the criminal-record extract less than 6 months old (with a Luxembourg identification number), a copy of the ID card or residence permit (both sides), the €50 duty for a paper application, and qualifications where required. [C · S4]

## Timing

- Acknowledgement within **15 days**, listing missing documents (the clock restarts when they arrive); decision at the latest **3 months** after the complete file (one more month for recognition of foreign qualifications); **no decision in time counts as a tacit permit**. [C · S1]
- In practice: an average of **6.62 days** in 2025 (6.18 in 2024). [C · S31]

## After the permit

- The **permit number** (or its 2D barcode) must appear on letters, e-mails, websites, quotes, invoices, shop fronts and construction-site panels. [C · S1]
- **The permit is public from the day it is issued.** The Ministry keeps a register of permits, and its data – the permit number, the business name, the address of the establishment, the authorised activities and the name of the dirigeant – « peuvent être librement consultées en ligne » (art. 32(1)). The application form says the same, and Guichet offers a search (« Rechercher une autorisation d'établissement »). So anyone can find a permit by name before the business has started and before any RCS entry; waiting with the RCS does not keep it private. [C · S1 art. 32(1); S4; S2]
- A change or extension of the business purpose, or a change of dirigeant, needs a **new permit**. [C · S1]
- Within **one month**, notify a new point of sale, a change of the dirigeant's usual residence or a change of the fixed place (a branch: within a month of its creation). [C · S1]
- If the dirigeant leaves, tell the Minister within one month; a provisional permit of up to six months can bridge until a new dirigeant is found. [C · S1]
- The permit **lapses** after voluntary cessation for more than two years, judicial liquidation, bankruptcy, or failure to report a change of the dirigeant's residence within one month. [C · S1] Guichet still lists « inutilisation pendant plus de 2 ans » after issue; that ground was in the 2011 text and was dropped by the law of 26 July 2023. [C · S42] Guichet also lists non-use for more than two years from issue, which the law text does not say. [I · S2]
- On cessation, tell the Ministry of the Economy of the dirigeant's departure within one month (cancellation via MyGuichet or by post); the other steps (CCSS, AED, RCS) are in lu-business-start. [C · lu-business-start S4]

## Working without a permit

A criminal offence: 8 days to 3 years in prison and/or a fine of €251 to €125,000 for individuals (€500 to €250,000 for companies). [C · S1]

## Reform under way (bill, not law)

Bill 8759 (deposited 3 June 2026) would replace the prior permit by an online **notification** for activities without qualification requirements (general commerce, list C crafts…): the dirigeant would get a notification number at once and could start. It would also state that unregulated liberal professions need no qualification – but still a permit. Watch list W-12. [O · S32]
