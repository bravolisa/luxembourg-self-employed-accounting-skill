# Official documents of the tax office (ACD), tax year 2025

Unchanged copies, so that the tools work without downloading anything. They belong to the Administration des contributions directes (ACD); the licence of this repository does not cover them.

| File | What it is | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| `100f-2025.pdf` | Form 100 F – income tax return for tax year 2025, the ACD's blank fillable PDF (20 pages) | https://impotsdirects.public.lu/dam-assets/fr/formulaires/pers_physiques/2025/100f-2025.pdf |
| `F100-XSD-2025.txt` | The schema MyGuichet uses to import a pre-filled Form 100 for tax year 2025 (XSD 0.18.0), as plain text. Its first lines give where and when it was fetched | https://impotsdirects.public.lu/dam-assets/fr/echanges-electroniques/decl_pph/acd-pp-2025-modele-100-xsd-import.zip |

Before filing, check on https://impotsdirects.public.lu that the ACD has not published a newer version. For tax year 2026 neither the form nor the schema is out yet.
