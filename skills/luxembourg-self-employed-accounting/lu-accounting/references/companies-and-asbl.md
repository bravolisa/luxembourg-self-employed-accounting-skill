# Accounts of companies and ASBLs

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files of the domain skills win if they differ._

Sole traders: bookkeeping-duties.md. Legal forms themselves: lu-entity-profiles.

## Companies (SARL, SARL-S, SA)

- **Double-entry books** (a system of books and accounts, partie double). [C · S16]
- Annual accounts and the **PCN balance of accounts** are filed with the RCS within one month of approval and at the latest **seven months after year end**; they are validated on **eCDF** first (unless IFRS is allowed). [C · S12 art. 75; S15]
- SARL and SARL-S accounts are approved by the partners **within six months** of year end – managers who have not put them to the partners by then commit an offence (company law art. 1500-2, 2°) – then filed within one month of approval. [C · lu-entity-profiles S6; S15]
- Filed accounts of SA, SARL and cooperatives are **public**. Small companies need not publish their P&L (nor a management report or auditor's report). [C · S12]

### Capital paid with equipment (contribution in kind) – the company's first entries

No BravoLisa decision exists for company capital; the entries below follow the official chart and are proposals. [I · S21 for the accounts]

| Event | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| The deed: equipment contributed for shares | The asset account – 2222 Machinery, 2233 Tools, 2234 Furniture, 2235 Computer equipment, 2232 Motor vehicles – at the contribution value | 101 Subscribed capital (nominal value of the shares); 111 Share premium (any value above it) |
| Her invoice for the VAT on the contribution (lu-vat contribution-to-a-company.md) | 421611 VAT paid and recoverable | 4712 Amounts payable to partners and shareholders, until the company pays her |
| Equipment **bought** from her instead | The asset account at the price without VAT; 421611 for the VAT | 4712 (or the bank) |
| Formation costs (notary, registration duty, RCS fee) | 201 Set-up and start-up costs, or an expense of the year | Bank |

- A contribution in kind is paid in full at formation, so accounts 102 (not called) and 103 (called but unpaid) are not used for it; they serve cash capital paid within the 12 months allowed since 2 June 2026. [C · lu-entity-profiles S6; S21]
- Depreciation then runs on the contribution value over the remaining useful life (lu-corporate-tax assets-from-the-founder.md).
- Invented example: machines contributed at €6,000 – debit 2222 €6,000, credit 101 €6,000; her invoice shows €1,020 VAT – debit 421611 €1,020, credit 4712 €1,020.

### Size classes (from financial years starting 2023)

| Criterion (small company, art. 35 RCS law) | Threshold | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Balance-sheet total | **€7,500,000** (was €4,400,000) | [C · S13] |
| Net turnover | **€15,000,000** (was €8,800,000) | [C · S13] |
| Average full-time staff | 50 | [C · S12] |

- Small = not exceeding two of the three; a company changes class only after crossing (or falling back under) two criteria in **two consecutive years**. [C · S12] The new thresholds apply to financial years from 1 January 2023, so existing companies are re-classified from **2025** at the earliest. [C · S13; S14]
- Guichet's SARL page (2019) still shows the old €4.4 million / €8.8 million – outdated. [C · S20 outdated]
- **Small companies**: abridged balance sheet and notes; no management report if the notes give the own-shares information; **no statutory audit**. [C · S12]
- Above that, the accounts are audited by an approved statutory auditor (*réviseur d'entreprises agréé*), who replaces the *commissaire* of an SA or of a SARL with more than 60 partners. [C · S12]

### Rules for every set of annual accounts (also a commercial sole trader filing with the RCS)

- Balance sheet, P&L and notes « forment un tout ». [C · S12 art. 26(1)]
- Every line shows the previous year's figure. [C · S12 art. 29(2)]
- No offsetting between assets and liabilities, or charges and income. [C · S12 art. 33]
- An abridged P&L is allowed when two of three limits are not exceeded: balance sheet €25,000,000, net turnover €50,000,000, 250 staff. [C · S12 art. 47]

## ASBLs (law of 7 August 2023)

| Class | Criteria – not exceeding two of three, two years in a row | Accounts | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small | Fewer than 3 full-time staff, €50,000 total income, €100,000 total assets | Simplified (cash) records of receipts and expenses; a statement of receipts and expenses with an annex (cash and bank balances, members by bracket, share of funds sent abroad) | [C · S17] |
| Medium | Not small, and not above 15 FTE, €1,000,000 income, €3,000,000 assets | Full annual accounts | [C · S17] |
| Large | Above two medium criteria two years in a row, or any ASBL recognised of public utility | Full annual accounts **audited** by a réviseur d'entreprises agréé | [C · S17] |

- The board submits the accounts and next year's budget to the general meeting **within six months** of year end; they are filed and published **within one month of approval** (law). The Ministry of Justice states seven months after year end as the outer limit. [C · S17; S18]
- Transition: an ASBL that adapted its statutes in 2024 applies the new accounting rules to its **2025** accounts (filed in 2026); one that adapted in 2025, or not in time, from its **2026** accounts (filed in 2027). [C · S19]
- The small-ASBL annex is a fixed form (RGD of 17 February 2025, annex A): total cash and bank balances at year end; members and adherent members in bands (up to 10, 11–50, 51–100, 101–500, over 500); the share of funds sent to other EU/EEA countries and outside, in bands (0–10%, over 10–50%, over 50%). It is filed with the statement of receipts and expenses at the RCS within one month of approval. [C · S25] Entities not bound to the PCN file directly on lbr.lu, not through eCDF. [C · S26]
