# Records and good habits

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files of the domain skills (lu-vat, lu-income-tax-business, lu-social-security) win if they differ._

## Retention and documents

- Keep accounting documents and invoices for **10 years**, including e-invoice XML with integrity guarantees. [C · S3] For VAT: invoices 10 years from their date, books 10 years from their closing; origin, integrity and legibility guaranteed all along; electronic storage allowed if the integrity data is kept too; storage abroad only where the AED has access (never in a country without a mutual-assistance agreement), and paper invoices stay in Luxembourg. [C · lu-vat S24 art. 65(4)–(6)] Books kept outside Luxembourg are declared on the annual VAT return (box 238). [C · lu-vat S3]
- A receipt is a valid simplified invoice only up to a small amount set by the VAT law; above it a full invoice is needed – the limit and the mentions: lu-vat, invoicing.md. [C · S3]
- Documents must be available on request; no special form is required for expenses to be accepted, but they must be correctly recorded. [C · S4]

## Separate business bank account

- Not legally required for a sole trader – no rule found. Say "no rule requires it", not "the law says you don't need one". [I · S5]
- Strongly recommended: business money is visible, bookkeeping is faster, checks by the ACD or AED are simpler. [I]
- Private spending from the business account: record it as a private withdrawal. [I · S4]

## Matching payments to invoices

- Asking clients to quote the invoice number is a good habit, but not enough on its own. [I · S5]
- Keep an invoice list and match each payment to an invoice monthly (or quarterly with little activity). If a client forgets the reference, match by hand and add a note. [I]
- Why it matters: on the simplified method income counts when received, so matching shows what was received in which year; under normal VAT, declared sales must match invoices. [I]

## Organising records

- Store documents digitally, one folder per year, and review monthly or quarterly depending on activity. [I]
- Plan ahead for VAT and CCSS thresholds (the VAT franchise threshold – lu-vat; the CCSS provisional base – lu-social-security). [I]
