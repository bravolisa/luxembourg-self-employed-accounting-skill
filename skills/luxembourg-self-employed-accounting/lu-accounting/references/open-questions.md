## ACC-O-04 · Accounts for foreign VAT and for cash-accounting suppliers
- Question: Which account holds foreign VAT to be reclaimed abroad, and which suspense account holds VAT on invoices from cash-accounting suppliers?
- Why it matters: Bookings for these cases.
- Working answer: Foreign VAT: the official chart has 421811 « Foreign VAT » (receivable) and 46151 (payable) [C · S21]; 421618 belongs to the Luxembourg AED accounts. Cash-accounting suppliers: book as normal invoices. [I · lu-vat S37]
- Who can settle: BravoLisa decision log.
- Status: open

## ACC-O-06 · One PDF or several for MyGuichet
- Question: Should several reports be combined into one PDF?
- Why it matters: Uploading the return.
- Working answer: MyGuichet lists the attachments it needs from your answers; PDF only. [C · S24] Every attached file takes an attachment type from a list, and « Compte de profits et pertes » is a type of its own – so the profit and loss account goes in as its own PDF. [C · lu-income-tax-personal S196 for the 2024 procedure; I for 2025] Whether the list also has a type « Bilan » is not visible in the published screenshots: one clearly named file per report remains the safe choice. [I] The founders can read the type list off any 2025 procedure in their own MyGuichet space.
- Who can settle: ACD / MyGuichet help.
- Status: open

## ACC-O-08 · Car: the yearly private share and the other entries without a decision
- Question: How does BravoLisa book the yearly private share of a business car (costs, depreciation, VAT on private use), a private car used for business, leasing beyond the instalment, and the sale or withdrawal of the car?
- Why it matters: Without a decision the private share can be taken out twice – once in the books and once on the tax form.
- Working answer: Until decided, no private-share entry is posted: the current design computes the business share on the tax form, outside the ledger. [B · S27] The entries proposed in car-entries.md §2 use accounts the official chart names (106166, 10629, 106172, 64421 / 64422, 74421 / 74422). [I – proposal · S6]
- Who can settle: BravoLisa decision.
- Status: open
