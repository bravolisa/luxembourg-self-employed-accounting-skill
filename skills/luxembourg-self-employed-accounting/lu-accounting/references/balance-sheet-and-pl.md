# Balance sheet and P&L for the tax return

| Point | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Who attaches them | Taxpayers determining profit by comparing net invested assets: Form 110 route A (commercial with regular accounts), Form 100 case 601 (liberal profession on the balance-sheet basis) | [C · S1] |
| Official form? | None. The ACD answered in writing (July 2026): « You are welcome to attach your own presentation. » Should the tax office need more, it will ask | [C · S2] |
| Weight of that answer | An emailed contact-centre position, not a ruling (« un courriel n'engage pas son expéditeur ») – good enough for format; escalate in writing if a point becomes load-bearing | [C · S2] |
| PCN for a liberal profession? | Not compulsory: the Code de commerce duty binds traders and commercial companies, and « ni la L.I.R. … ne comprennent aucune disposition impérative ni au sujet du système comptable à appliquer » (circular L.G.–A n° 63). Use PCN captions anyway for legibility | [C · S11; I for the Code's scope] |
| Abridged or full | Full. Whoever keeps accounts attaches « une copie non abrégée du bilan de clôture »; with double-entry books also a full P&L and a trial balance (« tableau comptable ») showing, for each main account, the opening and closing positions, the movements and the balance (RGD of 13 March 1970, art. 8(1)) | [C · S22] |
| Abridged eCDF formats | They do not meet the « non abrégée » rule, and they hide the turnover and the owner's account | [I · S22] |
| Tax adjustments | Mark the items that differ from tax law and state the adjustments made outside the accounts (art. 8(2)) – on MyGuichet, the « Redressement hors bilan » field | [C · S22; lu-income-tax-business S62] |
| Who must keep double entry | Every trader, farmer and liberal professional above the § 161 AO turnover threshold (amount: lu-income-tax-business, figures); below it the receipts-and-expenses method is open | [C · S11] |
| Upload | MyGuichet asks for each required attachment in its own place, chosen from your answers; PDF only, at most 7 MB per file (stated for companies; observed for individuals) | [C · S24] |

Whether the balance sheet and the P&L have separate upload places in MyGuichet: open point ACC-O-06.

## The owner's account (sole traders)

Money and goods moving between the owner and the business are neither income nor expenses: they go through the owner's account 106. [C · S21]

| Movement | Account (PCN annex) | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Drawings** – the owner takes something out | 1061 | Cash for daily life (10611); goods or services taken (10612); private insurance (10614x); social security contributions (106151); private share of running costs (10616x); private purchases paid by the business (10617x); income tax (106181) |
| **Contributions** – the owner puts something in | 1062 | Private money or loans paid in; proceeds of private assets sold (10624x); rent, wages or child benefit received on the business account (10625–10627); tax refunds (10628x); the business share of costs paid privately (10629) |

[C · S21]

- **Why profit does not change**: the tax profit is closing net invested assets − opening net invested assets + withdrawals (cash and in kind) − additional contributions (art. 18(1) LIR; Form 110 section A, lines 12–18). [C · lu-income-tax-business S20]
- **Value**: a withdrawal or contribution in kind counts at its operating value (« valeur d'exploitation ») on the day; a contributed asset's operating value becomes its cost for depreciation (art. 43(1) LIR). [C · lu-income-tax-personal S2]
- **Example (invented figures)**: the owner moves €3,000 from the business bank account to a private account – Dr 10611 3,000 / Cr 5131 3,000. No expense, no effect on profit. [I · S21]
