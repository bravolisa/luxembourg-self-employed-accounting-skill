# Who must keep which books

_Figures in this file: none – the bookkeeping turnover threshold is stated once, in lu-income-tax-business, figures/<year>.md; the other amounts are in the year files of lu-vat and lu-social-security._

| Who | Duty | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial traders (commerçants, with a commercial or craft permit) | Accounting under the Code de commerce (arts 8–11), which tax law adopts (§160 AO): double-entry books | [C · S11 §2] |
| Small commercial traders | Previous business year's turnover excl. VAT not above the bookkeeping threshold: may keep simplified books instead of double entry (arts 11, 13 C.com.). Above it, double entry from the next year – no tax notification is needed | [C · S11 §2.1.2] |
| All commercial traders | A complete inventory of everything the business owns and owes, once a year, whatever the bookkeeping system (art. 15 C.com.) | [C · S11 §2.1.2] |
| Commercial sole traders above the art. 13 threshold | Also file annual accounts with the RCS (eCDF, PCN format) at the latest 7 months after the end of the calendar year; traders under art. 13 C.com. are outside this duty | [C · S12 arts 25, 75] |
| Commercial sole traders filing with the RCS | Natural persons' deposits are not published or consultable by third parties | [C · S3] |
| Liberal professions | Not traders, so outside the Code de commerce duties | [C · S11 §2.1] |
| Liberal professions (and any business not bound by the Code de commerce), turnover above the threshold | Tax bookkeeping duty (§161 AO): accrual double-entry books, annual inventory, balance sheet – from the first business year after the tax bulletin for a year above it is notified; a single exceptional overrun does not trigger it; falling back below does not end it without the tax office's consent | [C · S11 §§3.2–3.4] |
| Notaries | Double-entry books at any turnover | [C · S11 §2] |
| Small liberal professions below the threshold | No double-entry duty; may use the simplified receipts-and-expenses method, but must still record income and expenses properly and keep documents | [C · S11 §3, S4] |

The threshold and the test in detail (what counts, unbilled work, advances): lu-income-tax-business, profit-methods.md.

A taxpayer on the simplified method without a detailed record of turnover has turnover determined on cash receipts. [C · S11 §3.3]
