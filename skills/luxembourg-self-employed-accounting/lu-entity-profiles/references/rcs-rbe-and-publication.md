# RCS, RBE and publication

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

Sole traders' RCS registration: lu-business-permit. This file covers companies and ASBLs.

## Company name – before forming

- Every new business must have a name clearly different from every other. When it registers a company or a change of name, the LBR only checks that the name **is not already registered**; trademarks and other name rights are not checked (« sans préjudice des dispositions légales assurant la protection du nom commercial »). [C · S36 art. 16]
- What the LBR counts as the same name: names that differ by one letter or number are different, and so are two words swapped; the legal form (« SARL », « SARL-S », « SA »…), accents, capital letters, spaces and punctuation do **not** make a name different; symbols with a meaning (« & », « @ », « € ») do. Only the name is checked, not an abbreviation or a trade sign. A struck-off company's name is free again. [C · S35]
- **Quick check:** the public search on the LBR website (lbr.lu) finds registered persons and entities by name or RCS number (RGD of 23 January 2003, art. 22). [C · S12] The fee list (annex J) has no fee for this search, so in our reading it is free. [I · S12]
- **Name certificate:** requested online from the LBR, after creating an LBR account (username and password, or LuxTrust). The LBR answers with a « certificat de dénomination libre » or « non libre », to download within 20 days. It **does not reserve the name**: whoever registers it first gets it. [C · S34] Guichet only refers to the RCS fee list; that this certificate is billed as an RCS certificate (figures/2025.md) is our reading. [I · S34, S12]
- A name that is free at the RCS can still infringe a trademark: also search the trademark registers (Benelux and EU) before printing anything. [I · S36 art. 16]
- In a SARL or SARL-S, the name is already in the draft articles sent with the permit application: check it just before applying and register soon after (sarl-s.md "Order of steps"). [I · S34, S18]
- Sole traders: a trade name must include the trader's surname and first names, and nothing suggesting a company – lu-business-permit rcs-and-rbe.md. [C · S36 art. 17]

## Trade and companies register (RCS)

- Every commercial company with legal personality (SARL, SARL-S, SA…) registers with the RCS (art. 6 RCS law); an ASBL gets its legal personality on registration (asbl.md). [C · S7; S5]
- Registrations and changes (new manager, address, partners…) are requested **within one month** of the event. [C · S7]
- Publication in the **RESA** (Recueil électronique des sociétés et associations) follows within 15 days of filing; acts can be relied on against third parties only from publication, unless the third party already knew. [C · S7]
- **Penalties** (law of 23 January 2025): if a filing is missing or late and not regularised within 30 days of a registered reminder, the LBR can impose **€40 a day** from the 7th month after the reminder (until the end of the 9th). [C · S7] This applies to every registered person or entity, ASBLs included: the law exempts ASBLs and foundations only from the fee surcharge (art. 19-6(3)(c), (5)(b)). [C · S7]
- Strike-off by the LBR does not dissolve the company; if not regularised, the LBR reports it to the public prosecutor. [C · S7]
- The late-filing **fee surcharge** for accounts does not apply to ASBLs and foundations. [C · S7]
- Fees: figures/2025.md.

## Register of beneficial owners (RBE)

- Entities registered with the RCS – companies, ASBLs, foundations – declare their **beneficial owners** in the RBE. [C · S4]
- **Commercial sole traders** are not registered entities for the RBE and file nothing. [C · S4]
- Registration and every change within **one month** of when the entity knew or should have known (art. 4 RBE law). [C · S13]
- Fines of **€1,250 to €1,250,000** for not filing or filing inaccurate data. [C · S4]
- Since the law of 23 January 2025, RBE data are no longer open to the general public: access is for authorities, anti-money-laundering professionals and persons showing a legitimate interest in fighting money laundering. [C · S13]
