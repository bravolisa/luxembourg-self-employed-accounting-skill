# ASBL (association sans but lucratif)

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

## The law

- The current law is the **law of 7 August 2023** on non-profit associations and foundations (Mémorial A n° 592), which repealed the law of 21 April 1928. [C · S5]
- It entered into force on **23 September 2023**; the 24-month transition for existing ASBLs ended on **23 September 2025** – it now applies to all. [C · S10]
- An existing ASBL that did not adapt its statutes: clauses contrary to the 2023 law are deemed unwritten and the law's mandatory rules apply (art. 77(4)). [C · S5]
- Guichet's page on creating an ASBL (2021) still describes the 1928 law (legal personality on publication, three members) – outdated. [C · S11 outdated; S5]

## What an ASBL is – and may do

- Legal definition: an association that **does not carry on industrial or commercial operations, or** does not seek to procure a material gain for its members (art. 1). [C · S5]
- Because the two conditions are joined by "or", an ASBL may run some commercial activity (e.g. a café at its events) if it seeks no material gain for its members; whether a commercial activity may be its main activity is not stated. [I · S5] Open point ENT-O-02. Tax on such activity: lu-corporate-tax, asbl-taxation.md.

## Founding and running it

| Rule | Status |
|---|---|
| At least **two founding members** | [C · S5] |
| Legal personality on the day of **registration with the RCS** (registration does not make it a trader) | [C · S5] |
| Board of at least **three directors**; mandates are **unpaid** | [C · S5] |
| Directors and daily managers take on no personal obligation for the ASBL's debts; liability only for carrying out their mandate and management faults | [C · S5] |
| Only a delegation of daily management **to a director** needs general-meeting approval (law of 4 December 2024) | [C · S10] |
| Gifts and legacies above **€30,000** need the Minister of Justice's authorisation – not gifts between living persons made by bank transfer from an EU/EEA bank | [C · S5] |
| On dissolution, net assets go to another association or public-utility foundation (EU/EFTA), a social-impact company with 100% impact shares, the State, a commune or a public body – **never to the members**; the statutes must say so | [C · S5] |
| A court may dissolve an ASBL that has not filed its accounting documents for two consecutive years | [C · S5] |

## Recognised as being of public utility (ARUP)

By grand-ducal decree, if the general-interest purpose goes beyond local interest, is permanent, and the ASBL carried out projects in the last three financial years. An ARUP is automatically a "large" association for accounting (full accounts and audit). Donations to it are deductible for donors (lu-corporate-tax, asbl-taxation.md). [C · S5]
