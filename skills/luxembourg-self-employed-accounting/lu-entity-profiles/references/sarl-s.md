# SARL-S (SARL simplifiée)

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

A SARL with a capital from €1, created by the law of 23 July 2016 (in force 2017). The general SARL rules apply unless stated here (sarl.md).

| Rule | Status |
|---|---|
| Capital between **€1 and €12,000** | [C · S6] |
| Contributions in cash or in kind; cash contributions may be paid up within 12 months of formation, for the whole capital (from 2 June 2026, art. 720-4). Equipment as capital is handed over in full at formation and needs its own declaration of honour: contribution-in-kind.md | [C · S6] |
| Partners: **natural persons only**, on pain of nullity – a company can never hold SARL-S shares | [C · S6] |
| **One SARL-S per person** at a time (unless inherited); a partner who joins a second one is joint guarantor of that company's later debts | [C · S6] |
| Managers must be **natural persons** | [C · S6] |
| Formed by **private deed** or notarial deed – no notary required | [C · S6] |
| Object: activities under the business-permit law of 2 September 2011 (crafts, commerce, industry and the liberal professions covered by that law) | [C · S6] |
| The founder first applies for the business permit – with the draft articles (« projet d'acte constitutif ») – and puts the permit number in the RCS file; « l'octroi définitif de l'autorisation d'établissement requiert l'enregistrement des statuts au registre de commerce » | [C · S2, S16, S18] |
| Legal reserve: at least 5% of net profit each year **until capital plus reserve reach €12,000** – not 10% of capital as in a SARL | [C · S6] |
| Above €12,000 of capital it must change its legal form | [C · S2] |

- **Liberal professions**: those under the permit law (e.g. architects, accountants) **can** use a SARL-S. Lawyers, doctors, dentists, vets and réviseurs d'entreprises are governed by other laws, so in our reading they cannot. The popular claim "liberal professions cannot use a SARL-S" is wrong as a general statement. [I · S18; C · S6]
- No existing company can be converted **into** a SARL-S (art. 100-3 excludes it as a target form); a SARL-S can only be created new, and can itself become a SARL. [I · S6] Whether that conversion needs a notary: open point ENT-O-01.
- Fees: an RCS registration costs much less than for a SARL (figures/2025.md).

## Order of steps

1. Check that the name is free – the public search on lbr.lu first, then the LBR's name certificate if you want proof; neither reserves the name (rcs-rbe-and-publication.md "Company name") – and that the place of business qualifies – a domiciliation address does not (lu-business-permit criteria.md). [C · S2, S34]
2. Apply for the business permit on MyGuichet in the company's name, with the draft articles; the dirigeant (the gérant) carries integrity and any qualification. For a company being created, the draft « est suffisant pour l'émission d'un accord de principe »; the articles registered with the RCS are asked for before the permit itself is issued. [C · S16, S18, S40] The Ministry's letter gives the number of a provisional permit for the RCS filing [C · S37]; the Chamber of Commerce reports it usually comes within 2–3 weeks, marked « non-valable ». [P · S38] The form's declaration of honour, its « Start Your Business » question and the attachments: lu-business-permit procedure-and-fees.md.
3. Sign the articles by private deed – safest once the provisional number has arrived, so the one-month RCS deadline does not run while you wait. [I · S7, S37] Cash capital may be paid within 12 months (art. 720-4, from 2 June 2026). Guichet's SARL-S page (last updated 2019) still says the capital is « entièrement libéré au moment de la constitution » – out of date. [C · S6; S2 outdated] What to write in the articles: "Writing the articles" below.
4. Register with the RCS within one month of the deed. The permit's reference number goes on the form (the letter itself is not attached); the LBR checks that it matches the person and the company and **returns the filing if the company name differs** from the one given to the Ministry. Attach the articles, the declaration of honour on the capital (signed by each partner) and each partner's ID and proof of residence. Publication in the RESA follows; the permit becomes final with this registration. [C · S37, S7, S18] The Chamber of Commerce reports that the final permit then comes in principle automatically, 1–3 days after the RCS accepts the filing. [P · S38]
5. Declare the beneficial owner(s) in the RBE within one month (rcs-rbe-and-publication.md). [C · S22]
6. Before or at the start of activity: the company's bank account; CCSS – the gérant's entry declaration within 8 days (self-employed if more than 25% of the shares and named on the permit, lu-social-security company-managers.md); VAT registration of the company within 15 days (lu-vat); the ACD is informed (lu-business-start registrations.md). [C · S16]
7. First invoice: company name with « SARL-S », registered office, RCS and permit numbers. [C · S2]

## Writing the articles

- **Language:** the deed filed with the RCS must be in French, German or Luxembourgish. A translation into any official EU language may be filed and published at the same time; where they differ, the translation cannot be used against third parties. [C · S36 art. 22-2] The Chamber of Commerce's "English only with a French or German translation" comes to the same thing. [P · S38]
- **Registered office:** the articles need only name the commune. The precise address goes on the RCS form (and usually in the founders' decisions at the end of the deed); it must lie in that commune and match the national register of localities and streets – the form has a box for an address missing from that register. [C · S37] The articles may allow the gérant to move the registered office to another commune and amend the articles accordingly; otherwise changing the articles needs partners holding three quarters of the capital. [C · S6 art. 710-26] A move of the place of business is also notified for the permit (lu-business-permit procedure-and-fees.md).
- **Name:** exactly the same as in the permit application (step 4). [C · S37]
- **Sole partner:** exercises the powers of the partners' meeting; the decisions are recorded in minutes or in writing. [C · S6 art. 710-29]
- **Capital clause:** say whether cash contributions are paid at formation or within 12 months (art. 720-4); templates written before 2 June 2026 only say "fully paid". [C · S6; P · S39]
- **Templates:** Guichet's SARL-S page links no articles template, only the declaration of honour on the capital (French and English Word files; Annexe A cash, Annexe B contribution in kind). [C · S2, S46] A Guichet model deed for a one-person SARL-S does exist at a direct address (Word file of 2017, written before 2 June 2026 – its capital clause says « entièrement libéré »). [C · S45] The Chambre des Métiers model 04 (private deed, one or several partners, updated 18 February 2025) also suits a commercial SARL-S once its object clause is written for the permit activity – the company-law rules do not differ between craft and commerce. [P · S39; I · S6] The Chamber of Commerce brochure refers to Word templates on Guichet; the one-person model was found on 20 November 2026, a model for several partners was not looked for. [P · S38; C · S45]
- **Filing format** (ministerial regulation of 27 May 2016, as the LBR summarises it): the articles as a text PDF/A, A4, black type on white, no images, a blank strip of at least 35 mm at the top of page 1, and a header with the name, the legal form and the precise registered-office address. The other documents (ID, declaration of honour, proof of residence) are filed as PDF/A and not published. [C · S37]

## Worked example (invented figures)

A SARL-S with €1,000 capital and €40,000 net profit: 5% = €2,000 goes to the reserve (capital plus reserve still far below €12,000). [I · our arithmetic on S6]
