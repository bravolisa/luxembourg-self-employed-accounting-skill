# SARL (société à responsabilité limitée)

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

Law of 10 August 1915 on commercial companies, arts 710-1 ff. (numbering since 2017).

## Forming it

- Minimum share capital **€12,000** (art. 710-5), **fully subscribed** at formation. Since 2 June 2026 (law of 18 May 2026) the €12,000 minimum may be **paid up within 12 months** of formation, or sooner if the articles say so; any capital above €12,000, any share premium and every contribution in kind are paid in full at formation (art. 710-6(1)). The notary checks the subscription and what is paid (art. 710-6(2)). [C · S6] Paying the capital with equipment – valuation without an auditor's report, what the deed says, liability, costs: contribution-in-kind.md.
- Until it is paid: the partners who still owe money are listed after the balance sheet with the amounts, and the votes on shares whose called payments are overdue are suspended (art. 710-7(4), (6)). [C · S6]
- One partner is enough (*société unipersonnelle*); at most **100 partners** – above that it must convert within a year. [C · S6]
- Formed by **notarial deed**, on pain of nullity (art. 100-4); every later change to the articles needs the same form – a notary each time (art. 100-12). [C · S6]
- The company needs its own business permit, held through a "dirigeant" (managers-and-liability.md); then RCS and RBE registration (rcs-rbe-and-publication.md).

## Running it

- Partners decide in general meeting; except for changes to the articles, no meeting is needed with 60 partners or fewer – each votes in writing. [C · S6]
- Unless the articles say otherwise, partners holding **three quarters** of the capital can amend the articles; increasing partners' commitments needs unanimity. [C · S6]
- **Single-member SARL**: since the law of 7 August 2023 the meeting and majority rules and the share-transfer approval do not apply. Contracts between the sole partner and the company (a loan, a management contract) must be recorded in minutes or in writing, except ordinary transactions on normal terms. [C · S25; S6]
- **Legal reserve**: at least **5%** of net profit each year until the reserve reaches **10% of the capital** (€1,200 on €12,000); it starts again if the reserve falls below (art. 710-23). [C · S6]
- **Share transfers** to non-partners need approval by partners holding at least three quarters of the shares (the articles may lower this, not below one half). [C · S6]
- **Annual accounts**: the partners approve the balance sheet and profit and loss account (art. 710-23). The deadline is six months after year end – managers who have not put the accounts to the partners by then commit an offence (art. 1500-2, 2°); filing follows within one month of approval (lu-accounting, companies-and-asbl.md). [C · S6]
- **Losses**: the SA's alarm procedure – a meeting on dissolution when losses cut net assets below half, then a quarter, of the capital (art. 480-2) – sits in the SA chapter; the SARL chapter (arts 710-1 to 710-31) has no such rule and does not refer to it. [I · S6] An owner with negative equity should see an accountant; insolvency rules are a separate question.
- Managers (gérants): managers-and-liability.md. Accounts: lu-accounting, companies-and-asbl.md. Taxes: lu-corporate-tax.

## Ending it

- A sole partner who dissolves the company without liquidation must attach certificates from the CCSS, the ACD and the AED showing that contributions and taxes are paid, or the dissolution is void (law). Guichet says this for every voluntary dissolution of a SARL. [C · S6; S1]

## Worked example (invented figures)

A one-person SARL with €12,000 capital and €40,000 net profit: 5% = €2,000, but only until the reserve reaches €1,200 – so €1,200 if the reserve is empty. [I · our arithmetic on S6]
