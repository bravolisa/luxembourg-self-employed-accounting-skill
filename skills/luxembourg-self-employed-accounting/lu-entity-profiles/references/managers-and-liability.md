# Managers (gérants) and their liability

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

The manager's social-security status (employee or self-employed) is in lu-social-security, company-managers.md.

## Powers and dismissal (SARL)

- Each gérant can do everything necessary or useful for the company's object, except what the law or articles reserve to the partners. [C · S6]
- The company is bound by its managers' acts even beyond the corporate object, unless it proves the third party knew (publishing the articles is not proof). [C · S6]
- Unless the articles say otherwise, gérants can be dismissed only for legitimate reasons. [C · S6]
- A legal person can manage a classic SARL; a SARL-S needs natural persons. [C · S1; S6]

## Civil liability

- Towards the company, for carrying out the mandate and for management faults (art. 441-9, applied to SARLs by art. 710-16). [C · S6]
- Jointly towards the company and third parties for damage from breaches of the companies law or the articles. [C · S6]

## Tax and VAT liability

- **Income taxes**: the legal representatives must fulfil all the company's tax duties – filing on time as well as paying its taxes from the funds they manage (§ 103 AO). [C · S15]
- They are **personally liable** next to the company only if three conditions are met: a **culpable** breach in that capacity, a loss for the State, and causation (§ 109(1) AO). A mere breach is not enough. [C · S15]
- The ACD enforces this with a *bulletin d'appel en garantie* (§ 118 AO). [C · S15]
- **VAT**: managing directors, gérants and any de jure or de facto daily manager must make sure the company meets its VAT duties and pays its VAT; they are personally and jointly liable when, through **culpable** non-performance, VAT was not declared or paid; the AED can issue a *décision d'appel en garantie* (arts 67-1 to 67-3 VAT law). [C · S14]
- Being "only a name" on paper is no protection: the duties attach to the role. [I · S15, S14]
- Liability for social-security contributions is a separate regime – not covered yet.

## The dirigeant on the business permit

- A company must designate at least one natural person, the **dirigeant**, whose integrity, management role and real link with the company are checked – the owner or an agent registered with the RCS, e.g. a gérant. [C · S16]
- The permit is issued to the enterprise: to the sole trader personally, or to the company the dirigeant manages. [C · S16]
- The dirigeant must not have evaded social or tax charges (withholding taxes included), personally or through a company he or she managed. [C · S18]
- A company needs a **new permit** when it changes the dirigeant on whom qualification and integrity rest. [C · S18]
- After the permit, the dirigeant of a SARL, SARL-S or SA declares his or her entry with social security as employee or self-employed. [C · S16] Which status: lu-social-security, company-managers.md.
