## Before answering

1. Which form exists today, and which is being considered?
2. Activity and permit: commercial, craft, permit-based liberal profession, or a profession under its own law (lawyer, doctor…)?
3. How many founders, and are any of them companies?
4. Expected profit and risk – for "which form" questions also load lu-corporate-tax and lu-income-tax-personal.
5. Year: fees in [2025](references/figures/2025.md), [2026](references/figures/2026.md).

## Rules people get wrong most often

- A SARL needs **€12,000 of capital and a notary** – also for every later change of its articles; since 2 June 2026 the €12,000 may be paid up within a year. A SARL-S can be formed from **€1 by private deed**. [C · S6]
- A SARL-S can only have **natural persons** as partners and managers, and one person can hold only **one** SARL-S. [C · S6]
- "Liberal professions cannot use a SARL-S" is wrong: permit-based liberal professions can; professions under their own laws (lawyers, doctors…) cannot. [I · S6, S18]
- SARL-S reserve: 5% of profit until capital plus reserve reach **€12,000**, not 10% of capital. [C · S6]
- ASBL: **two founders** suffice and legal personality starts with **RCS registration** – Guichet's 2021 page still describes the old 1928 law. [C · S5; S11 outdated]
- A gérant is personally liable for the company's taxes and VAT only after a **culpable** failure – but being "only a name" is no protection. [C · S15, S14]
- Companies and ASBLs declare beneficial owners in the **RBE** within a month; sole traders do not. [C · S4, S13]
- Articles filed with the RCS must be in **French, German or Luxembourgish** – an English text can only be filed in addition, as a translation. [C · S36]
- The RCS only refuses a name that is **already registered** – one different letter is enough, adding « SARL-S » is not – and trademarks are not checked. A « dénomination libre » certificate **does not reserve** the name. [C · S36, S35, S34]
- Equipment can be the share capital of a SARL or SARL-S **without an auditor's report, whatever its value** – the founders value it and answer for the value. It stays a contribution: nothing has to be sold, there is no sale contract and no payment. Only the **tax is worked out as on a sale**: for a VAT-registered sole trader 17% VAT on the value, and income tax on the real value less the book value – unless the whole business passes. Never tell a user the equipment « has to be sold ». [C · S6, S41; I · lu-vat S24]
- Moving into a company creates a **new legal person**: new permit, new VAT registration, staff transfer automatically, and for income tax it counts as a transfer of the business. [C · S16, S26, S17; I · S19]
