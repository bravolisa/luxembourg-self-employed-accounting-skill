# Affiliation and the Mutualité des employeurs (MDE)

- **Affiliation**: file the « déclaration d'entrée pour travailleurs indépendants » with the CCSS at the latest 8 days after starting; it is not automatic when the business permit is issued. [C · S13; S1, S5]
  - **The start date** is the one the person enters on the form (« Début de l'activité »). [C · S54] A permit alone is not a start: the person « doit être en possession de son autorisation avant le début de son activité professionnelle indépendante ». [C · S13] No official text says which act marks the start. Our reading: the first day of acting as a business – work for a client, a signed contract, a sale or an invoice – and the same date for the AED (VAT registration) and, for a trader, the RCS; no invoice dated before it. [I] Open point SS-O-09.
- **MDE**: voluntary fund for self-employed people; « Son adhésion à la Mutualité est volontaire »; new members start in class 2; classes 1–4, 0.23–2.66% (2026). [C · S14, S11] While the CNS pays no sick pay to a self-employed person – until the end of the month in which the 77th day of incapacity falls – the MDE reimburses 80% of the contribution base. [C · S14, S23] The CNS pays for a month once 77 days of incapacity are reached over the 18 consecutive months before that month; this is checked month by month, so payment can stop again. [C · S26] Apply at the latest 3 months after the start of CCSS affiliation for cover from the start; otherwise cover starts on 1 January of the next year. [C · S14]
- MDE reimbursements count as business income (certificate-and-tax.md).
