# Exemptions and side activities

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

| Situation | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Low income (dispense) | Professional income not above ⅓ of the social minimum wage **per year** (monthly equivalent: figures year files): exemption from affiliation for all risks, but only **on request** – insurance continues unless the person expressly asks for the exemption. The person is then not covered for anything. Legal basis cited by the form: arts 5, 88 and 180 (paragraphs 2 and 3) CSS. **Part of a year**: « le revenu professionnel annuel est calculé en fonction de la période d'activité effective » (art. 180(2)) – compare the income of the months of activity with ⅓ of the minimum wage for those months | [C · S3, S13, S24; S28 art. 180(2)] |
| Exemption granted wrongly | If income turns out higher, the person is affiliated retroactively and contributions are calculated on the final income the ACD reports. An exempted person can rejoin retroactively only by showing a tax return with income above ⅓ of the minimum wage for that year | [C · S3] |
| Side activity | Activity next to a main affiliation (employee, another main activity, pensioner): base = real professional income, **but at least ⅓ of the minimum wage** [C · S13]. The CCSS has to know of the main affiliation to apply this base; no official text found that requires a proof from the person [I] | [C · S13; I · S13] |
| Reduced pension base (art. 244 CSS) | Main activity with income **below the minimum wage** (no lower limit): pension base = real income, at least ⅓ of the minimum wage; health and accident stay on the minimum wage; dependency on real income. Request at the start of affiliation or for a full calendar year (1 January – 31 December); the reduction is granted retroactively once the final tax bulletin for the year is in | [C · S13, S24] |
| – how to ask | CCSS form « Demande de réduction des cotisations à titre indépendant »: signed, sent to the CCSS (postal address on the form), with the date from which the reduction applies (« à partir du ») and the estimated or fixed income of the year. The form repeats the effect: health (cash benefits) and accident on the minimum wage, dependency on real income, pension on real income but at least ⅓ of the minimum wage, and « Le revenu servant de base au calcul de la pension sera diminué en conséquence » | [C · S49] |
| – stopping it, asking again | The reduction applies only « sur demande » (art. 244); without it the pension base is the normal minimum (the minimum wage, art. 241). The CCSS takes the request for whole financial years (1 January–31 December) and grants it on each year's final tax assessment, so giving it up brings the minimum-wage base back from 1 January, and it can be asked again for a later full year – our reading; no text covers a withdrawal: SS-O-08 | [C · S28 arts 241, 244; S13, S24; I] |
| Paying more for the pension | Inside compulsory insurance the base is the real income, recalculated after the tax bulletin – paying on more than that is not possible (art. 241). Voluntary **complementary** pension insurance tops up compulsory contributions: open to someone with 12 months of insurance in the three years before the request (RGD of 5 May 1999, art. 1, al. 2; CSS art. 173); it starts on the first day of the month after the request (art. 3), and the chosen base runs on, adjustable each January (art. 4). The regulation asks for no reduction of activity, and the CCSS describes it as a way to increase contributions [I · S50, S52]; whether it can run beside the art. 244 reduction: SS-O-08 | [C · S28 arts 173, 241; S50; O] |
| Old-age pensioner over 65 | Own-account work after 65 with an old-age pension: no pension insurance (« ne sont pas soumis à l'assurance », art. 178(1)); health and dependency stay. No such rule for an early pension | [C · S28] |
| Foreign pension scheme, work here up to a year | Exemption on request, branch by branch: pension (art. 181), health (art. 6), accident (art. 86) | [C · S28] |
| Illness | Pension contributions waived on request for whole calendar months in which illness stops the business and the cash benefit is suspended; not if someone else runs the business (art. 244(2)–(3)) | [C · S28] |
| Occasional activity | Not habitual, duration known in advance, under 3 months per calendar year, and the person's only professional activity (art. 4(1) CSS): exemption on request from health and pension insurance; accident insurance stays mandatory | [C · S13] |
| Cultural or sporting activity for a non-profit (asbl) | A side activity in the cultural or sporting field for an asbl, with income of at most ⅔ of the minimum wage a year (pro rata for a part year): exempt **on request** from health insurance (art. 4(4) CSS) and pension insurance (art. 179(2) CSS). **Accident insurance stays** – no accident provision covers this case. The law only requires a side activity (« à titre accessoire ») and income that « ne dépasse pas » ⅔; the CCSS page adds that it is carried on **next to a salaried job** and words the limit as « inférieur à » ⅔ – follow the CCSS in practice. | [C · S28 arts 4(4), 179(2); S13] |

Example (2026, Jan–May figures): a side activity earning €800 a month is billed on the floor of €901.25, about €230 a month at 25.15%; income this low could instead be exempted on request, losing the cover. [I · arithmetic on S11, S13]

Example (2026, invented figures): a manager starts on 1 September and earns €2,000 a month – €8,000 for four months, against about €3,695 for those months (⅓ of €2,771.33 × 4): no exemption. Dividing the €8,000 over twelve months would wrongly suggest one. [I · arithmetic on S28 art. 180(2), S9]

Caution for users: exemptions also remove cover. The wiki advises they are rarely a good idea unless the person is insured another way. [I · S1]

One way to stay insured for health: a spouse or partner (marriage or PACS) and children can be co-insured free of charge through the CNS on the main insured person's affiliation, but only if they have no cover of their own; for a spouse or partner it is not automatic and must be requested; children qualify up to 29 if their resources are below the minimum guaranteed income. [C · S25] So an exempted self-employed person with an insured spouse or partner may ask for co-insurance; check with the CNS first. [I · S25]

## What a lower or higher pension base changes

- The old-age pension at 65 needs **120 months** of insurance (art. 183). Months are counted per calendar month; a self-employed month counts with at least 10 calendar days (art. 175). [C · S28] A month on the reduced base still counts as a full month of insurance. [I · S28 arts 171, 175]
- The pension has two parts (art. 214): a **flat-rate part** that grows by one fortieth per year of insurance, up to 40 years, and a **proportional part** – a rate times the sum of the revalued contribution bases. [C · S28 art. 214] So the base mainly changes the proportional part; the years count either way. [I · S28 art. 214; S49]
- Whether paying more is worth it depends on the career, age and other pensions – the skill gives no general answer. The CNAP gives a pension estimate on request to insured people aged 56 to 64 (MyGuichet or post). [C · S51]
