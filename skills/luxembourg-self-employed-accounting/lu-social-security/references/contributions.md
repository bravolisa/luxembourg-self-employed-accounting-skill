## Contribution base

- Minimum: the social minimum wage. Maximum: five times it (not for dependency insurance, which has no minimum or maximum). Side-activity floor: one third of it. [C · S10, S11, S6]
- The amounts follow the minimum wage and can change during a year (1 May 2025, 1 June 2026): take them for the right months from [figures/2025.md](figures/2025.md) and [figures/2026.md](figures/2026.md). Annual amounts are the monthly amounts of each month added up. [I]

- **What lowers the base**: only the income itself – for the self-employed the profit, so a business expense lowers both CCSS and income tax. Special expenses (private pension contract, insurance, RCPi contributions) lower income tax only. Below the minimum base a lower profit no longer lowers CCSS, except the dependency contribution. And the pension contribution is not lost money: it builds the person's pension rights. [I · S6, S22; lu-income-tax-personal deduction-review.md]

## Provisional base and regularisation

- **Time limits:** the prescription of contributions follows the law on the recovery of direct taxes (CSS art. 432(1)); contributions withheld from wages but not paid over prescribe only after 30 years. A refund of contributions paid wrongly must be claimed within **five years** from the end of the year in which they were paid (art. 432(2)). [C · S28 art. 432]

- The CCSS bills every month on a provisional base. **First year**: the social minimum wage unless the person gives a more realistic estimate of income (« à défaut d'une estimation plus réaliste »), or ⅓ of it for a side activity next to a salaried job. A main activity is never billed below the minimum wage, except with the reduced pension base (exemptions-and-side-activity.md). [C · S6, S24]
- **Later years**: « le dernier revenu connu », either from the tax administration (ACD) or from the person's own adjustment request, which can be filed at any time. [C · S6, S22]
- **Regularisation**: once the ACD sends the definitive income from the tax assessment, the CCSS recalculates the year and bills or refunds the difference. [C · S6, S22] This often arrives one to two years after the year. [I · S1]
- Invoices run two months behind the month billed. [C · S21]
- Pay within 10 days of the statement, even when disputing it; late-payment interest is 0.6% per full calendar month. A direct-debit authorisation can be given to the CCSS. [C · S21, S24]
- **Advice**: adjust the provisional base early if income changes significantly, up or down, to avoid a large catch-up bill. [I · S1, S4]
- **How to adjust**: order the personalised « formulaire d'adaptation du revenu provisoire » on the CCSS website (matricule, first year, e-mail); it comes by post to the address in the national register within 3–4 working days. Enter the expected yearly income per category and per year (up to five years back), sign and return it; the change shows on the next month's statement. Once the ACD has sent a definitive income, only an ACD corrective certificate (« certificat rectificatif ») changes it. [C · S35] Plain-language guide: the wiki article on adjusting monthly CCSS payments. [S7]
