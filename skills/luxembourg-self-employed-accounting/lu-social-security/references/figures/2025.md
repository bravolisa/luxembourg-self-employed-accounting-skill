# CCSS figures – 2025

The social minimum wage changed on 1 May 2025 (index tranche); use the period of the income month. [C · S10, S19]

| Item (monthly) | 1 Jan – 30 Apr 2025 | 1 May – 31 Dec 2025 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social minimum wage (unskilled, 18+, full time) = minimum contribution base | €2,637.79 | €2,703.74 | [C · S8, S10, S11] |
| Maximum contribution base (5×) | €13,188.96 | €13,518.68 | [C · S10, S11] |
| One third of the minimum wage (side activity floor, exemption limit) | €879.26 | €901.25 | [C · S11] |
| Helping spouse cap (2×) | €5,275.59 | €5,407.47 | [C · S11] |
| Dependency abatement (¼ of the minimum wage) – **not** for the self-employed (social-security code art. 377: their point 4 is not on the list) | €659.45 | €675.93 | [C · S8 for the amount] |

The 1 January 2025 values include a legal increase of the minimum wage of 2.6%; 1 May 2025 added an index tranche (+2.5%). [C · S19]

Full calendar year 2025: the minimum contribution base adds up to **€32,181.08** – 4 months × €2,637.79 + 8 months × €2,703.74. [I · our arithmetic on S10, S11]

Full calendar year 2025: the exemption limit (one third of the minimum wage per year) adds up to **€10,727.04** – 4 months × €879.26 + 8 months × €901.25. For a partial year, check with the CCSS. [I · our arithmetic on S11]

## Rates 2025

| Branch | Rate | Status |

| Health – cash benefits | 0.50% | [C · S11] |
| Pension | 16.00% | [C · S11] |
| Accident | 0.70% base rate × bonus-malus factor 0.85–1.50 (0.595–1.050%) | [C · S11] |
| Dependency insurance | 1.40% on all income, no minimum and no maximum | [C · S11, S6] |
| Mutualité des employeurs (optional) | Class 1–4: 0.07 / 0.99 / 1.48 / 2.64% | [C · S11, S14] |

Typical total without the Mutualité, accident factor 1.0: 22.80% on the capped base + 1.40% dependency = **24.20%**. With the Mutualité: 24.27% (class 1) to 26.84% (class 4). [I · our arithmetic]
