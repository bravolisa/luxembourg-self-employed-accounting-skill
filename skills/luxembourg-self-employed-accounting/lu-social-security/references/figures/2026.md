# CCSS figures – 2026

The social minimum wage changed on **1 June 2026** (index tranche 968.04 → 992.24). Use the period of the income month. [C · S9, S10, S19]

| Item (monthly) | 1 Jan – 31 May 2026 | From 1 Jun 2026 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social minimum wage (unskilled, 18+, full time) = minimum contribution base | €2,703.74 | €2,771.33 | [C · S9, S10, S11] |
| Maximum contribution base (5×) | €13,518.68 | €13,856.63 | [C · S9, S10, S11] |
| One third of the minimum wage (side activity floor, exemption limit) | €901.25 | €923.78 | Jan–May [C · S11]; June [C · S9 – IGSS « Seuil de revenu en matière d'anti-cumul », the official ⅓ value, €901.25 in January as the CCSS] |
| Helping spouse cap (2×) | €5,407.47 | €5,542.66 (2 × €2,771.33, exact) | [C · S11]; June [C · S24 rule « le double du salaire social minimum »; S9 amount] |
| Dependency abatement (¼ of the minimum wage) – **not** for the self-employed (social-security code art. 377: their point 4 is not on the list) | €675.93 | €692.83 | [C · S9 for the amount] |

Full calendar year 2026: the minimum contribution base adds up to **€32,918.01** – 5 months × €2,703.74 + 7 months × €2,771.33. [I · our arithmetic on S9, S10, S11]

Full calendar year 2026: the exemption limit (one third of the minimum wage per year) adds up to **€10,972.71** – 5 months × €901.25 + 7 months × €923.78. For a partial year, check with the CCSS. [I · our arithmetic on S11, S9]

Typical total without the Mutualité, accident factor 1.0: 23.75% on the capped base + 1.40% dependency = **25.15%**. With the Mutualité: 25.38% (class 1), 26.10% (class 2), 27.81% (class 4). The often-quoted "25–28%" holds only with the Mutualité. [I · our arithmetic]
