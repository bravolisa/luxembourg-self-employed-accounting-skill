# The CCSS certificate and the tax return

## Income tax

- Compulsory health, accident and pension contributions of the self-employed are **special expenses** (dépenses spéciales), deductible without limit (art. 110 n° 2 LIR). They go in the personal return, not in business expenses. [C · S16]
- The **dependency-insurance** contribution is not deductible as a special expense, an operating expense or a cost of earning income. [C · S17, S18]
- In Form 100 they go on page 16, boxes 1601–1604 (« Prélèvements et cotisations en raison de l'affiliation obligatoire des salariés et des non-salariés »). [C · lu-income-tax-business S23]
- Voluntary Mutualité contributions are deductible under art. 110 n° 4 LIR. [C · S23]
- **Mutualité reimbursements** (MDE sick-pay compensation) are taxable since tax year 2011 (art. 11 n° 1a LIR) [C · S23]; the 2025 MyGuichet return asks for them in business income in both the commercial and the liberal-profession branches. [P · S4 observed form wording]

## Municipal business tax (commercial and craft activities)

All compulsory personal contributions paid in the year (« versées en 2025 » – the year of payment, arrears included) to a Luxembourg social-security institution, **including** the dependency contribution, reduce the business profit used for the municipal business tax: Form 110, line 95, « suivant certificat ». [C · S18; lu-income-tax-business S20, lu-income-tax-business S35] For income tax they remain special expenses (dependency excluded), as above.

## Worked example (invented figures)

| Item | Amount | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Compulsory contributions on the certificate | €9,600 | – |
| of which dependency insurance | €600 | Not a special expense [C · S17] |
| Reduces the profit for ICC (commercial activity only) | €9,600 | All compulsory contributions incl. dependency, Form 110 line 95 [C · S18; lu-income-tax-business S20, lu-income-tax-business S35] |
| Special expense for income tax | €9,000 | €9,600 − €600 [C · S16] |
| Mutualité reimbursement received | €1,200 | Add to business income [P · S4] |

The 2025 certificate (« Certificat des cotisations sociales payées en 2025 pour votre activité indépendante ») shows « Cotisations déductibles » – the special expense, Form 100 box 1601/1602 –, « Cotisations non-déductibles » (the dependency insurance) and « Total cotisé » – Form 110 line 95 –, plus a table of the Mutualité reimbursements received. Its footer says it can be attached to Form 100 or to Form 163. [P · S36 observed]

## Where the certificate goes

The certificate is a mandatory attachment in the MyGuichet return for liberal professions (both methods); see lu-annual-return. [P · S4 observed]

## Domestic employee certificate is a different document

A CCSS remuneration extract for a private-household employee is not a self-employed contribution certificate. Its gross, employee contributions, dependency, flat tax, credits and net have separate roles; a monthly net registration must not be added again. Use lu-income-tax-personal domestic-employment-income.md for the regime and regularisation request, and employee-tax-credits.md for CCSS payment distinctions. [C · lu-income-tax-personal S108; lu-income-tax-personal S110; lu-income-tax-personal S113]

## Foreign schemes and exempt income — 2025

Read [cross-border-work.md](cross-border-work.md) before accepting foreign contribution lines. Verify compulsory legal character, the covered instrument, person and income connection;
keep exempt-income contributions separate and prevent double counting. Ordinary non-residents are not universally excluded from the specified compulsory deductions. Foreign contribution components and historic corrections may require case review. [C · S46]
