## SS-O-07 · Billing of a self-employed manager and voluntary health cover
- Question: May a self-employed person or manager who invoked the low-income exemption then insure health voluntarily? (The billing half of this point is answered: the manager is billed personally.)
- Why it matters: Whether "health without pension" is possible for a low earner.
- Working answer: Billing: the manager owes the contributions personally – for the self-employed and the managers assimilated to them the law puts the whole charge on the insured person (arts 32, 240), and a company must pay and withhold only for people who work for another's account (art. 428(2)). [C · S28] The CCSS bills a self-employed person on a personal account – the person's own identification number followed by « 60 » – with a monthly statement in his or her name; so the manager is billed personally, not through the company's employer account. [C · S21 for the account; I for managers – high confidence, matches the 2026 case lead] Voluntary health insurance after the exemption: not settled. The law opens it only to residents who « ne peuvent bénéficier autrement d'une protection »; an exempted person can ask to come back into compulsory insurance, and a spouse's co-insurance comes first. Someone compulsorily insured for six months before invoking the exemption fits the CCSS's wording for continued insurance better than the optional route. [C · S28 arts 2, 5(3); S53 for the texts; O for the answer]
- Who can settle: CCSS.
- Status: open

## SS-O-08 · Leaving the reduced pension base, or topping it up
- Question: A self-employed person on the art. 244 reduced pension base wants to pay on the full minimum base again: from when does withdrawing the request apply, and can the reduction be asked for again in a later year? Can such a person instead add voluntary complementary pension contributions without having reduced the activity?
- Why it matters: The pension base drives the proportional part of the pension; the person needs to know when a change takes effect and whether it can be undone.
- Working answer: The reduction works by whole financial years: the CCSS takes the request « dès le début de l'affiliation ou bien pour un exercice entier, c'est-à-dire du 1er janvier au 31 décembre » and grants it for good only on the final tax assessment of each year. [C · S13, S24] So giving it up brings back the minimum-wage base for whole calendar years, from 1 January; neither the law nor a regulation makes the request irrevocable or limits how often it can be made, so it can be asked again for a later full year in which the income is below the minimum wage. [I · S28 art. 244; S13 – medium confidence] Whether a withdrawal sent during a year still counts for that same year is written nowhere. [O] Topping up instead: complementary insurance is open with 12 months of insurance in the three years before the request (RGD 1999 art. 1, al. 2); the regulation asks for no reduction of activity, and the CCSS names « augmenter leurs cotisations » as its purpose. [C · S50, S52; I for "no reduced activity needed"] Up to the minimum wage a top-up costs the same as giving up the reduction; only a base above the minimum wage adds something. [I] Whether the CCSS admits complementary insurance while the reduction runs: not stated. [O] Voluntary complementary pension contributions are special expenses (art. 110 n° 4 LIR). [I · lu-income-tax-personal S82]
- Who can settle: CCSS.
- Status: open

## SS-O-09 · Which act marks the start of a self-employed activity
- Question: The entry declaration is due 8 days after the activity starts. Which act counts as the start when the business permit was issued earlier and no work has been done yet?
- Why it matters: The 8-day deadline and the first month of contributions run from the start; people hold a permit for weeks or months before the first client.
- Working answer: The date is the one the person enters on the form (« Début de l'activité »); holding the permit is not starting, because the permit must be in hand « avant le début de son activité ». [C · S54, S13] The start is the first day of acting as a business – work for a client, a signed contract, a sale or an invoice; use the same date for the AED and the RCS. [I – no official definition found]
- Who can settle: CCSS.
- Status: open
