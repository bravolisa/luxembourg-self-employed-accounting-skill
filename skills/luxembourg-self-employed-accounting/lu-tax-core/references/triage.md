# Triage questions

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files of the domain skills (lu-vat, lu-income-tax-business, lu-social-security) win if they differ._

Ask only what changes the answer. Each row says why the question matters and what to assume if the user cannot answer.

| # | Question | Why it matters | If unknown |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which **tax year** (income year)? | Rates, thresholds, CCSS parameters and form line numbers change every year | Assume the latest completed year and say so |
| 2 | **Status**: sole trader (commercial / craft / liberal profession), company (SARL, SARL-S, SA), employee with a side activity, not registered? | Decides the income category (commercial profit art. 14 LIR vs liberal-profession profit art. 91 LIR), the forms (110 vs 152), municipal business tax, bookkeeping duties | Ask. Do not guess commercial vs liberal: some activities (IT consulting, for example) can be either, depending on the business permit and the activity |
| 3 | **VAT profile**: normal regime (annual / quarterly / monthly returns), franchise (art. 57bis), exempt activity (art. 44), cross-border SME scheme (EX number)? | Invoices, VAT deduction, which return and which boxes | Ask; a VAT number does not mean the person charges VAT (franchise users have one too) |
| 4 | **Profit method**: regular accounts (art. 18(1) LIR), accrual without regular accounts (Form 110 route B), or simplified receipts and expenses (art. 18(3) LIR)? | Timing of income and expenses, which annex, whether unpaid invoices count | Most small liberal professions use the simplified method; above the bookkeeping turnover threshold a bookkeeping duty can arise (lu-income-tax-business, figures) |
| 5 | **Residence**: Luxembourg resident? Cross-border worker? | Non-residents are taxed differently; social security coordination rules apply | Assume resident and say so |
| 6 | **Household**: single, married or partnered (joint taxation)? | Personal deductions and ceilings, co-owner rules for a home | Ask only for personal-tax questions |
| 7 | **Purpose**: planning, booking a document, or filling a specific form/box? | Depth and format of the answer | Infer from the question |

Topic-specific follow-ups:

- **VAT cross-border**: Is the customer a business or a private person? In which country is the customer established? Goods or services?
- **Home office**: rented or owned? A separate room with a door? Any other office or coworking desk?
- **Car**: private car or bought/leased for the business? Total km and business km for the year?
- **CCSS**: main activity or side activity? First year of activity?
