# Starting as self-employed in Luxembourg

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files of the domain skills win if they differ._

Follow lu-tax-core for labels and answer shape. This skill is a procedure; the rules live in the domain skills it names at each step. Source ids in [references/sources.md](references/sources.md).

```
Start-up progress:
- [ ] 1. Activity and situation clear (activity, main or side, residence, diplomas, premises)
- [ ] 2. Legal form chosen (sole trader, SARL-S, SARL)
- [ ] 3. Aid checked before ordering anything
- [ ] 4. Business permit obtained
- [ ] 5. RCS (commercial; craft when it counts as commercial) and, for companies, RBE
- [ ] 6. CCSS affiliation (8 days), VAT registration (15 days), ACD
- [ ] 7. Bank account, invoice template, records set up
- [ ] 8. First-year picture explained (CCSS provisional base, tax advances, VAT returns)
```

## 1. Understand the situation

Activity, main or side activity (an employee starting on the side?), residence, diplomas and experience, premises (shop, workshop, office, home, coworking). These decide everything below. Use lu-tax-core triage.

## 2. Legal form

Most freelancers start as sole traders; compare with a SARL-S or SARL in lu-entity-profiles `choosing-a-form.md` (liability, costs, taxes, the manager's social security).

## 3. Aid – before ordering anything

Investment, consultancy and fair aid must be requested **before** the first binding order; the €12,000 start-up aid needs own premises outside the home and no other income. lu-subsidies. [C · lu-subsidies S1, lu-subsidies S10]

## 4. Business permit

Whether a permit is needed, which qualification, documents, €50, about a week in practice: lu-business-permit. The permit comes **before** the activity. [C · S10, S7]

## 5. Registers

Commercial sole traders register with the RCS within a month – the RCS law names only « commerçants personnes physiques », so a craft sole trader registers when the business counts as commercial (most do, because they also sell goods); liberal professionals do not; companies and ASBLs also file in the RBE. lu-business-permit `rcs-and-rbe.md`; lu-entity-profiles.

## 6. CCSS, VAT, ACD, chambers

[references/registrations.md](references/registrations.md); order and deadlines: [references/timeline.md](references/timeline.md).

## 7. Set up the business

- Bank account (needed for the VAT registration; a separate account is advice, not law) – lu-accounting `records-and-habits.md`.
- Invoices: mandatory mentions, the permit number, the franchise mention if applicable – lu-vat `invoicing.md`; lu-business-permit `procedure-and-fees.md`.
- Records and bookkeeping duties – lu-accounting `bookkeeping-duties.md`.

## 8. The first year

- CCSS bills monthly on a provisional base (the minimum wage unless a realistic estimate is given) – lu-social-security `contributions.md`.
- Income-tax advances may be set on an estimate; the first return is due by 31 December of the following year; the tax credit for the self-employed comes only through it – lu-income-tax-personal `filing-and-assessment.md`, `tax-credits.md`.
- VAT returns according to the regime – lu-vat.

## When the activity stops

CCSS within 8 days, AED within 15 days, RCS, Ministry within a month – [references/registrations.md](references/registrations.md).

Close with: this is general information, not legal or tax advice; for a company or a regulated profession, see an adviser or the House of Entrepreneurship.
