# Timeline – from idea to first invoice

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files of the domain skills win if they differ._

| When | Step | Deadline or typical time | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before anything else | Business permit – the activity may not start before it | Legal maximum 3 months after a complete file; 6.62 days on average in 2025 | [C · S10, S7] |
| At the start of the project | A workshop or other classified establishment may also need an operating licence (commodo) | Can take several months – apply early | [C · S9] |
| Before applying for aid | Apply for investment, consultancy or fair aid before ordering anything | Before the first binding order (lu-subsidies) | [C · lu-subsidies S1] |
| Commercial sole trader (craft: when the business counts as commercial) | RCS registration | Within 1 month | [C · lu-business-permit S18; I · lu-business-permit S18] |
| Start of the activity | CCSS affiliation | At the latest 8 days after starting | [C · S5] |
| Start of the activity | VAT registration (also for the franchise) | Within 15 days (RGD of 1 December 2009, art. 1) | [C · S2, S15] |
| After the permit | ACD: the Ministry informs it; contact the ACD if no letter comes | – | [C · S1] |
| After the permit | Chambre de Commerce or Chambre des Métiers membership – automatic by law | – | [C · S11, S12] |
| Before the first invoice | Permit number on invoices, quotes, website and e-mails; invoice rules (lu-vat invoicing.md) | – | [C · lu-business-permit S1] |
| First year | Income-tax advances may be set on an estimate; CCSS bills on a provisional base | – | [C · lu-income-tax-personal S38; lu-social-security S6] |

A realistic order for a sole trader without premises issues: permit (about a week) → RCS (if commercial; a craft when it counts as commercial) → CCSS and VAT in the first days of activity → first invoice. [I · S7, S5, S2]
