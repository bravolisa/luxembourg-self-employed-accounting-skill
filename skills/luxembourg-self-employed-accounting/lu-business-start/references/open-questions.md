## START-O-02 · Chambre de Commerce contribution of a new sole trader
- Question: The contribution is based on the profit of the year before last, which a new business does not have. What does a new sole trader pay in the first two years?
- Why it matters: Start-up costs.
- Working answer: Probably the €14 minimum; no start-up exemption found. [I · lu-business-permit S24]
- Who can settle: Chambre de Commerce.
- Status: open
