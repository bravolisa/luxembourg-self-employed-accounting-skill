# Registrations after the permit

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files of the domain skills win if they differ._

After the permit, a sole trader affiliates with the CCSS as self-employed, files an employer declaration if hiring, and registers for VAT. [C · S8]

## Social security (CCSS)

- Affiliation is **not automatic** with the permit: the self-employed entry declaration is due at the latest **8 days** after the activity starts. [C · S3, S5] Details, exemptions and side activities: lu-social-security.
- Holding the permit is not starting: the start date is the one entered on the CCSS form, and nothing has to be filed with the CCSS, the AED or the RCS before the activity really begins. [C · lu-social-security S54, S5; I for the RCS – lu-business-permit rcs-and-rbe.md] The permit itself is public from the day it is issued (lu-business-permit procedure-and-fees.md).

## VAT (AED)

- Everyone whose activity makes them a taxable person registers with the AED – **franchise users included** (they get a VAT number but charge no VAT). The AED wrote that a VAT number is needed « no matter how minor his annual turnover will be », the franchise being opted for at registration. [C · S6 art. 62; S15 art. 1; S16 art. 16; S14] The AED's written answer is a position, not a public text; the public AED franchise FAQ says the franchise is requested in the initial declaration when the activity starts. [C · lu-vat S1]
- **By law, franchise users register too:** every taxable person established in Luxembourg – franchise users included (art. 4(1)) – declares the start of its activity (art. 62(1) 1°) and is identified by a VAT number (art. 62(5)(a)), within 15 days (RGD 1 Dec 2009 art. 1). The regulation's exemption for franchise users (art. 2 e)) was deleted by the RGD of 29 March 2013, art. 16. [C · S6 art. 62; S15 art. 1; S16 art. 16] Guichet's VAT-registration page (last changed 23 Jan 2025) still lists franchise users under optional registration and among those « dispensés de l'immatriculation » – out of date; do not cite it for this. [C · S2 for Guichet's wording]
- Guichet: register within **15 days** of starting; the VAT law states the duty to declare the start, change and end of the activity (art. 62). [C · S2, S6] The 15 days are in the RGD of 1 December 2009, art. 1 (« dans les quinze jours du commencement de cette activité »), also for franchise users since 2013; ending the activity is declared within 15 days too (art. 5). [C · S15]
- After registering: renew the declaration on any substantial change in how the activity is run (a new branch of activity, a branch office, a change of legal form), and tell the competent VAT office of any change of address of your home, residence or registered office. The regulation sets no deadline for these. [C · S15 art. 4]
- The registration asks for a bank account (IBAN); Guichet says to open one first, although no law found requires a separate business account for a sole trader (lu-accounting records-and-habits.md). [I · S2]
- Franchise or normal regime: lu-vat profiles-and-thresholds.md.

## Income tax (ACD)

- When a permit is granted to a natural person, the Ministry of the Economy informs the ACD; the ACD normally writes to the new business. If it does not, contact the ACD to file the initial declaration. [C · S1]
- First return, advances and the tax credit for the self-employed: lu-income-tax-personal, filing-and-assessment.md.

## Chambers

- **Chambre de Commerce**: natural persons with a commercial, industrial or financial activity are members by law from the day of their RCS registration. [C · S11] Liberal professionals who are not traders are not members. [I · S11] Contribution on a start-up: open point START-O-02.
- **Chambre des Métiers**: membership starts by law on the day the craft permit is granted; Guichet still offers an affiliation form. [C · S12] Contribution amounts: lu-business-permit figures.

## Sector extras

- HORECA: registration as a food business with the Food Safety Division. [C · S13]
- Taxi, security, architects and others: lu-business-permit special-sectors.md.

## When the activity stops

CCSS deregistration within **8 days**, AED cessation declaration within **15 days**, RCS deregistration (paid), tell the Ministry of the Economy within **one month**; no separate notice to the ACD is needed. [C · S4] The last annual VAT return is due within two months of stopping (lu-vat, normal-regime.md). [I]
