# Own home and private letting

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

How the home office of a self-employed person shares the mortgage interest is a business question and still open (lu-income-tax-business, IT-O-03). This file covers the private side.

## Own home (*habitation personnelle* / *selbstgenutzte Wohnung*)

- The notional rental value (*valeur locative* / *Nutzungswert*) still exists but is set at **0%** of the unit value. Deductible loan interest therefore creates a negative rental income that reduces other income. [C · S16; I · S21]
- From tax year 2023 the rental value starts when the owner can **freely dispose** of the home (availability), no longer when it is occupied; homes occupied before 2023 keep the occupation date. [C · S20]
- **Interest ceiling from tax year 2024** (RGD of 20 December 2024): interest is deductible **in full** for the year the rental value is fixed and the following year; then up to **€4,000** a year (years 2–5 after), **€3,000** (the next 5 years), then **€2,000** – each ceiling multiplied for the jointly taxed spouse or partner and each child giving the taxpayer a child reduction. [C · S27]
- The 2025 Form 100 shows the same rule as availability dates: available after 31.12.2023 – full; 2020–2023 – €4,000; 2015–2019 – €3,000; before 2015 – €2,000 (forms/2025/form-100.md). [C · S1] For 2026 the dates move on by one year (figures/2026.md). [I · S27]
- The ceiling rises for each child for whom the taxpayer gets the child tax reduction (art. 122 LIR): « La majoration pour les enfants est octroyée pour les enfants pour lesquels le contribuable obtient une modération d'impôt pour enfant » (RGD of 12 July 1968, art. 4(2)). That reduction goes to the parent whose household the child belongs to, so the regulation, the ACD page and the form point to the same children. [C · S64] From tax year 2028 a draft regulation would count 50% per parent when both get the reduction (watch list W-16).
- **Before the home is available** (construction, off-plan purchase, renovation of an uninhabitable building): interest and loan set-up costs are deductible in full; maintenance costs are not. [C · S3]
- No interest deduction for a **secondary residence**. [C · S3]
- A joint mortgage of spouses or partners: in the MyGuichet assistant each enters **half** of the interest. [C · S61]
- **One spouse alone owns the home and borrowed for it**, joint taxation: the whole family ceiling still applies. The regulation raises the ceiling for the spouse on the sole condition of joint taxation and asks nothing about ownership (RGD of 12 July 1968, art. 4(2)) [C · S27, S64]; the interest goes in the owner's column, and the split between the two columns does not change a joint return's tax. [I · S27, S64] A loan signed only by the spouse who does not own the home: open point PIT-O-14.
- **Which loan interest belongs to the home** is decided by what the money was actually used for, not by the loan's name or security; for a loan used for several purposes the ratio is fixed when it is drawn and kept for its whole life. [C · S187]
- **Refinancing** a home already lived in: the set-up costs of the new loan are not deductible (after the rental value is fixed only interest counts), and the full-deduction years and ceiling steps do not start again; extra money borrowed for other things is consumer interest in special expenses. [I · S3, S20, S27] An early-repayment penalty: open point PIT-O-31.
- Home-loan interest is **not** a special expense and not in the €672 ceiling; it goes on Form 100 page 10, sheet L (detail L2). [C · S4; S1]
- Outdated pages: Guichet's page on main-residence loan costs (2018) still shows €2,000 / €1,500 / €1,000, and an ACD newsletter of June 2024 described a regime that was replaced in December 2024. Do not use them. [C · S35, S34 outdated; S27]
- Bridging loans (*crédit-relais*) when changing homes: no text gives them a rule of their own. A bridging loan used to pay for the new home is ordinary debt of that home – full deduction before availability and for the year the rental value is fixed and the next year, then the ceiling. [I · S27, S22, S187] The months in which the old and the new home are both owned (secondary-residence exclusion): open point PIT-O-04.

## Letting a private flat or house

- Net rental income = rents received − costs of earning income, declared on Form **190/210** with Form 100 – one form, also for a share in co-owned property (with Form 200); a rental loss offsets the year's other income. [C · S14]
- Deductible without limit (if the tenant does not bear them): loan interest and financing fees, property tax, communal charges, maintenance and repairs, insurance, management, cleaning, depreciation, contributions to the building's works fund. [C · S14]
- During a vacancy of less than two years, the tax office may accept costs as linked to future rents. [C · S22]
- **Flat-rate option**: 35% of gross rent, at most €2,700 per building a year, for buildings completed at least 15 years ago; interest, management, property tax and communal charges stay deductible on top. Leaving the flat regime bars a new election for the following 15 years; actual A–D costs cannot also be claimed. [C · S14]
- Works up to €2,500 incl. VAT are deductible at once, even when they are investments. [C · S14]
- **Depreciation base**: the price without the land, plus notary fees, registration duty, VAT and commissions; the land can be estimated at 20% if unknown. [C · S14]

### Depreciation rates for rented housing

| Case | Rate | Status |
|---|---|---|
| General rule since tax year 2023 | 2% a year | [C · S25] |
| At most two qualifying buildings per person in a lifetime; a co-owned building consumes one slot for each owner using the regime; completed from 2023, less than 5 years old on 1 January | 4% | [C · S95] |
| Acquired or built before 2021, while completion is less than 6 years old | 6% (transitional) | [C · S95] |
| Acquired or built in 2021–2022, while less than 5 years old | 4% (transitional) | [C · S25] |
| Energy renovation with state aid, works less than 9 years old | 6%; **10% from tax year 2026** | [C · S95 for 2025; S26 for 2026] |
| Off-plan (VEFA) flats bought 1 Jan 2024 – 30 Jun 2025 (to 30 Sep 2025 if the preliminary contract was registered by 30 Jun 2025) | 2% depreciation **plus** a special construction abatement of 4% of the base (max €250,000 a year) while less than 6 years old – the "6% VEFA measure" is these two together (art. 129f) | [C · S2; S23] |

- ACD example: a flat completed on 30 June 2019 and bought in 2020 – 6% for 2021 to 2025, then 2% from 2026. [C · S24]
- **Special real-estate abatement** (art. 129e): 1% of the 4% depreciation base, at most €10,000, for rented housing acquired after 2020 and less than 5 years old. [C · S15]
- Net rental income through an approved **social rental agency** (*gestion locative sociale*) is 90% exempt from tax year 2024. [C · S14]

## Selling property

The main residence has a separate conditional exemption. For other private property, the 2025 two-year/five-year holding-period transition and the conditional July–September extension must be checked before classifying the gain. Non-speculative gains (held more than 5 years) are taxed at half the global rate after an allowance of €50,000, €100,000 for jointly taxed spouses, over 10 years, shared with gains on substantial shareholdings (arts 99ter, 130(4), 131(1) c), 132(2)); the main residence is exempt (art. 102bis). [C · S2] A quarter of the global rate applied instead for sales from 1 January 2024 to 30 June 2025 [C · S185], and for deeds from 1 July to 30 September 2025 when the compromis was registered by 30 June 2025 [I · S186]. The date of sale is the date of the notarial deed. [C · S185] Use complex-home-and-rental-cases.md and the updated 2025 law, not the stale ACD summary alone. [C · S82 arts. 99bis, 99ter, 131; S97]

## 2025 certificate and output checks

First distinguish state assistance, exempt cash employer support and taxable employer-loan benefits.
For cash support, reconcile gross bank interest less the exempt employer portion; for an employer loan, actual interest can include the taxable deemed benefit in the deduction base. See [employer-interest-support.md](employer-interest-support.md) before using any subsidy amount.
Do not deduct principal repayments or classify a mortgage-insurance premium as interest. The annual interest ceiling is **not prorated** for a partial year. [C · S3, S21, S22]

Keep acquisition/completion, availability and occupation dates separate. The availability FAQ has a transition rule for a home acquired/completed before 2023 but occupied later: the rental-value anchor is 1 January 2023. An acquisition date alone cannot select a 2025 ceiling. [C · S20]

The ordinary equal joint mortgage is split half each in the ACD tutorial. A jointly taxed sole owner and borrower uses the whole family ceiling (above); unequal shares and a borrower who is not the owner: PIT-O-14. [C · S61; I · S27, S64; O for the crossed cases]
For 2025 the PDF debt table carries actual net interest; the allowed negative amount goes to page-10 income. The tutorial's legacy XML fields carry actual interest in L0360/L0380 and allowed positive amounts in L0480/L0540. Its screenshots concern 2024; the same field codes exist in the 2025 XSD, but runtime equivalence still needs RET-O-01 read-back. [C · S1, S61, S75; I for 2025 runtime mapping]

Unequal ownership/borrowing, individual assessment, moving, mixed use, rental cost choices and depreciation slots: [complex-home-and-rental-cases.md](complex-home-and-rental-cases.md).

## Foreign principal home — 2025

For an assimilated non-resident, qualifying foreign-home interest creates a foreign rental loss in the worldwide **rate base**, not a direct Luxembourg-source deduction. Use [cross-border-deductions.md](cross-border-deductions.md) for evidence, refinancing guards and the separate ownership/borrowing issue. [C · S139, S138] [O · PIT-O-14]
