# Form 100 (modèle 100 F) – tax year 2025, page and box map

_Figures in this file: 2025 (printed on the form). The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

The personal income tax return for residents and non-residents. All values and wording below are read from the official 2025 form. [C · S1]

- **Deadline**: « à remettre remplie et signée pour le 31 décembre 2026 », otherwise a surcharge for late or no filing. [C · S1 p. 1]
- **Unsigned returns do not count**: « Les déclarations non signées sont considérées comme non avenues ». Both spouses or partners sign page 20 when taxed together. [C · S1 p. 20]
- Business profit is worked out on the annexes (Form 110 for commercial and craft profit, Form 152 or own accounts for liberal professions – lu-income-tax-business) and only the result is carried to pages 5–6.

## 2. Household, residence and options (pages 2–4)

- **Children (page 2)**: children under 21 on 1 January 2025 (or born in 2025), children 21+ in continuous vocational training or studies, children 21+ receiving continued family allowance. Tick « modération d'impôt pour enfants » only if the child relief was **not** already given as family allowance, study aid or volunteer aid. [C · S1 p. 2]
- **Single-parent credit (CIM, box 228)**: request on page 2; the printed wording refers to CIM not paid by an employer/pension fund. Partial payment also requires reconciliation under art. 154ter(5), not rejection or a second full credit. The exact eligibility/support and cohabitation rules are in single-parent-and-child-relief.md. [C · S1 p. 2; S80–S82]
- **Civil status (301–309)**: one choice among single (301), married (302), divorced (303) and widowed (304), with the date « depuis le » (305) for 302–304; and, separately, separated under a legal dispensation (306), a judgment of legal separation (307) or a court dispensation (308), with its date (309). Page 1 addresses, and what to tick in a year of change: cross-border-and-family.md. [C · S1 p. 3]
- **Married, one spouse non-resident (310)**: joint taxation (art. 3 d) LIR) if at least 90% of the household's professional income is earned in Luxembourg by the resident spouse. [C · S1 p. 3]
- **Partners (312)**: joint taxation (art. 3bis LIR) if the partnership existed for the whole year and the partners shared a home; both sign. [C · S1 p. 3]
- **Options (316–323)**: joint taxation (art. 3), pure individual taxation (art. 3ter(2)) or individual taxation with reallocation of income (art. 3ter(3)), with split percentages (default 50/50). Married people are taxed jointly unless they choose otherwise **by 31 December 2026** for 2025. [C · S1 p. 3]
- **Non-residents (page 4)**: part-year residents may ask to be taxed as residents for the whole year (401). **Assimilation to residents (402, art. 157ter LIR)** if at least 90% of world income is taxable in Luxembourg, or the net income not taxable in Luxembourg is below €13,000; residents of Belgium also if more than 50% of the household's professional income is taxable in Luxembourg (Belgium treaty art. 24 § 4a). All income, Luxembourg and foreign, must then be declared. Same options as residents (408–415). [C · S1 p. 4]

## 5. Extraordinary charges (page 17)

- **Actual charges (art. 127 LIR)**: unavoidable charges that considerably reduce the ability to pay (e.g. illness costs with reimbursements, supporting needy parents) – details required. [C · S1 p. 17]
- **Flat abatements**: disability and infirmity by reduced working capacity with a medical certificate (RGD 7 March 1969); household help, care because of dependence and **childcare** costs (RGD 19 December 2008) – monthly amounts and months. [C · S1 p. 17]
- **Children not in the household (art. 127bis)**: children under 21 (or born in 2025) whose upkeep the taxpayer mostly (> 50%) paid; children 21+ in studies. Not granted when both parents live together with the child. [C · S1 pp. 17, 20]

## 6. Requests and withholding (pages 18–19)

- Request for the art. 32(1a) depreciation method (catching up depreciation not deducted earlier), with a tax balance sheet showing the undeducted amount: 1801/1802 commercial, 1803/1804 agricultural and forestry, 1805/1806 liberal profession. [C · S1 p. 18]
- **Investment tax credit** carried from Form 800 (boxes 213–215 there → 1814–1819 here, incl. the software credit). [C · S1 p. 18]
- Tax credit for hiring unemployed people (Form 805, ADEM certificate); overtime tax credit; special agricultural abatement. [C · S1 p. 18]
- **Child bonus (bonification d'impôt pour enfant)**: page 18 separates the ordinary post-moderation request (1828) from the transitional shared-residence request for the other household (1837). Form 104 supports the relevant parent designation; the shared-allowance bonus has its own conditions. Calculation and evidence: single-parent-and-child-relief.md. [C · S1 p. 18; S82, S83, S85]
- **No box for the tax credit for the self-employed (CII) or its CO2 credit**: the ACD grants them in the assessment from the profit on page 5 or 6 and the compulsory affiliation (tax-credits.md). [C · S1]
- Page 19: Luxembourg tax withheld on salaries, pensions, dividends, tantièmes and savings interest; foreign tax creditable under a treaty or an annex; DAC6 question on reportable cross-border arrangements (boxes 1915/1916). [C · S1 p. 19]

## 7. Summary (page 20)

Net income per category (commercial and agricultural, liberal 2009–2012, salaries, pensions, capital, rental, miscellaneous) → total net income – special expenses → taxable income. The ACD then applies the income abatements: extraordinary charges (arts 127, 127bis), the extra-professional abatement (art. 129b), the special real-estate abatement (art. 129e), and the special construction abatement (art. 129f). CIM (art. 154ter), also shown in « Réservé à l'Administration », reduces tax and must not be subtracted from box 2038 taxable income. [C · S1 p. 20; S82]

## 8. Filling in the PDF – what the form leaves to you

Learned by filling the 2025 PDF (October 2026). The PDF adds figures up; every amount, including the capped ones, is the filer's.

- **The filer applies the ceilings.** Box 1471 takes « le montant le moins élevé » of the premiums (1468 + 1469) and the ceiling (1470); 1527/1528 the private-pension amounts within €3,200 each; 1551/1552 the lower of the home-savings contributions and the ceiling; 1554 the flat minimum (€480, with a second salary-recipient amount subject to the article 113(2) low-income reduction and liability period; payroll-certificate-deductions.md). [C · S1 pp. 14–15] The total 1553 adds only 1301–1309, 1471, 1501–1502, 1527, 1528, 1551 and 1552, and the higher of 1553 and 1554 goes into 1639. [I · S1, read from the PDF's calculation scripts]
- **Own-home interest is a minus figure** in 1021–1024: the rental value is 0%, so the deductible interest is a negative rental income; the form adds these boxes into the totals 1029–1032 (page 20, 2025–2028). [I · S1; home-and-rental.md]
- **Page 20 repeats the income-page totals** as the same fields (e.g. 2009 = 648, 2013/2014 = 759/760, 2025 = 1029, 2037 = 1639), so they cannot differ. [C · S1]
- **Tax office (page 1)** is picked from a list. A resident's office depends on the commune of residence or the profession; each office's page on the ACD site lists its communes (« Le bureau d'imposition Capellen est compétent pour les contribuables des communes de … »). [C · S68]
- **No bank-account boxes** on the 2025 form (the 2024 form had them); the ACD's collection offices manage bank details (« la gestion des coordonnées bancaires »). [C · S1; S68]
- **Filing electronically**: the MyGuichet assistant can be pre-filled with an XML file in the ACD's yearly schema, and an agent files with Form 101 – lu-annual-return forms/2025/myguichet-xml.md. [C · S69]

## Verified family field names (2025; read 2026-11-04)

The printed name box has separate surname/given-name fields. Under-21/born-in-year row bases are 201, 204, 207, 210; student-21+ bases 213, 217, 221; continued-allowance base 225. For base `b`:
`A.<b>a` surname, `A.<b>b` given name, `D.<b+1>` birth date/ID, `C.<b+2>` rebate request;
student rows add `A.<b+3>` institution. Suffixes use four digits. Allowance already received means no rebate tick. CIM is the separate `C.0228`. [C · S1]

Mortgage debt rows start at 1033, 1038, 1043: creditor `A.<b>`, economic relation `A.<b+1>`, balance `N.<b+2>`, actual net interest taxpayer/spouse `N.<b+3>/<b+4>`. Home A uses `A.1048` locality, `A.1049` number, `A.1050` street, `D.1051` availability date. Allowed interest reduces income through negative `N.1021/N.1022`. [C · S1]

Childcare: `C.1715` request; provider `A.1716/1717`; monthly actual cost `N.1718/1719`; month count `N.1720/1721`; annual actual cost `N.1722/1723`. The abatement is separate from these costs and from taxable income `T.2038`. No single monthly average can represent a varying-cost claim safely;
use its monthly annex (lu-annual-return form100-family.md). [C · S1, S72, S73; B for the annex]

Paid gift totals remain actual amounts. Their default contribution to T.1639 is replaced by the allowed donation in the generated snapshot; downstream 2037/2038 follow it. The conflicting calculation action is removed and a calculation/carry annex is emitted. This is a documented output convention, not an officially observed portal workflow. Decimal-cent adjustment and stored/rendered values are tested. [B · S150]

PU child counts must serialize as integers, not two-decimal amounts. The residence key reference uses the direct declaration registry, not merely the nested rental-income home's attribute.
An optional retained-amount recap can be schema-valid even when numerically inconsistent; do not infer live calculation authority from it. Prior-used PU stays PDF/calculation-only in this release.
Donation carry-only input and incoming vintages remain manual; never invent a current gift to satisfy the required XML detail row. [C · S75 for structure; B/O · S150 for safeguards]

C0228 is checked only when the reviewed annual retained CIM exceeds all paid CIM. This is our paper-generation inference from its unpaid-credit wording and art. 154ter(5); XML ENF_CRE_IMP continues to request annual application, independently of payment. No-income CIM households stop because the A0237 means-of-subsistence explanation is not mapped. Neither convention changes legal entitlement; live XML interpretation remains RET-O-01.
