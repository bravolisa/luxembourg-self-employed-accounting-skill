# Special employment payments — 2025 manual form map

Figures in this file: 2025 – the year files win if they differ.

Read employer-interest-support.md, termination-compensation.md and domestic-employment-income.md before mapping. A valid box or XML enumeration does not establish entitlement. [B]

For reviewed wholly Luxembourg-taxable salary, include the gross once in its salary contract row on page 7; specify the allowed other exemption in **738/739** with explanation **742**. Contributions go to their separate deductible-contribution category only to the legally deductible extent; tax withholding goes to **1901/1902**. This is a manual bridge from the certificate, not a full payroll recalculation. [C · S1; I for bridge]

Employer interest support uses ECSP PPPH for the gross benefit and its exemption. Salary gross must contain the benefit once; reviewed other exemptions use738/739 with explanation 742. Home interest follows pages 10–11 sheetL/L2 after the cash/loan reconciliation, then the applicable cap. Consumer interest follows the separate special-expense category. These are reviewed manual mappings; the household generators stop. [C · S169, S1; I for bridge]

Domestic flat pay on an established regularisation request uses rowE722/723; contributions 1601/1602 and withholding 1901/1902. See salary-certificates.md for exempt-income columns, all-contract scope and XML uncertainty. [C · S1; B/O]

Qualifying extraordinary income is disclosed in page 12 EX after inclusion in its original income category; it is not a second receipt. The annual household generator does not calculate article 131 relief. [C · S1, S82; B]
