# Form 100 — 2025 cross-border and family fields

The official 2025 French PDF was opened and pages 3, 4, 10 and 16 checked visually on 2026-11-04; pages 1, 3 and 20 were read box by box, with the fillable PDF's fields, on 2026-11-11.
This is a PDF map. The household-file fields for a change of status or address during the year are in lu-annual-return forms/2025/family-events.md; the map does not verify a non-resident tax balance. Use the legal guides before selecting a request. [B]

## Page 1: identity and addresses

Two columns: the taxpayer (left) and the spouse or partner (right). [C · S1 p. 1]

| Boxes (taxpayer / spouse or partner) | What |
|---|---|

| 105 / 106 | National identification number (13 digits), or the date of birth |
| 107 / 108 | Place of birth |
| 109 | File number – « à indiquer obligatoirement (si attribué) » |
| 110 / 111, 112 / 113, 114 / 115 | Profession, daytime telephone, e-mail |
| 116, 117 / 118, 119 | Current address (« Domicile ou séjour habituel actuel »): house number, street |
| 120, 121 / 122, 123 | Postcode, locality |
| 124 / 125 | Country |
| 126 / 127 | Earlier address: « Du 1/1/2025 au » – the date until which it applied |
| 128, 129 / 130, 131 | Earlier house number, street |
| 132, 133 / 134, 135 | Earlier postcode, locality |
| 136 / 137 | Earlier country |
| 138–141 | Non-residents: foreign tax number and the country that issued it |

- The earlier-address block is filled « uniquement en cas de changement entre le 1/1/2025 et le 31/12/2025 ». [C · S1]
- A spouse or partner who lives elsewhere has no block of their own: the own current address goes in the right-hand column (118, 119, 122, 123, 125). If that spouse moved out during 2025, the former shared address and its last day go in 127, 130, 131, 134, 135 and 137. [I · S1 – from the layout]
- One earlier address per person; a second move in the year has no place on the form. [C · S1] Whether « actuel » means the day of filing or 31 December is not stated: lu-annual-return RET-O-06. [O]

## Page 3: civil status – what the form offers

- **301–304 are one choice** (single, married, divorced, widowed). A printed brace ties the date 305 « depuis le » to 302, 303 and 304. The form has no status for partners and none for an informal separation. [C · S1 p. 3]
- **306–308 are a second, independent choice** under « Séparé(e) »: 306 « en vertu d'une dispense légale », 307 « en vertu d'un jugement de séparation de corps », 308 « en vertu d'une dispense de l'autorité judiciaire »; a brace ties the date 309 to them. A status and a separation can therefore both be ticked. [C · S1 p. 3]
- 306 has no use for a Luxembourg decision: Luxembourg law has no dispensation by law at present. [C · S199; I for the box] An informal separation fits none of 306–308. [I · S199]
- **312–315 are the partners' joint request, not a status.** 312 asks for joint taxation and declares that « le partenariat a existé du début à la fin de l'année d'imposition 2025 »; 313 is the date of the partnership declaration; 314 / 315 say whether the certificate is attached or was already presented. Leave all four empty in a year in which the partnership was declared or ended. [C · S1 p. 3; I for leaving 313 empty]
- In the fillable PDF the two choices are the fields `R.0301` (states `/celibataire`, `/marie`, `/divorce`, `/veuf`) and `R.0306`, whose state for box 308 is `/judic`; the states for 306 and 307 contain an accented letter stored in Latin-1, so a tool must write the raw state name. [C · S1 – read from the PDF's fields]

## Page 3: a year of change – what to tick

Neither the form nor any ACD page says whether to show the status of 1 January or the one after the event. [O · PIT-O-42] Until the ACD answers: show the status after the event with the event's date – « depuis le » asks for a status that still holds, and a 2025 date tells the office that the status was different on 1 January. [I – medium] Who files, jointly or alone, and in which class: family-changes.md.

| Event in 2025 | Paper boxes | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Marriage | 302 with the wedding date in 305; one joint return | [I] |
| Partnership declared | Each partner's own status in 301–304; 312–315 empty; each files alone | [I · S1] |
| Moving apart without a court decision | 302 with the wedding date; 306–309 empty; the spouse's own address in the right-hand column of page 1 and the former shared address in the earlier-address block; one joint return | [I] |
| Court permission to live apart | 302 with the wedding date in 305, and 308 with the date of the decision in 309; one joint return; a copy of the decision | [I – medium] |
| Divorce after a court permission of an earlier year | 303 with the date of the divorce in 305, and 308 with the date of the permission in 309 – the three class-2 years count from the permission; own return without a spouse column; a copy of the decision | [I – low to medium] |
| Death of a spouse | 304 with the date of the death in 305; one joint return with both columns | [I – medium; an office could equally expect 302] |
| Widowed in an earlier year | 304 with the date of the death | [I] |

The ACD applies the three class-2 years itself, on a copy of the court's permission or of the divorce judgment; there is no box to ask for them. [C · S55]

## Page 20: signatures

Two places only – « Signature contribuable » and « Signature contribuable conjoint / partenaire » – and « Les déclarations non signées sont considérées comme non avenues ». The form has no place for an agent or for heirs and no instruction for a death. [C · S1 p. 20] The year a spouse died: family-changes.md and PIT-O-41.

## Page 16: compulsory social contributions

| Income link | Taxpayer | Spouse/partner |
|---|---|---|
| Non-exempt income | 1601 | 1602 |
| Exempt income | 1603 | 1604 |

Keep contribution legal eligibility and income allocation separate from the existence of a box. Dependency is excluded; do not deduct the same contribution twice. The exempt columns do not turn foreign income into Luxembourg-taxable income. [C · lu-social-security S47, lu-social-security S46, lu-social-security S18]

Example: a validly assimilated joint household has EUR 9,000 LU independent contributions, including EUR 600 dependency, and EUR 3,000 qualifying spouse contributions linked to exempt foreign salary. With those assumed classifications, enter EUR 8,400 in 1601 and EUR 3,000 in 1604, not 1602. Invented amounts; the final rate calculation is a separate manual step. [I]

## Page 10: foreign principal-home loss

Use exempt-income taxpayer/spouse columns for the qualifying foreign-property component:
construction-period rental loss in **1019/1020**, available-home deductible interest in **1023/1024**. L2 records lender, debt purpose, year-end balance and actual interest net of subsidies/bonuses, plus property and availability details. The source certificate's interest and the capped deductible amount are distinct. [C · S1]

The qualifying foreign loss belongs in the worldwide rate computation. It does not reduce the Luxembourg-source base directly; joint owner/borrower allocation remains PIT-O-14 where not established. [C · S139, S138] [O · PIT-O-14]
