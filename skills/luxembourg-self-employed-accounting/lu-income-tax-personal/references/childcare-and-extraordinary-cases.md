# Childcare, combined claims and extraordinary charges

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. Annual amounts belong to the year files. Read the 2025 form map separately; no 2026 form mapping is implied._

## Choose the route before adding amounts

The domestic-help, dependence-care and childcare flat abatement is **one optional regime across all three categories**. Selecting it excludes an actual-cost article-127 claim for any of these categories, even when the other claim uses another provider or invoice. It can coexist with a distinct medical-cost claim or disability abatement. Do not merely check for duplicate invoices. [C · S74 care regulation art. 3(3)–(4); S73; S10]

Under the flat route, aggregate eligible costs, subtract all third-party reimbursements, apply the monthly limit, then the annual limit. There is no ordinary normal-charge deduction. Do not multiply the allowance by child, provider or care category. The two payer columns of a joint household do not create two allowances. [C · S73; I · S82 art. 3 for the joint-household application]

The child must give the taxpayer a child moderation and meet the age/disability condition. Providers need the relevant approval, including providers in another EU state; approval should appear on their documents. Qualifying care fees are not reduced by an invented standard amount for meals/upkeep. Domestic workers must principally work inside the dwelling and be declared to compulsory social security, whether employed directly or supplied by a company/association. Chauffeur, gardener and concierge costs do not qualify as ordinary domestic help under this regime. Retain the CCSS remuneration certificate for direct employment. [C · S73; S71]

Failure of a flat-route condition does not automatically disqualify an actual article-127 claim, but its independent unavoidability, extraordinary nature and normal-burden conditions must then be proved. [C · S73; S90]

## Payment year and irregular invoices

Article 108 applies to articles 127 and 127bis: expenditure normally belongs to its payment year, with the statutory exception for periodic amounts paid shortly before/after the economic year boundary. Do not copy a previous return or receipt into 2025 without dates. [C · S82 arts. 108, 127(7)]

This annual timing rule does **not** establish how a multi-month advance or arrears payment uses the **monthly** care ceiling. Keep payment dates, service periods, refunds and the provider's schedule separately. PIT-O-15 remains open; do not automatically allocate everything to the payment month, spread it over service months, or divide the annual total by twelve. The supported generator route still requires an evidenced, unambiguous monthly schedule. [O · S73 paragraph 36; B for the review stop]

## Actual extraordinary charges

Aggregate independently eligible unreimbursed actual charges, excluding categories taken through a flat regime or deducted elsewhere. Subtract **one** normal charge, using taxable income, class and eligible child count from the table in extraordinary-charges-and-abatements.md. Ordinary living costs do not become deductible because they are large. Support of a needy parent requires the parent's resources/subsidies and unavoidable need; an ordinary care-home stay is not automatically an extraordinary expense. Health-driven separate care-home accommodation while the spouse remains at home requires the circular's conditions and evidence. [C · S82 art. 127; S9; S90]

Disability: choose the flat or the evidenced actual disability costs, never both for the same expenses. The flat does not absorb the normal charge on other actual costs, such as separate medical costs. Each qualifying member of the joint household (spouse or partner, minor children) gets their own flat, and the flats add up – unlike the one common care flat. A disabled minor with their own salary uses the flat first in that salary's withholding; the household claims only the part left over. [C · S90 §§49–50; S11]

The rate comes from the decision that fixed it (accident insurance, war damage, a court) or else from the treating doctor's certificate. Loss of capacity from old age alone does not count. Keep two situations apart: [C · S90 §§38, 44–46]

- **The rate changes during the year**: each period gets the amount of its own band, in twelfths; a period without incapacity counts as 0%. Example: 35% for six months, then 75%: 225 × 6/12 + 585 × 6/12 = **€405**. The circular's own example: 90% for four months, then 60%: 645 × 4/12 + 450 × 8/12 = €515.
- **Several injuries at the same time**, each with its own compensated rate: take the highest single rate, or on request one overall rate for all of them. Never add the percentages.

Each band includes its lower limit and stops below its upper limit: exactly 35% is in the €225 band, exactly 75% in the €585 band. Near-blindness or the need for another person's constant help gives €1,455 instead of, not on top of, the €735 top band. A person taxable for only part of the year (arrival, departure) gets the flat for the whole months of liability: arriving on 15 February gives 10/12. Amounts: figures/2025.md. [C · S11; S90 §§46–48]

## Children outside the fiscal household: article 127bis

Check **each child** first: age at the beginning of the year, qualifying full-time studies for the older branch, necessary support, and the taxpayer's payment of **more than half** of total upkeep/education. Exactly half fails. Only one claimant per child. Both parents living together with the child excludes this route. Grants count in the support analysis; student loans do not. The circular also tests the child's own net income against 60% of the social minimum wage; collect the relevant period/resources rather than guessing an annual wage figure. [C · S91; S82 art. 127bis]

Then pool the actual eligible support and the ceilings for all children who independently pass. The 2025 circular's example is €4,000 and €8,000 for two qualifying children: min(€12,000, 2 × €5,424) = **€10,848**. It is not €4,000 + €5,424. The ordinary article-127 normal charge does not reduce this abatement. Excess ordinary child upkeep cannot be recycled into article 127; distinct exceptional medical costs require a separate test. [C · S91 paragraph 13]
