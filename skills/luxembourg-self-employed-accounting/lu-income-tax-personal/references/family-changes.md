# Marriage, partnerships and family changes — 2025

Figures in this file: 2025 — the year files win if they differ. Read on 2026-11-04.
This guide determines the legal route. What the tools write and calculate for a change during the year: lu-annual-return forms/2025/family-events.md and salary-balance-calculator.md; non-resident calculations stay manual.
Form positions are in [the 2025 family map](forms/2025/cross-border-and-family.md); the MyGuichet fields for a change during the year are in lu-annual-return, forms/2025/family-events.md.

## Establish the timeline first

Record each adult's residence, civil status and relevant dates: marriage, registered partnership, common homes, court permission to live separately, divorce and death. Collect the decision or certificate, not only an address change. Separately establish each child's fiscal household, benefits and support. The document checklist is a preparation convention. [B]

Resident spouses married at the start of the year and not legally or judicially separated fall within joint assessment. Residents marrying during the year also qualify for that year; spouses becoming resident during the year have the article 3(c) route. Residence periods still need their own analysis. [C · S82]

For a resident married to a non-resident, article 3(d) requires a joint request, no factual separation, and the **resident spouse** earning in Luxembourg at least **90% of the household's professional income**. The non-resident spouse must substantiate annual income. This is a different test from the per-person non-resident assimilation test under article 157ter. [C · S82]

## Foreign PACS and other registered partnerships

Joint partnership assessment requires both the partnership and a common domicile or residence throughout the entire calendar year, plus a joint request. Moving together between successive common homes does not itself break the requirement. Non-resident partners must also meet the applicable assimilation conditions. [C · S82, S129, S130]

The ACD names French PACS, Belgian legal cohabitation and existing German registered life partnerships as foreign forms. Informal cohabitation alone does not establish registered status.
Obtain a certificate from the competent authority of the registration state covering the **whole tax year**. Luxembourg civil-register entry is an optional alternative proof route for income tax, not a prerequisite for every foreign partnership. [C · S130]

A partnership does not itself put class 2 on salary or pension tax cards. An intended joint return after year-end does not change this payroll rule. [C · S130]

Eligible partners may choose joint assessment or individual assessment **with reallocation**.
The married-person option of pure individual assessment is not provided for partners; without a relevant joint option they are assessed separately on their own income. [C · S130]

The declaration year and dissolution year fail the full-year partnership condition. Death or marriage ends the partnership on the event date; marriage may instead open the married-couple route. Ending a partnership, including by death, does not grant the married-spouse three-year class-2 transition. [C · S129]

## Married-couple assessment choices

The individual-assessment request is joint. For 2025, the choice deadline is **31 December 2026**.
A choice made before or during the tax year can be changed until that deadline; a new request made after the tax year is generally irrevocable. Do not treat every past election as either permanent or freely revocable. [C · S82, S1]

Pure individual married assessment divides qualifying child-related increases to the relevant ceilings equally between spouses. It does not give each spouse the full joint child increase.
This ceiling rule is distinct from the child's fiscal-household assignment and any child tax credit or rebate. [C · S82]

## Judicial separation and divorce

For resident spouses, the transitional class-2 tariff is **separate assessment**, not continued joint assessment.
Its three years follow the year of legally or judicially authorised cessation of common life;
the later divorce judgment does not restart the clock. Prior use within the statutory five-year lookback can prevent a new transition. Provide the judicial permission or equivalent decision.
[C · S55]

### What counts, and from when (residents)

- **Key date: 1 January.** Joint taxation applies when, on 1 January, the spouses are married, both resident and do not live apart « en vertu d'une dispense de la loi ou de l'autorité judiciaire ». [C · S82 art. 3] The ACD: « à la date clé du 1er janvier à laquelle doivent être réunies toutes les conditions de l'imposition collective, seule la séparation de fait « en vertu d'une dispense » […] entre en considération ». [C · S197]
- **The year of the court's permission is the last joint year.** In the circular's example a spouse allowed to live apart on 3 January is still taxed jointly for that year; and « l'année du référé est la dernière année de l'imposition collective (année N) et la période transitoire commence l'année suivante (N+1), même si le divorce a lieu pendant cette même année (N+1) ». [C · S197; S38 – FAQ 5.4] So a permission on 1 July 2025 means a joint return for 2025, and class 2 alone for 2026, 2027 and 2028. [I – the rule applied to dates]
- **Both are needed: the court's dispensation and an actual separation after it.** A separation that began earlier counts only from the dispensation; a dispensation without actual separation does not end joint taxation. [C · S197]
- **What is a dispensation.** In practice always a court decision: an order on provisional measures during divorce or legal-separation proceedings, or the judgment itself when no order was made (« c'est le jugement au fond qui vaut »). Luxembourg law has no dispensation "by law" at present. [C · S197; S199] A permission to live at separate addresses under art. 215 of the Civil Code, outside such proceedings, is not one: joint taxation continues. [C · S197]
- **No court decision, no separate taxation.** Spouses who simply move apart stay jointly taxed, in that year and in every later year; a new address, a declaration to the commune or a letter to the ACD changes nothing. [C · S38 – FAQ 5.4; S199] The only other way to be taxed apart is the joint request for individual taxation (for 2025: by 31 December 2026). [C · S197]
- **The three years count from the permission, not from the divorce**: their start is « la date d'accomplissement de la double condition de séparation de fait et de dispense légale ou autorisation judiciaire de vivre séparément ». [C · S198; S55] Guichet's divorce page lists the divorce judgment among the events whose year is still joint; that is right only when no dispensation existed before 1 January of that year. [C · S202 for the page; I for the reading – the law and the ACD circular win]
- **Several spouses in one year** (for example a divorce and a new marriage): joint taxation is with the first spouse only – « cette personne n'est imposable collectivement qu'avec son premier conjoint ». [I · S203 – text as published in 1991; later amendments not checked] MyGuichet's assistant is closed to someone married more than once in the year (lu-annual-return, forms/2025/myguichet.md).

### The three class-2 years: assessed alone

« Pendant cette période transitoire de trois années d'imposition, le tarif de la cote d'impôt dû est calculé conformément à la classe d'impôt 2, sans qu'il y ait imposition collective des conjoints. » [C · S55] The person files alone, on his or her own income. The same holds for a widowed person in the three years after the death – without the five-year condition, which the law sets only for divorced and separated people. [C · S82 art. 119, n° 3]

| Item | In a class-2 year assessed alone | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Tariff | Class 2: twice the tax on half of this one person's adjusted taxable income | [C · S82 art. 121; S55] |
| Insurance and loan-interest ceiling | One person's ceiling plus the increase per child; no increase for a spouse (« n'est accordée que si les conjoints sont imposés collectivement en vertu de l'article 3 ») | [C · S82 art. 109] |
| Home-savings ceiling | By the person's own age; no spouse increase | [C · S82 art. 111] |
| Private pension | One person's ceiling | [C · S82 art. 111bis] |
| Flat salary costs and the special-expense minimum | Once – the doubling needs jointly taxable spouses | [C · S82 arts 107, 113] |
| Extra-professional abatement | None: it is for spouses taxed jointly | [I · S82 art. 129b; S197] |
| Employee tax credits (CIS, CO2) | On the person's own salary | [C · S82 art. 154quater] |
| Single-parent credit (CIM) | None while in class 2 | [C · S80] |
| Request for individual taxation (art. 3ter) | Not available | [C · S197] |

Amounts: the year files. Proof for the tax office: a copy of the court's permission or of the divorce judgment. [C · S55]

### Non-residents

For non-residents, apply the ACD's specific divorce-year rule. A person divorced during 2025 whose qualifying permission to live separately predates 1 January 2025 may request class 2 without joint assessment for the divorce year, subject to article 119(3)(c). Other cases where the person was married at the start of the year retain married treatment for articles 157bis and 157ter for that year. That treatment is not automatic joint class 2: ordinary married non-residents normally remain class 1 unless the appropriate assimilation/assessment route applies. Do not copy the resident timeline to a still-married non-resident without checking this specific route. [C · S131]

## Death

For resident married spouses who meet the joint-assessment conditions and have not validly elected individual assessment, the year of death is the last joint-assessment year. The survivor has the following three years' transitional class 2 and then normally class 1a, subject to a new marriage or other relevant change. A partner's death follows the partnership rules above.
[C · S38, S129]

### The year of death and the years after, in detail

- **The joint return for the year of death** is filed by the surviving spouse and holds the household's whole income: « le contribuable veuf est imposable collectivement avec son conjoint décédé (déclaration d'impôt conjointe) et déclare l'ensemble des revenus du ménage ». [C · S201; S38 – FAQ 1.7, 5.5; S200] The deceased spouse's income counts up to the death. [I · S82 art. 6]
- **A couple's ceilings and flat amounts apply in that year**, because joint taxation exists. [I · S82 – high confidence]
- **The extra-professional abatement is not cut** when one spouse dies during the year: « l'abattement de 4 500 euros n'est pas réduit si l'un des époux décède au courant de l'année, parce que dans ce cas l'assujettissement du contribuable (époux survivant) a existé durant toute l'année ». [C · S166]
- **The deceased spouse's employee credits** (CIS, CO2) cover only the months with salary. [C · S82 art. 154quater for the rule; I for applying it here]
- **Not settled:** whether the deceased spouse's own flat costs, the special-expense minimum and the spouse increases of the ceilings are cut to the full months before a death during the year. The law reduces them when the taxpayer's liability did not last all year; no text says whose liability counts in the joint year. A death on 31 December raises no such question. [O · PIT-O-40]
- **The three following years**: class 2, assessed alone (table above). **From the fourth year**: class 1a, with or without a child, until a new marriage. Class 1a alone gives no single-parent credit – that credit needs a child. [C · S82 arts 119, 154ter; S38 – FAQ 5.5; S201] Example: a spouse died in 2021 → class 2 in 2022, 2023 and 2024; class 1a from 2025. [I – the rule applied to dates]
- **The deceased's tax debt or refund** passes to the heirs who have not renounced the estate. [C · S38 – FAQ 1.6; S67]
- **Who signs.** A return is signed by the taxpayer or an agent, and an unsigned return counts as not filed. [C · S189; S1] Electronically, a joint return needs one signature per person, or one person signs for both with a mandate; when one of the two died during the tax year, the filer « peut joindre l'acte de décès à la place du contrat de mandat ». [C · S205; S56] So the survivor signs for both and attaches the death certificate. [I – high] On paper no text says who signs in the deceased spouse's place: the survivor signs in his or her own place, leaves the other empty and attaches the death certificate; with other heirs, ask the tax office first. [I – medium; O · PIT-O-41]

## Maintenance is not one deduction

Qualifying divorced-spouse maintenance follows article 109bis; support for a child outside the household has the separate article 127bis route. CIM support received is another distinct test.
Do not transfer the amount or conditions from one route to another. [I · S82]

The divorced-spouse deduction is capped at **EUR 24,000 per ex-spouse**, with full-month proration where tax liability exists for only part of the year. For a court-ordered pre-1998 divorce, the required joint annual request is irrevocable. A non-resident payer claiming this deduction uses Form 100 and must meet assimilation conditions. [C · S82, S8]

The older ACD maintenance webpage still contains a pre-1998 EUR 23,400 figure; use the 2025 coordinated law for the current ceiling. The inconsistency remains recorded in the source history. [C · S82; S8 outdated]

Do **not** automatically cap a recipient's taxable maintenance at EUR 24,000 when the payer is abroad. Article 96(3)'s capped inclusion refers specifically to a resident divorced-spouse payer; classify the foreign receipt under article 96 and the applicable treaty before fixing its taxable amount. This is a guard against an unsupported extrapolation, not a complete foreign-maintenance computation. [I · S82]

## Worked examples — invented households

| Facts | Expected route |
|---|---|
| French PACS signed in March 2025 | No 2025 joint partnership assessment; a complete 2026 year may qualify if the other conditions hold. |
| French PACS predates 2025; common home throughout 2025; foreign authority certifies the full year | Foreign proof can suffice without Luxembourg civil registration; verify non-resident assimilation separately. |
| Resident couple marries in July 2025 | Married-couple joint route for 2025, unlike a partnership first registered in July. |
| Married on 1 January; the court permits separate residence on 1 July 2025 | Joint return for 2025; each alone in class 2 for 2026–2028. |
| Spouses move apart in July 2025 without any court decision | Still jointly taxed, in 2025 and afterwards. |
| A spouse dies on 31 December 2025 | Joint return for 2025 with both incomes; the survivor alone in class 2 for 2026–2028, class 1a from 2029. |
| Widowed since 2021, no child, not remarried | Class 1a for 2025; no single-parent credit. |
| Partnership declared in June 2025 | Each partner assessed alone for 2025 in his or her own class; 2026 is the first possible joint year. |
| Resident court permission in 2024, divorce in 2025; no disqualifying prior transition | Separate class-2 assessments in 2025–2027; no restart in 2026. |
| Non-resident permission and divorce both in 2025; married on 1 January | Apply the non-resident married-treatment rule for 2025 and assess assimilation; do not grant an unconditional separate class-2 transition. |
| Resident earns EUR 60,000 professional income in Luxembourg; non-resident spouse earns EUR 20,000 abroad | 75% household professional share fails article 3(d); do not substitute the resident's personal 100% share. |

These examples apply the confirmed rules above; they are component expectations, not completed or professionally approved returns. [I]

## Evidence and remaining limits

Keep foreign annual income certificates, relevant tax assessments, partnership/civil-status certificates, successive common-address proof, judicial decisions and maintenance agreements.
For children, keep residence/assignment, benefit, study and support evidence. [B]

Foreign judgment recognition, disputed status, maintenance with a foreign payer, mixed-residence periods and exact non-resident rate calculations still require their specific legal and factual analysis. Existing household PDF/XML support is not proof of those calculations. [O]
