# Residents and non-residents

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

For 2025 country allocation, prior employers, foreign spouse income and exact assimilation tests, read [cross-border-income.md](cross-border-income.md). Social insurance has its own decision tree: lu-social-security `cross-border-work.md`; residual exceptions remain SS-O-06.

## Who is resident

- A person is a **resident taxpayer** with a tax domicile (*domicile fiscal*) or habitual abode (*séjour habituel*) in Luxembourg; a **non-resident** has neither but has Luxembourg-source income. [C · S2]
- Tax domicile: where a person has a home in conditions showing they will keep and use it. Habitual abode: where a person stays in circumstances showing the stay is not merely temporary. [C · S48, S49] A stay in Luxembourg of **more than six months** always makes a person fully taxable, also for the first six months: « Unbeschränkte Steuerpflicht tritt jedoch stets dann ein, wenn der Aufenthalt im Inland länger als sechs Monate dauert » (§ 14(1) StAnpG). [C · S67]
- Domestic residence and treaty residence are separate. For dual residents, apply the treaty sequence, beginning with permanent home and then closer personal/economic ties when homes exist in both states; a residence permit alone is insufficient. [C · S82, S132, S133, S134]
- Residents are taxed on **worldwide** income; non-residents only on **Luxembourg-source** income (art. 156). [C · S2]
- **Moving in or out**: resident and non-resident periods are taxed separately; a part-year resident can ask to be taxed as a resident for the whole year (Form 100 box 401). [C · S2, S1]

## Non-residents with a Luxembourg business

- A non-resident's commercial profit is Luxembourg income when earned through a permanent establishment or permanent representative in Luxembourg. [C · S2]
- Non-residents whose Luxembourg income bears no withholding – such as business profit – **must file a return**. [C · S45]
- Classes: married non-residents with Luxembourg professional income are in class 1 (class 2 only if assimilated); single non-residents 1 or 1a; qualifying widow/divorce transitions need their own conditions. Non-resident divorce-year rules: [family-changes.md](family-changes.md). [C · S2]
- **Minimum rate**: for non-residents with professional income, the rate on Luxembourg income other than salaries and pensions – so including business and liberal-profession profit – cannot be below **15%** unless they are assimilated (art. 157bis). [I · S2] Relief: the tax may not exceed the tax on the same income increased by the 0% band (€13,230), which helps small incomes. [C · S2]
- Non-residents with only non-professional Luxembourg income (e.g. rent from a Luxembourg flat): class 1 with a 15% minimum rate (art. 157(5)), softened by art. 157(5a). [C · S37]
- Non-assimilated non-residents cannot claim ordinary article 127 charges or CIM, and special expenses are restricted. **Article 127bis non-household-child support remains a separate eligible route**, subject to its conditions; qualifying compulsory contributions are not universally barred. [C · S82, S139, lu-social-security S46]

## Assimilation to residents (2025 detail)

- The ordinary tests are alternatives: at least 90% of the qualifying person's whole-year worldwide income taxable in Luxembourg, or net income not subject to Luxembourg tax strictly below EUR 13,000. [C · S82]
- For married non-residents, one qualifying spouse can suffice. A spouse with no Luxembourg-source income does not confer eligibility merely through low foreign income. [C · S82, S138]
- The up-to-50-working-day salary fiction is only for the 90% eligibility calculation, not the EUR 13,000 test or actual taxation. Keep it separate from treaty working-day allowances. [C · S82, S138]
- Belgian residents have the additional strictly-more-than-50% household professional-income route. [C · S139]
- On joint assimilation, both spouses' worldwide income, computed under Luxembourg rules, sets the rate applied to Luxembourg-taxable income. Evidence of annual foreign income is required. [C · S82, S138]
- Requests and dates: [the 2025 manual map](forms/2025/cross-border-and-family.md). Foreign deductions: [cross-border-deductions.md](cross-border-deductions.md). Eligibility, actual taxable income and rate income are separate calculations; the complete non-resident engine is not implemented. [B]
