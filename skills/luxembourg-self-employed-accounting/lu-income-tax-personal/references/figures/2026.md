# Personal income tax figures – tax year 2026

Everything in figures/2025.md applies unless changed below, except the newly researched special-employment section, whose values and first-payment-year anchors require a separate2026 check. The 2026 scale is the 2025 scale: the LIR in force on 1 January 2026 still carries the scale of the law of 20 December 2024, with no indexation for 2026. [C · S2, S41]

| Item | 2026 value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Scale, surcharge, class 1a parameters | Same as 2025 | [C · S2] |
| CII | Same as 2025 (€300 / €600 / nil from €80,000) | [C · S43] |
| CO2 credit for the self-employed | **€216** (profit €936–€40,000), phased out to €79,999 | [C · S43] |
| Private pension savings (art. 111bis/111ter) | **€4,500** per person, one ceiling for all contracts | [C · S6, S2] |
| Abatement for staying in work after pension age (art. 129g, new) | Up to **€9,000** a year (€750 a month), self-employed included | [C · S31] |

| Own-home interest ceilings (by availability date) | Available in 2025 or 2026 – full; 2021–2024 €4,000; 2016–2020 €3,000; before 2016 €2,000; × household members | [I · S27 – our reading until the 2026 Form 100 is published, watch list W-08] |

## Bill for 2026 (voted on 28 October 2026, not law yet)

CIC indépendant 2026 (bill 8775, first vote 28 Oct 2026 – watch list W-04): a credit for 1 June – 31 December 2026, once per person, with compulsory affiliation (a second one for a spouse only as helping spouse), only through the assessment; nothing below €13,500 of annual net profit (extraordinary income left out). Yearly amount by annual net profit, rounded up to the cent [O · S59]:

| Annual net profit | CIC indépendant 2026 |
|---|---|
| €13,500 – €15,000 | [(profit − 13,500) × 4/125] / 2 × 7/12 |
| €15,000 – €25,200 | [(profit − 15,000) × 3/850 + 48] / 2 × 7/12 |
| €25,200 – €55,200 | [(profit − 25,200) × 37/2,500 + 84] / 2 × 7/12 |
| €55,200 – €114,000 | €154 |
| €114,000 – €119,100 | [(profit − 114,000) × 4/425 + 528] / 2 × 7/12 |
| €119,100 – €170,100 | €168 |
| €170,100 – €179,000 | [(profit − 170,100) × 3/356 + 576] / 2 × 7/12 |
| above €179,000 | €189.88 |

Example (invented): a profit of €40,000 gives [(14,800 × 37/2,500) + 84] / 2 × 7/12 = [219.04 + 84] / 2 × 7/12 ≈ €88.39. [I · our arithmetic]

CIC salarié 2026 (same bill): a monthly credit for June – December 2026 per employee, once for all salaries, with compulsory affiliation; paid by the employer through payroll when the tax card carries the CIS entry, otherwise through the assessment; it cannot be combined with the CIC indépendant or the pensioners' CIC for the same person. Monthly amount by monthly gross salary, rounded up to the cent [O · S59]:

| Monthly gross salary | CIC salarié 2026 per month |
|---|---|
| €1,125 – €1,250 | [(gross − 1,125) × 4/125] / 2 |
| €1,250 – €2,100 | [(gross − 1,250) × 3/850 + 4] / 2 |
| €2,100 – €4,600 | [(gross − 2,100) × 37/2,500 + 7] / 2 |
| €4,600 – €9,500 | €22 |
| €9,500 – €9,925 | [(gross − 9,500) × 4/425 + 44] / 2 |
| €9,925 – €14,175 | €24 |
| €14,175 – €14,916 | [(gross − 14,175) × 3/356 + 48] / 2 |
| above €14,916 | €27.13 |

A pensioners' CIC 2026 follows the same pattern (art. 154septdecies in the bill). In a couple where one spouse is self-employed and the other employed, each gets their own: the CIC indépendant through the return, the CIC salarié through payroll. [O · S59; I for the couple]

## Deadlines for tax year 2026

| What | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Form 100 (and Forms 110/152) | 31 December 2027 | [I · S46 rule: 31 December of the following year] |
| Income-tax advances 2026 | 10 March / 10 June / 10 September / 10 December 2026 | [C · S2] |

The 2026 forms are not published yet (watch list W-08).

The 2025 preparation-boundary rows on donations, category flats, CIP, RELIBI, non-household support and rental cost choice continue under the 2026 LIR and standing guidance, except dated 2025 property-sale/VEFA acquisition windows. Pension CO2 is €216 (phase-out factor 0.54%), not €192; qualifying rental energy renovation is 10%, not 6%. The 2025 PDF map is not a 2026 map. [C · S2; S26; S30; S90–S96]

## Final payment rounding

A positive final balance after withholding and advances is rounded down to the whole euro. [C · S2 art.154(2), read 2026-11-04] Preserve refund cents; article 154(7) does not state a corresponding floor. [I · S2] See scale-and-calculation.md for the order.
