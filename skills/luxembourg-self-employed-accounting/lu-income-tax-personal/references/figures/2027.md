# Personal income tax – known changes from 2027

Nothing below is law yet. Never use this file for a 2025 or 2026 answer.

| Proposal | From | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| New scale (art. 118): one more index tranche (about × 1.025); 0% band up to €13,565; 42% above €240,803 | Tax year 2027 | Bill 8800 – the new art. 118 text is in this bill; bill 8775 only announces it | [O · S60, S59] – watch list W-03, W-04 |
| CO2 credit for the self-employed (and for employees and pensioners) €240, phase-out factor 0.006 | Tax year 2027 | Bill 8800 | [O · S60] – W-03 |
| Tax credit for employees on the social minimum wage (CISSM): €179 a month for a monthly gross of €1,800–€3,000, tapering to nil at €3,600, from 1 January to 30 June 2027 (the bill's report announces €200 from 1 July 2027) – voted at first reading 28 Oct 2026 | 1 Jan 2027 | Bill 8775 | [O · S59] – W-04 |
| Single tax class from 2028 – scales and amounts: [2028.md](2028.md) – voted at first reading 28 Oct 2026 | 2028 | Bill 8676 (deposit text; the July 2026 amendment not read) | [O · S33] – W-05 |
| Speculation period for movable assets (shares, crypto-assets…) 6 → 12 months (art. 99bis), also for carried interest and for substantial shareholdings (art. 100) | Tax year 2027 | Bill 8800, art. 4, 2° and 3° | [O · S60] – W-03 |
| Business losses carried forward without time limit (art. 114) | 2027 | Bill 8800 (lu-corporate-tax figures/2027.md) | [O · lu-corporate-tax S31] – W-03 |
