# Personal income tax – the single tax class from 2028 (bill 8676)

_Figures in this file: 2028–2052. Bill 8676, voted at first reading on 28 October 2026 (37 for, 11 against, 12 abstentions); waiver of the second vote requested; not law yet – watch list W-05. Never use this file for a 2025–2027 answer._

From tax year 2028 the classes 1, 1a and 2 are replaced by one scale for everyone (« tarif U »), with individual taxation. Couples already taxed jointly keep a transitional scale (« tarif T ») until the end of tax year 2052. [O · S33 – committee report of 9 Oct 2026, the text voted]

## Who is taxed how

| Situation in 2028 | Scale | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Single, divorced, single parent, and anyone married or partnered **from** 1 January 2028 | Tarif U, each person on their own income | [O · S33 art. 3, 118] |
| Married before 1 January 2028 (or partnership declared before then) and taxed jointly | Tarif T on the couple's joint income, until tax year 2052; on joint request they can choose tarif U instead | [O · S33 art. 3bis, 3ter, 118bis] |
| Widowed within the 5 previous years, if taxed jointly the year before the death | Tarif T (unless they had opted for U) | [O · S33 art. 118bis(2)(b)] |

Tarif T is today's class-2 scale written for the whole joint income (each band edge doubled), so a couple that stays on it pays as today, before the other changes below. [I · our comparison of S33 art. 118bis with today's art. 118]
