## Final payment rounding

A positive final balance after withholding and advances is rounded down to the whole euro. [C · S82 art.154(2), read 2026-11-04] Preserve refund cents; article 154(7) does not state a corresponding floor. [I · S82] See scale-and-calculation.md for the order. This does not extend the salary household calculator beyond its 2025 scope.
