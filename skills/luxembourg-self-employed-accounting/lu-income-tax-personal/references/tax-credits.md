# Tax credits

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

Tax credits reduce tax; abatements reduce income. Refundability depends on the credit: CII and CIM can be refundable, while the child rebate and child bonus are limited to tax due. [C · S2, S82]

## Tax credit for the self-employed (CII) and its CO2 credit

- Everyone with commercial, agricultural or liberal-profession profit taxable in Luxembourg gets a **tax credit for the self-employed** (*crédit d'impôt pour indépendants*, CII) and a **CO2 tax credit for the self-employed** (CI-CO2 indépendant) (art. 152ter LIR). [C · S2]
- Condition: the person is personally affiliated, for that profit, as a compulsory insured person to Luxembourg (or treaty-covered foreign) social security. [C · S2]
- **CII amount** (same in 2025 and 2026), by net profit: €300 + (profit − €936) × 2.9% between €936 and €11,265; **€600** between €11,266 and €40,000; €600 − (profit − €40,000) × 1.5% between €40,001 and €79,999. [C · S43]
- **Below €936 of profit – including a loss – the CII is €300** and there is no CO2 credit; **from €80,000** of profit neither is granted. [C · S2; S57]
- CO2 credit: €192 (2025) or €216 (2026) for profit between €936 and €40,000, then phased out up to €79,999 – figures. [C · S44, S43] The 2026 phase-out: €216 − (profit − €40,000) × 0.54% between €40,001 and €79,999 [C · S2]; for 2025 the same shape from €192, i.e. × 0.48% [I · S44]. Yearly amounts are rounded up to the cent. [C · S2]
- They are given **only through the assessment** (Form 100): deducted from the tax and paid out by the ACD if the tax is lower. A self-employed person who does not file does not get them. [C · S2; S57]
- **There is no box to tick**: the 2025 Form 100 has a box only for the single-parent credit (228) and a request for the overtime credit, none for the CII. The ACD computes the CII and CO2 credit itself in the assessment from what the return shows. [C · S1, S43] What the person must make sure of: the business profit is declared – page 5 (commercial or craft, from Form 110) or page 6 (liberal profession, balance sheet or Form 152) – and the compulsory affiliation shows: the CCSS certificate attached and the contributions on page 16, boxes 1601–1604. [I · S1, S43] The credit then appears on the tax bulletin. [I] In MyGuichet the special-expenses part asks whether the person was personally affiliated as a compulsorily insured person for a declared self-employed activity – answer Yes when it applies (lu-annual-return, forms/2025/myguichet.md). [I]
- They count once for all self-employed income and **cannot be combined** with the employee credits (CIS, CI-CO2 salarié) or the pensioner credits (CIP, CI-CO2 pensionné) for the same months. [C · S57]
- Business profit plus a salary or pension in the same year: the credits are computed **month by month**, each month giving one credit, ranked by the highest average monthly income. [C · S57]
- Spouses taxed jointly: the credits are computed **separately for each** spouse or partner. [C · S57] Two CIIs in joint taxation require the spouse to be affiliated as a helping spouse (« conjoint-aidant ») – a condition written in the law (art. 152ter(1)). [C · S2] Two spouses who each run their own business, each with an own affiliation: probably each gets an own credit – the helping-spouse sentence reads as the condition for a second credit on one business – but no ACD text names the case; say it is not confirmed (open point PIT-O-05). [I · S2, S57]

## Employees and pensioners (side income)

- Employees use the year-specific CIS/CO2 schedule and paid-versus-retained reconciliation in [employee-tax-credits.md](employee-tax-credits.md). One-category assessment has a multiple-card/no-card gate; mixed salary/business months retain the circular's category ranking. Do not subtract a full credit again when already paid correctly. CISSM has separate hours and payment-route rules. [C · S82; S57; S124; S125]
- Pensioners use the separate CIP schedule, including its lower minimum and low-income band; do not copy CIS. See donations-pensions-and-investments.md and figures/2025.md, including per-month reconciliation against credits already paid. [C · S82, S101, S57]

## Single-parent tax credit (CIM)

Use [single-parent-and-child-relief.md](single-parent-and-child-relief.md) for CIM eligibility, income/support formulas, cohabiting parents and new partners, payroll reconciliation, and worked boundary cases. Class 1a alone is not sufficient; married people and class-2 transition cases are excluded. The 2025 request is on Form 100 page 2, but partial payroll payment still requires reconciliation under art. 154ter(5). [C · S80–S82]

## Bill for 2026 (not law yet)

The *CIC indépendant* 2026 (bill 8775, voted at first reading on 28 Oct 2026, not law yet – the scale is in figures/2026.md): a credit for 1 June – 31 December 2026, not granted below €13,500 of annual net profit, €189.88 a year above €179,000, given only through the assessment – watch list W-04. [O · S59]
