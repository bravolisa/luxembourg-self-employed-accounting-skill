# Employer interest support and deductible interest

Figures in this file: 2025 – the year files win if they differ. Existing home-interest figures govern the separate deduction ceiling.

**Classify what happened first.** A cash reimbursement of bank interest (bonification d'intérêts) is valued at nominal value. A loan granted directly/indirectly by the employer below the statutory benchmark produces an interest saving (économie d'intérêts). For the latter use the difference between1.5% and the actual rate on the relevant outstanding balances and periods; no positive interest-saving benefit where the actual rate is at least the benchmark. The prescribed benefit is not calculated using the commercial mortgage rate or all interest paid. Ordinary banking calculation applies; the opening/closing balance average is available only with competent tax-office authorisation. Valuation timing is account closing, no later than year end. [C · S173, S174; I for nonnegative branch]

**Establish the exemption separately.** Housing benefit cap€3000 yearly, raised to€6000 for joint spouses and the statutory single-parent employee specified by article 119(2)(b). Qualifying loan purpose is the personal home, first building land, or construction/renovation intended for personal housing. Secondary residence excluded. Other-loan benefit cap€500, doubled to€1000 in those same qualifying cases. These are employee-benefit exemptions, not the home-interest deduction cap and not amounts multiplied by each child. Joint PACS partners receive the doubled limit in the annual assessment; payroll cannot apply that PACS increase. Collect all employers' and both adults' benefit/exemption details before using a household ceiling. Do not duplicate a yearly ceiling per payslip or employer. [C · S172, S121, S129; B for aggregation control] <!-- separate fact -->

**Reconcile deduction with the exact amount already paid and exempted.** Let B be total employer benefit, E its properly allowed exemption, T=B−E its taxable portion. Let S be separately identified state interest assistance. These formulas presume eligibility, no overlap and amounts for the same year's qualifying debt; obtain an explanation rather than inventing negative interest.

- **Cash support reimburses bank interest:** if I is bank interest BEFORE the employer reimbursement and state support, the deduction base is I−E−S. Equivalent expression: I−B−S+T. Subtract only the exempt employer portion. Do not add T once more to I−E. If the bank figure is already net of B/S, reconstruct its basis or add only the applicable taxable portion; never deduct the support twice. [I · S82, S173, S22]
- **Reduced-interest employer loan:** if P is the actual interest paid by the employee, deduction base is P+T−S. The exempt deemed saving is not a cash cost and must not be subtracted from P. ACD's illustration confirms paid1500 plus taxable saving1000 gives2500 before the housing cap. [C · S22; I for mixed-state algebra]
- **State support:** its reduction of costs is distinct from the employer salary-benefit exemption. Establish whether bank totals already incorporate it. [C · S22; B for intake]
- Apply the relevant2025 home-interest eligibility/availability/household cap only AFTER this reconciliation. Consumer-loan interest goes through its own special-expense cap shared with qualifying insurance; a benefit exemption does not itself prove deductible use of the loan. Do not send rental/business borrowing into the own-home route. [I/B; existing guides]

**Documents and salary placement.** Obtain the annual employer certificate and benefit breakdown; bank/employer loan agreement, use of proceeds, actual interest and balance/period statement; cash subsidy evidence; each adult's other employer benefits; state assistance; joint/PACS/single-parent evidence. Monthly payslip text is an extraction clue only. Preserve gross benefit, certified exemption, taxable remainder and whether each is already in gross; never add an already included benefit twice. Annual ECSP2025 has PPPH in remuneration and exemptions; The [2025 special-employment map](forms/2025/special-employment.md) identifies the paper exemption destination after eligibility is reviewed. Amounts ultimately affect salary gross/exemption and the correct interest category. Confirm certificate and form semantics before rendering XML. Exempt earnings columns are not a substitute for the specific salary-exemption row. [C · S112, S169, S1, S75; I for mapping]

**Tax calculation.** The taxable benefit T belongs in salary income, while the reconciled interest may reduce taxable income subject to its own cap. Consequently the cash received is not automatically the taxable-income change and there is no separate fixed “interest subsidy tax rate.” Recompute the supported full household assessment only after these two sides reconcile. Contributions and credits must come from evidenced, separately reviewed rules; no automatic assumption that income-tax exemption means exemption from every social contribution. [I/B]

**Tool boundary.** Existing automatic salary exemptions accept only HSUP/SJFE. Employer-interest support remains a reviewed manual branch; never disguise PPPH as ordinary salary or put E into an arbitrary subsidies field merely to obtain output. Add an explicit known-employer-support stop to the home/consumer household routes. The guide supports manual reconciliation, not a complete automated return or liveXML claim. [B]

## Remaining boundaries

A change of family status during the year does not prorate the limit: the doubling follows joint taxation, which holds for the whole tax year (marriage during the year – joint for that year; spouses not separated on 1 January – joint for that year; partners only if the partnership lasted all year). [C · S172, S82; I for the conclusion] Individual assessment, the order across several loans or employers, contributions and paid-credit reconciliation need further evidence before automatic preparation. PIT-O-36 records the unresolved scope; RET-O-01 retains live import semantics. A known employer benefit must not be hidden in ordinary gross or a generic subsidy field. [O/B]
