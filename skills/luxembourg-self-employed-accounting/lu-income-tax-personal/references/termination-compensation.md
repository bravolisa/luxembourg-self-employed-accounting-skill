# Payments when employment ends

Figures in this file: 2025 – the year files win if they differ.

A final settlement is not one tax category. Obtain the final payslip, annual certificate, termination letter, employment/collective agreement and settlement or court order where relevant. Record each component, payment date, legal basis, gross, certified exemption and withholding. A payslip label alone does not establish exemption. [B]

## Classify before calculating

| Component | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Final ordinary wages, worked/released notice salary, unused-leave compensation | Start with salary treatment; do not reuse the severance exemption merely because paid on exit. Notice compensation is not the same as statutory severance. Any distinct exemption or extraordinary-income claim needs its own legal basis. [I · S82 art95; S177] |
| Statutory or collectively agreed severance, article 115(9)(a) | Exempt category; the ordinary twelve-month cap below addresses(b)–(d), not(a). Check pension entitlement and the age/income restriction before treating(a) as fully exempt. [I · S82 art115(9)] |
| Court-set or transaction-set compensation for unfair termination; voluntary indemnity on employee resignation or mutual termination,115(9)(b)–(d) | Aggregate these amounts for their common statutory ceiling. Require evidence that the payment fits the relevant category. [C · S82] |
| Qualifying collective-redundancy, social-plan or approved employment-preservation-plan indemnity,115(10) | Separate statutory route requiring the Comité de Conjoncture opinion; do not assume a signed private settlement supplies this approval. [C · S82; S176] |

For 2025 first payments, the January 1 unskilled monthly minimum is **EUR2637.79**. Therefore the ordinary twelve-times ceiling is **EUR31653.48**, and four-times restricted ceiling **EUR10551.16**. These products are arithmetic from the statutory multipliers. The later2025 wage-index increase does not replace the January 1 value. A payment divided over years keeps the minimum from January 1 of its first-payment year; do not grant a fresh full ceiling to each instalment. [I · S82 art115(9),(10); S175]

Article 115(9)(a)–(d) exemptions are unavailable to people entitled to old-age or early old-age pension. For someone aged60 or more at exit, without either pension entitlement, who normally had taxable salary above 150% of the article 153(1)(1) general assessment limit, the maximum is four monthly minima. Preserve this statutory income test; age60 alone does not trigger the restriction. Do not mechanically export these article 115(9) exclusions to the distinct115(10) route. [C · S82]

Where a package mixes115(9) and 115(10), require review of common/overlapping compensation and ceiling allocation. The wording of the two provisions does not justify inventing two cumulative exemptions for the same payment. This guide does not resolve every combination. [O/B]

## Form100 and the final tax

The 2025 annual ECSP format uses **IDPT** for remuneration and **IDEP** for its article 115(9)(a) exemption. **IDCC** covers the specified other115(9)/115(10) categories. They are annual certificate codes; monthly payroll labels can differ. Gross and exempt portions are distinct, never two extra receipts to add to the annual subtotal. [C · S169]

Use [the 2025 special-employment map](forms/2025/special-employment.md) for reviewed paper preparation and the explicit XML boundary. [B]

Ordinary tax is calculated on the resulting annual taxable household income, then reconciled with actual withholding. ACD's prior opinion is optional for the ordinary payroll exemption route; the employer's withholding is an advance, not a final settlement. [C · S176]

A large one-off payment is not automatically extraordinary income. Article 132(1)(2) requires its specific economic-period and involuntary-concentration conditions; compensation replacing other-year income may instead need132(1)(4) analysis. Request under 132(4), identify the legal category and net eligible amount. The extraordinary-income request repeats amounts already included in net income; do not add them again. [C · S82; S1]

For the 132(1) route,131(1)(b) uses four times the tax difference from adding one-quarter of qualifying extraordinary income to ordinary income, subject to the statutory 24% ceiling. The full131 computation also considers its EUR250 threshold, extraordinary-charge allocation, normal-tax comparison and separate surcharge rules. This is not a blanket half-rate rule and is not implemented by the ordinary salary-balance calculator. [C · S82 arts131–132; B for software boundary]

## Cross-border boundary

Determine which country may tax each component before Luxembourg exemptions. For Germany, the 2024 ACD treaty circular uses five-year salary allocation for ordinary mixed-state termination compensation, a separate rule for qualifying collective agreements and a counterfactual workplace rule for released notice. Do not apply the old2012 rule or a blanket last-year ratio. France and Belgium need their own treaty analysis. [C · S137 §§2.3.1–2.3.3; O for unresearched country specifics]

## Invented component examples

1. Aged40, no pension entitlement, eligible statutory severance EUR20000 plus qualifying mutual-termination indemnity EUR40000, first paid2025: statutory portion exempt EUR20000; other indemnity exemption EUR31653.48; taxable excess EUR8346.52. Final wages/notice/holiday remain separately classified. This is not the final household tax. [I · S82/S175]
2. Same eligible115(9)(d) indemnity EUR40000 split EUR20000 in 2025 and EUR20000 in 2026: keep one first-payment-year ceiling EUR31653.48; no second annual EUR31653.48 exemption. Any allocation/recognition conflict needs review. [I · S82/S175]
3. Employee aged62 with early old-age pension entitlement receives EUR20000 statutory severance: do not claim the ordinary 115(9) exemption. Entitlement matters, not whether pension was actually drawn. [I · S82]
4. EUR40000 described only as “settlement”: do not classify all as exempt or claim half-rate tax. Ask for the component/legal basis and pension/age facts. An unsigned social-plan draft does not establish115(10) approval. [B]

## Supported scope

This guide supports evidenced classification and reviewed manual calculation. Automatic salary-detail exemptions currently accept only HSUP/SJFE; termination benefits and extraordinary-income computation remain unsupported. Stop rather than disguise IDPT/IDCC as ordinary salary or omit the taxable excess. Tax exemption does not establish social-contribution exemption. [B/O]
