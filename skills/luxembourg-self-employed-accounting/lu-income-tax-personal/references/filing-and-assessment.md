# Filing, advances, assessment and appeal

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

## Who must file

- Income tax on business profit is assessed after the year, on the basis of the return (*imposition par voie d'assiette* / *Veranlagung*). [C · S45]
- A **self-employed person should file every year**: the profit bears no withholding, and the CII and CO2 credits are only given through the return (tax-credits.md). [I · S45, S57]
- A resident whose income bears no withholding (for example only business profit) must file once taxable income reaches the tax-free minimum of tax class 1 – the 0% band of the scale: **€13,230** for 2025 and 2026 (€12,438 under the 2024 scale). The regulation dispenses only « si … le revenu imposable est inférieur au minimum exonéré de la classe d'impôt 1 » (RGD of 13 March 1970, art. 2, 2°); the class 1 figure applies whatever the person's own tax class, and anyone the ACD individually asks to file must file. [C · S189; S82 art. 118] The ACD page still shows €12,438 and is dated 28/10/2024 – not updated to the 2025 scale; the regulation and the scale decide. [C · S45 for the page; I for "not updated"] Below the threshold, file anyway: the CII is only credited or refunded through the assessment. [C · S82 art. 152ter(3)]
- A resident with salary or pension who also has **more than €600** of other net income – business profit counts – must file whatever the income level. [C · S38]

## Deadline and how to file

- The return for year N (Form 100, plus Form 110 for the municipal business tax) is due by **31 December of N+1**, since tax year 2022: 2025 returns by 31 December 2026; forms online from 7 April 2026. [C · S38, S39]
- **No extension**: the law sets the deadline « au plus tard pour le 31 décembre de l'année qui suit » (§ 167(3) AO, law of 23 December 2022, which also repealed § 167(4)), and the ACD's FAQ says it cannot be extended: file without a missing document and send it later. [C · S66, S38] The ACD's « Délais » page (2024) still mentions a motivated request – read it as meant for deadlines set in ACD letters. [I · S46]
- Since tax year 2024 every income category, business profit included, can be declared in the **MyGuichet electronic assistant**; exclusions include a change of country of residence during the year and a request for individual taxation. [C · S56]
- Unsigned returns do not count; both spouses sign. [C · S1]

## Advances (avances / Vorauszahlungen)

- Taxpayers assessed by return pay quarterly income-tax advances on **10 March, 10 June, 10 September and 10 December** of the tax year (art. 135 LIR). [C · S2]
- Each is in principle a quarter of the last assessed tax (after withholding); it can be based on the expected tax of the current year, and changed on a motivated request or by the ACD. [C · S2] Worth asking when profit drops sharply.
- A new business without a previous assessment: the tax office sets advances on an estimate; they must be paid on time even while the assessment is pending. [C · S38]
- Municipal business tax advances (commercial and craft only): 10 February, 10 May, 10 August, 10 November – lu-income-tax-business.
- **Advances are not declared**: the 2025 Form 100 has no box for income-tax advances paid, and the MyGuichet import schema has no field for them; the ACD deducts the advances it received when it assesses the tax. Keep the payment proofs to check the bulletin. [C · S1; lu-income-tax-business S62]

## The assessment notice (bulletin) and paying the balance

- After the assessment, the tax is reduced by child rebates, withholding taxes, the CIM and the advances paid; any **balance is due within one month** of notification of the bulletin. [C · S2]
- **Late payment interest**: the law sets 1% a month and lets a regulation lower it; in practice the ACD charges **0.6% a month** on unpaid tax and advances, and on a late-filing surcharge. [C · S2; S54]
- **Late filing**: a surcharge of up to 10% of the tax (§ 168 AO), and a penalty payment after a final reminder. [C · S54; S2]

## Contesting the bulletin

- File a **réclamation** with the ACD director within **3 months** of notification – also against a bulletin that fixes advances. [C · S47]
- By post or fax, not by phone or e-mail; in Luxembourgish, French or German. [C · S38]
- It does **not** suspend payment; a stay (*sursis*) must be asked separately. [C · S38]
- For a simple mistake (e.g. a forgotten document) a correction request (*demande en redressement*) can be sent by post within 3 months of the first bulletin. [C · S38]
