# Preparing a cross-border household — 2025

This is an intake and review workflow [B]. Legal rules live in the named domain references.
It does not add non-resident calculation or new JSON/XML support.

## Decide before filling

1. Build each adult's residence and civil-status timeline; identify any move, foreign PACS, court permission, divorce or death. Read personal-tax `family-changes.md` and `cross-border-income.md`. Domestic residence, treaty residence and assimilation are distinct.
2. List every annual income source for both adults, including earlier employers and foreign-only spouse income. Classify each source under the treaty before assigning its tax country.
3. For employment, keep the threshold-day counter separate from actual salary allocation.
   For independent activity, establish premises/base and attributable profit. Registration alone is insufficient. Record unresolved category or employer facts.
4. Determine social insurance for each person using social-security `cross-border-work.md`.
   Keep residence institution/A1 decisions separate from tax-day calculations and healthcare rights.
5. Review the non-resident filing and assimilation route. Preserve separate actual taxable, exempt/rate and eligibility-adjustment amounts. Reconstruct foreign category income using the governing country's rules rather than copying a foreign net total indiscriminately.
6. Review each foreign expense under personal-tax `cross-border-deductions.md`: approved care, targeted reimbursement versus tax relief, insurance contract/roles, qualifying savings products, and ownership/borrowing/availability for the foreign main home.
7. Use personal-tax `forms/2025/cross-border-and-family.md` for manual PDF requests and taxpayer/spouse exempt columns. Percentage allocation fields are not currency fields.
8. Retain the foreign-return reporting handoff. Tax paid abroad is not a universal Luxembourg credit. Record any relief-method uncertainty and authority needed to settle it.

## Document checklist

| Area | Collect and reconcile |
|---|---|
| Residence/status | Residence periods, available homes, relevant residence certificates, marriage/PACS records, common-home history, judicial decisions, full-year partnership proof |
| Employment | All annual salary certificates and periods, contracts, actual dated work locations, partial days, training/travel/on-call facts, pay allocation and withholding |
| Business | Legal category, real premises/base and use, activity by location, accounts/profit schedule, functions/assets/risks, attribution evidence |
| Partner and other income | Foreign salaries, pensions, business/rental/investment income, returns/assessments, contribution certificates; no omission simply because no LU employer exists |
| Social insurance | Existing A1/exception decisions, all concurrent activities, expected work pattern, changes, institution correspondence and corrected contributions |
| Children/care | Birth/study/disability and fiscal-household facts, CAE and foreign benefits, provider approval, service/payment months, itemised care, subsidies/refunds, foreign tax relief |
| Insurance/savings | Full policy and annual certificate, authorisation, policyholder/insured/payer, covered risks and split, Luxembourg product-compliance certificate where needed |
| Home | Deed/shares, borrower/interest payer, loan purpose, interest distinct from principal, subsidies, availability/use timeline, refinancing trail |

Prior-year returns are history and leads, never proof that the same rule, amount or family status applies in 2025. Preserve originals locally; use synthetic facts for shareable test cases.

## Calculation and verification boundary

The existing household tools do not thereby gain complete non-resident/assimilation, foreign-rate, individual/reallocated or family-transition support. Do not mark a foreign household resident to bypass a validation stop. Record manual working, unresolved facts and the reviewer's identity.
Offline schema validity or a filled PDF is not live MyGuichet acceptance or tax correctness.
RET-O-01 remains open; no live upload or filing was performed by this research release.

Prepared component cases in the research archive are internally reviewed expectations, not professionally verified completed returns. Future document fixtures must state their upstream version and verification status before being used as acceptance answers.
