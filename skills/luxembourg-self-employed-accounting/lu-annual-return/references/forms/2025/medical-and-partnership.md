# Joint partners and actual medical costs — 2025

Figures in this file: 2025. Personal-tax year files own figures; source identifiers refer to that skill. Sources reopened 2026-11-05.

## Registered partners with common children

Article 3bis allows qualifying registered partners to request joint annual assessment; full-year partnership and common home are cumulative. Spouse rules broadly apply, with statutory exceptions including payroll assessment. Foreign registrations may qualify legally. The automation below is a narrower Luxembourg-registration subset. [C · lu-income-tax-personal S82 art.3bis; lu-income-tax-personal S129; lu-income-tax-personal S130; lu-income-tax-personal S127]

Supported: full-year resident 2025 couple, partnership declared before the year, common home throughout the year, both never married, no status change or competing individual assessment/election. Set both `status: PA`, `underlying_civil_status: CE`, `joint_taxation_option: true`. `partnership` requires `registered_in_luxembourg`, `both_request_joint`, `same_home_full_year`, `no_status_change`, `no_competing_election_or_assessment` all true, `declaration_date`, `common_home_since`, `evidence`, `certificate_evidence`, boolean `certificate_already_presented`. Evidence must also resolve any incompatible earlier assessment/election. Common children require `common_child_of_partners: true` plus existing assignment/moderation evidence and both-parent custody. Other children's entitlements are not denied; they need a broader attribution model. Existing equal joint ownership/borrowing and childcare rules remain unchanged. [B]

Paper: request312, declaration313, attached314 or previously presented315; civil status301 célibataire. Do not mark married option317 for a partnership request. XML: both PA with declaration date, EL_PARTENAIRE, ASS_DEM_3BIS and actual ASS_DEM_3BIS_DOC. Both adults must sign and attach evidence. Annual class 2 does not rewrite historical payroll classes. Paid CIM is not silently discarded; the current route stops if that needs a class 2 recovery. No live import/read-back certified. [C · lu-income-tax-personal S1; lu-income-tax-personal S75; lu-income-tax-personal S130; I for PA date field use; B for scope]

## Actual article 127 medical costs

ACD requires unavoidable, extraordinary, materially burdensome costs and a claim; an unreimbursed invoice is not enough. Deduct all refunds once and exclude costs already deducted elsewhere. [C · lu-income-tax-personal S82 art.127; lu-income-tax-personal S9; lu-income-tax-personal S90]

Supported `medical_expenses[]`: one to twenty records with unique `invoice_id`, adult `paid_by` (`taxpayer`/`spouse`), `patient`, `kind: medical/dental/prescribed_medicine`, `article127_eligible: true`, `eligibility_evidence`, `provider`, `payment_evidence`, 2025 `payment_date`, `currency: EUR`, `gross`, `refunds`, description at most fifty characters. Require `no_other_deduction`, `no_forfait_or_childcare_overlap`, `refunds_final_and_complete` all true. One payer per invoice; split or unallocated invoices require review. [B]

Patient can be the payer; the other declared adult in a supported joint assessment; or `patient: child` with an integer `child_index` referring to `children[]`. For a different patient, require `patient_identity_evidence` linking the provider invoice, `household_bears_cost: true`, and `no_other_household_claim: true`. The child must be in this tax household, born after 31 December 2007 and no later than the payment date, with `extraordinary_child_treatment: true` and `treatment_evidence`. This minor-throughout-2025 boundary is software scope, not a statutory age limit. Ordinary child upkeep is already covered by moderation; additional extraordinary unavoidable treatment can qualify. A routine receipt alone does not establish it. Cross-paid joint-adult inclusion is an interpretation of collective assessment and article 127, not a published generic ACD cross-payment example. [C · lu-income-tax-personal S82 art.127(4a); lu-income-tax-personal S90 §19; I for joint-adult application; B for boundaries]

All records, including existing self-paid adults, now require `pending_refunds: false`, `expected_refunds: 0`, `refund_review_evidence`, and `refund_items[]` (empty only for no refunds). Each item has unique `refund_id` within the invoice, `recipient` identifying a declared adult, `date`, `amount`, and insurer-statement `evidence`. Items must sum exactly to `refunds` and every receipt date must be in 2025. Preserve the insurer identity and invoice allocation in the evidence; pooled/unallocated reimbursements need review. A later-year item stops even if total refunds is zero or a finality flag says true. Review statutory, complementary, mutual/employer/accident and supplementary CNS entitlements before asserting finality. A first CNS statement alone is insufficient. Keep invoices and individual statements in the private case record. [B]

Payment-year attribution follows article 108/circular 127 §4. CNS supplementary participation instead follows the service year and can be requested to the end of N+2; this makes later reimbursements realistic but does not decide their income-tax correction year. Pending, expected or later-year refunds remain PIT-O-34. Never discard one or automatically move it into either tax year. [C · lu-income-tax-personal S82 art.108; lu-income-tax-personal S90 §4; lu-income-tax-personal S158 art.154bis; O for tax correction]

`medical_review` requires `income_complete`, `no_foreign_or_exempt_progression`, `all_actual_article127_costs_included`, `evidence`, and the supported `tax_class`. Route is full-year resident, ordinary class 1, reviewed class 1a/CIM or supported joint class 2. Established positive Luxembourg moderation is required for children; no more than five are automated. Childless older/widowed/transition class 1a, outside-household/adult-child expenses, pending or cross-year reimbursements, foreign progression, other actual extraordinary costs and disability flats remain review cases. Explicit foreign salary or foreign-only/zero-differential child moderation contradicting the review stops. These are software limits, not legal denials. [B]

Let R be taxable income before abatements (paper 2038), N the aggregate eligible gross payments less all refunds, p the year-file normal-burden percentage. Deduction is max(0,N−R×p/100), once per household. Subtract it after 2038 alongside distinct qualifying childcare/extra-professional abatements; never reduce the donation net-income base or gross CIS/CO2 base. CIM is recomputed on adjusted income with its existing guards. [C · lu-income-tax-personal S82 art.127; lu-income-tax-personal S9; lu-income-tax-personal S73; I for adapter ordering]

Exactly40000 uses the ACD worked example's band;60000 uses the band ending there. At20000,30000,50000 the overlapping published endpoints require review if they change relief. Fractional-cent final relief stops pending rounding confirmation; an outcome invariant across the endpoint alternatives can proceed. More-than-five-child table extension is not assumed. PIT-O-33/34 remain open. [C · lu-income-tax-personal S9 for example/table; O/B for guards]

Paper1702/1703 shows net actual costs by payer BEFORE the normal burden, with 1701 requested and the generated annex preserving gross payments/refunds and the calculation. This net paper mapping is an explicitly labelled interpretation of the form with the net-cost rule. XML independently preserves gross `CFM_MNT_FRA_MAL` and refunds `CFM_MNT_TOT_REM` only for coincident adult payer/patient. Every child or cross-paid adult case explicitly stops XML; no medical row is silently omitted. The PDF and annex remain available, with payer and patient separately identified. The official tutorial shows a child selector H0020 for 2024, but does not prove cross-payer semantics for 2025 (PIT-O-34). [C · lu-income-tax-personal S156 for historical UI; B/O for guard] Never put net costs in the gross field then subtract refunds again. All supported output validates offline; live semantic read-back remains RET-O-01. [C · lu-income-tax-personal S1; lu-income-tax-personal S75; I for paper mapping]

The annex is a required companion, not an embedded signature or supporting receipt. Regenerate both outputs after any change. Generic `--what-if` changes to income or special expenses stop with medical costs because the burden must be recomputed from the changed household. [B]
