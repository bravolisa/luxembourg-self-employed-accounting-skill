# Documents and data to collect (tax year 2025)

## Business side

- [ ] List of sales invoices for the year (and receipts by date, for the simplified method)
- [ ] Purchase invoices and receipts, with proof of payment
- [ ] Bank statements of the business account (and private account where business payments went through it)
- [ ] VAT returns filed for the year (or the simplified return) and the recapitulative statements
- [ ] Asset register: purchase invoices of equipment, dates, prices; previous Form 113
- [ ] Commercial and craft traders: the year-end inventory of everything the business owns and owes – required every year at any turnover (art. 15 Code de commerce) [C · lu-accounting S11]
- [ ] Car: total km and business km (logbook or equivalent), odometer at 1 Jan and 31 Dec, car cost invoices
- [ ] Home office: floor plan or m², photos, lease or deed, charges statement, calculation note
- [ ] Loan contracts and interest certificates (business loans)
- [ ] Insurance policies and premiums (professional)
- [ ] Previous year's tax assessment and the current advance notice (bulletin des avances)

## Personal side (rest of Form 100 – lu-income-tax-personal)

- [ ] CCSS contribution certificate for the year
- [ ] Mutualité (MDE) reimbursements received
- [ ] Commuting: home and work communes (distance units)
- [ ] Household: marriage, partnership (certificate), separation, divorce or death during the year; children's dates of birth and whether family allowance was paid; maintenance paid or received
- [ ] Other income: salary and pension certificates; bank statements of interest and dividends (withholding certificates); rental income and costs (Form 190/210), loan statements for a rented flat
- [ ] Own home: loan interest certificate, date the home became available
- [ ] Special expenses: insurance premiums, consumer-loan interest, home-savings and private-pension certificates, supplementary pension certificate (self-employed), donation receipts
- [ ] Abatements: childcare and household-help invoices, medical costs not reimbursed, disability certificate

## 2025 family evidence

- Children: current-year allowance/study/volunteer-aid evidence, birth dates, tax-household assignment, continuous full-time education/continued allowance where relevant; support and prior CIM if requested.
- Childcare: provider approval, certificate, actual monthly payments, reimbursements, payer and child;
  distinguish regular monthly payments from advances/arrears. Earlier returns do not prove these facts.
- Own home: bank certificate separating interest, principal, subsidies and insurance; year-end debt;
  acquisition/completion, availability/occupation dates, ownership/borrowing rights and private use.

Input and unsupported subcases: [form100-family.md](forms/2025/form100-family.md). Keep document references in the private case record; synthetic regression fixtures contain no client documents. [B]

## Single-premium mortgage death insurance (2025)

Collect the contract and insurer's proof of payment in 2025, subscription date, subscriber and insured-person identities/birth dates, split for two insured lives, loan purpose and disbursement breakdown, child entitlement and chosen allocation. Keep ordinary premiums and property interest separate. For the same property, obtain prior assessments and calculation details for 2020–2024 showing the increased deduction actually used; an old premium amount alone is insufficient.
Record an evidenced absence of earlier use, not an assumed zero. See lu-income-tax-personal `mortgage-death-insurance.md` for the rules and worked examples. This is the preparation checklist;
mandatory uploads remain the ACD list in attachments.md. [B; basis: lu-income-tax-personal S74, S77]

## Salary certificates and domestic employment

Collect all annual employer/CCSS/direct-payer certificates, card and correction history, covered dates, taxable/exempt gross components, actual deductible and non-deductible contributions, withholding and each credit already paid. Preserve unknown versus explicit zero and replacement/ duplicate links. Employer-advanced sickness already in gross is not another CNS payment. [B;
basis: lu-income-tax-personal S112; lu-income-tax-personal S124]

For domestic flat pay, obtain regime evidence, all affected contracts/annual extracts and the explicit regularisation request covering the jointly taxed married spouses. Monthly net records are corroboration. Capture workplace schedules and frequencies for commuting and actual/theoretical hours and benefits for CISSM. Follow the personal-tax salary guides for eligibility and unresolved partner/credit cases. [C · lu-income-tax-personal S108; lu-income-tax-personal S125; B for intake]

## Additional automated branches, 2025

For consumer interest obtain the contract, borrower/payer, annual net interest, subsidy/refund breakdown and year-end balance. For donations obtain current receipts, recipient eligibility for the payment year, payer, reversal/counter-benefit checks and assessed prior carry balances (the latter still require manual use). For housing PU obtain subscription/payment dates, own-life roles, property/loan purpose, full paid premium, child allocation and five years of actual increased deductions allowed. For CIM obtain civil-status history, fiscal child assignment, positive Luxembourg moderation, other-parent residence, regular support/direct costs and all paid-CIM certificates with explicit withholding basis. Unknown is not zero. The machine input contract and refusal boundaries are in [form100-deductions.md](forms/2025/form100-deductions.md). [B]

## Special-payment review before salary or interest calculation

Read the annual certificate and relevant monthly/final payslip together. Search the source document for PPPH, interest support/savings, *bonification/économie d'intérêts*, IDPT/IDEP/IDCC, severance, *indemnité de départ/licenciement*, and extraordinary-payment codes. These are review clues, not proof of eligibility or universal payslip labels. An ordinary salary subtotal or absent code does not prove that the benefit is absent. Confirm with the household when a component is unclear. [B]

Keep a private review record with source document/page, original label/code, covered year and person, amount or explicit unread/unknown state, and inclusion in gross. When normalising a pay row to `kind: S`, preserve a known original code as `source_code`; never discard it to get output. [B]

| Finding | Evidence needed before the manual calculation |
|---|---|
| Interest support | Cash versus employer loan; loan purpose; gross inclusion; benefit and exempt split; bank actual interest and net/gross basis; state assistance; household/PACS allocation |
| Termination payment | Component breakdown, final payslip/annual certificate, legal basis and settlement or decision, payment dates, pension entitlement and relevant age/income facts |
| Domestic CCSS work | Worker-side annual extracts for every contract, regime and election, gross/social/dependency/tax/net components, paid credits and their covered periods |

Use the corresponding personal-tax guides for the legal calculation. Preserve an unclear finding until the missing facts are obtained; never set it to zero, remove the component or infer an exemption.
The employer's certificate, bank certificate and prior return may contain overlapping information, not three additional deductions. [B]

For structured household or salary input, optionally attach `special_payment_review` with `status` (`clear`, `present`, `unclear`) and a non-empty `evidence` reference. `clear` means a documented check found no special-payment issue needing this manual branch; it does not mean an unsupported payment has been approved for automation. Present/unclear, unknown status or missing evidence stops. Known codes and existing benefit/regime markers still override a clear assertion. Legacy inputs without this field remain accepted; this optional record does not claim that an OCR detector exists. [B]
