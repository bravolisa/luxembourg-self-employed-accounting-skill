# Phones, laptops, software and other equipment – from purchase to return

_Figures in this file: 2023–2027. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

The question users ask most often about small purchases: "I bought a phone (laptop, tablet, monitor, chair, software) for the business – what can I deduct, and do I get the investment tax credit?" This file walks through it; the rules themselves live in depreciation-and-investment-credit.md, mixed-expenses.md and lu-vat.

## Step by step

| Step | Question | Answer | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is it used for the business? | Only the business part counts. Running costs of a mixed-use item (phone plan, internet) are split by documented business use; an item used only privately is not a business cost | [C · S1 for the principle; I for the split] |
| 2 | What price? | Without VAT when the VAT is deductible (normal regime); with VAT under the franchise, where VAT cannot be deducted | [C · S7] |
| 3 | €870 or less? | Can be written off in full in the year of purchase (Form 152 line 42, Form 112 line 46, with a detail annex) – or depreciated like a larger item | [C · S7; S4, S5] |
| 4 | More than €870 | Depreciated over its useful life (Form 113, which feeds Form 110 or 152). There is no official table of useful lives: the business sets it by the practice of its sector and keeps to it; first year pro rata (or the ACD's half-year / full-year shortcut) | [C · S1, S3; depreciation-and-investment-credit.md] |
| 5 | Investment tax credit? | Only commercial or craft businesses (art. 14), not liberal professions; new item; depreciated over **at least 3 years**; used in the Luxembourg business. 12% of the price (2024–2026), on Form 800, deducted from the tax of the year, carried forward up to 10 years | [C · S3 art. 152bis §1, §7, §8; S31, S33] |
| 6 | VAT (normal regime) | Deduct the input VAT; for an item kept in the business but also used privately, the private use is taxed as a supply (lu-vat normal-regime.md); for mixed services, deduct the business share | [C/I · lu-vat normal-regime.md] |
| 7 | Later | Selling it: the price is business income and what is left to depreciate is an expense of that year. Taking it private: a withdrawal at its value at that time. Contributing it to your own company for shares: an exchange, taxed like a sale at its real value (contribution-to-a-company.md) | [I · S3 arts 43, 22(5)] |

Not confirmed by the ACD, our reading: the credit is on the full price of a business asset that is also used privately (IT-O-25), and an item written off at once under the €870 rule keeps the credit when its usual useful life is three years or more (IT-O-26). [I – depreciation-and-investment-credit.md]

## By kind of item

| Item | Usual treatment | Investment credit (commercial or craft only) |
|---|---|---|
| Phone, tablet, laptop, monitor, printer, camera | €870 or less: write off at once, or depreciate; above: depreciate | Yes if new and depreciated over 3 years or more – a 2-year period loses it; under €870 written off at once: probably yes when its usual useful life is 3 years or more – our reading, not confirmed (IT-O-26) |
| Office furniture (desk, chair, shelves) used only for work | Same | Same rule |
| Software bought (a licence you own) | Depreciated like other assets, or written off at once if €870 or less | Separate 12% software credit (Form 800 part II, D), capped at 10% of the year's tax, no carry-forward; not if bought from a linked company [C · S3 art. 152bis §7(1) n° 5, §7(4), §8] |
| Software subscription, cloud, apps paid monthly or yearly | A running expense of the year (office costs) – not an asset | No [I] |
| Phone plan, internet | A running expense, business share only (Form 112 line 17 / Form 152 telephone line) | No |
| A car | Its own rules: car-and-travel.md | Excluded, except fully electric or hydrogen passenger cars (first €50,000) [C · S3 art. 152bis §7(2) n° 4] |
| Repairs, cases, chargers, small accessories | Expense of the year | No |
| Used (second-hand) item | Depreciated over its remaining useful life | No – except in the first 3 years after the business is first set up [C · S3 art. 152bis §7a] |

## What changes by year

| Rule | 2023 | 2024 – 2026 | 2027 and later |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate write-off | €870 | €870 | No change announced |
| General investment credit | 8% up to €150,000 of investment, 2% above (figures/history-2023-2024.md) | 12%, no tranche; 14% for assets approved for special depreciation | Proposals: figures/2027.md |
| Software credit | – | 12%, capped at 10% of the tax | figures/2027.md |

An item keeps the rules of the year it was bought; its credit is claimed for that year. [I]

## Typical cases (invented figures)

- **Phone €1,200 without VAT, commercial sole trader, normal VAT regime**: depreciated over 3 years (€400 a year on Form 113); investment credit 12% = €144 (Form 800); VAT deducted; if it is also used privately, the credit stays on the full price – our reading, IT-O-25 – with the private share of depreciation taken out on Form 113 and the private use taxed for VAT.
- **Laptop €800 without VAT, the same trader**: either written off at once (€800 expense this year; the €96 credit probably still applies because a laptop's usual life is three years – our reading, not confirmed, IT-O-26), or depreciated over 3 years (€266.67 a year) with a credit of €96 and no doubt. Which pays more depends on the trader's tax rate this year and next.
- **Laptop €800 with VAT, translator under the franchise (liberal profession)**: €800 written off at once; no VAT to deduct; no investment credit.
- **Design software subscription €40 a month**: €480 a year as an office expense; no credit.
