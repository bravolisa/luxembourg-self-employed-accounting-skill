# Income categories and forms

## Two categories for self-employed people

| | Commercial or craft profit | Liberal-profession profit |
|---|---|---|
| Law | Art. 14 LIR (bénéfice commercial) | Art. 10 n° 3 and art. 91 LIR |
| Typical activities | Trade, crafts, HORECA, many IT and consulting activities run under a commercial permit | Doctors, lawyers, architects, translators, teachers, many consultants – check the permit and the activity |
| Main annex | Form 110 (+ 112 on route B, + 113) | Form 100 page 6, plus Form 152 on the simplified method (+ 113) |
| Municipal business tax (ICC) | Yes – section V of Form 110 | No – liberal professions are outside ICC (municipal-business-tax.md) |
| Status | [C · S20, S24] | ICC exclusion [C · S49] |

Some activities can be either category (IT consulting is the classic case); ask rather than guess.

- **The business permit does not decide the tax category.** The permit law sorts activities for access to the profession; LIR arts 14 and 91 sort profit for tax, and the court applies only the LIR criteria (54190C). Holding the general commercial permit and declaring liberal-profession profit is therefore consistent: the activity is the same, only the two laws label it differently. Mismatches exist both ways – journalists need no permit but are liberal for tax; a pharmacist is commercial for tax. [C · S63 for the court's approach; I for the independence – no sentence of the law states it] The ACD may still read the commercial permit as a hint: declare the same category every year and keep proof of the personal, intellectual work (degree, development contracts).

- **The courts' test** (Cour administrative, 22 July 2026, n° 54190C): first the conditions of a business under art. 14; then the activity must be listed in art. 91 or be « semblable » to one listed, judged by higher education, essentially personal work, intellectual character and a relationship of trust. Decisive: « des prestations personnelles qui constituent l'objet même de l'activité ». A pharmacist was held commercial. [C · S63] For IT work, independent expert design or advice points to art. 91; selling products or reselling others' services points to art. 14 (IT-O-21). [I · S63] The ACD's own Form 100 schema lists « Ingénieur » (INGE) and « Expert-conseils » (EXPE) among the liberal-profession codes. [C · S62]
- **Insurance agents**: commercial profit in full – « La même activité est également, en totalité, de nature commerciale » (circular L.I.R. n° 14/1); the 2025 e-filing schema also lists « Agent d'assurance » as a commercial activity. [C · S64, S62] Brokers are not named; they also earn intermediation commissions, so treat them the same. [I] Commissions can be declared on Form 100 page 4, C « Bénéfice divers » (boxes 509–516), with a flat-rate deduction instead of actual costs (circular L.I.R. n° 18/1; amounts: figures/2025.md); higher actual costs need records and receipts. [C · S65; lu-income-tax-personal S1]

## Which annex for which method

| Situation | Expense detail |
|---|---|
| Commercial, regular accounts (Form 110 route A, lines 11–18) | None on the form – attach balance sheet and P&L |
| Commercial, accrual without regular accounts (Form 110 route B, lines 19–47) | **Form 112** at line 41 |
| Commercial, simplified receipts-and-expenses (Form 110 route C, lines 48–58) | Free annex, VAT included (line 54) |
| Liberal, balance-sheet basis (Form 100, case 601) | None – attach balance sheet and P&L |
| Liberal, simplified receipts-and-expenses | **Form 152**, carried to Form 100 page 6, cases 609–612 |

[C · S20, S23, S24]. Form 112 = accrual, net of VAT with a VAT column; Form 152 = cash, amounts paid, with a line for VAT paid to the AED. Neither crosses to the other method. [I · S24]

Form 152 belongs only to the simplified receipts-and-expenses method, which is closed to anyone who must keep regular accounts (§ 161 AO) or keeps them voluntarily (« ne doit en outre pas tenir une comptabilité régulière », RGD of 3 December 1969, art. 1; circular L.I.R. n° 18/5, point 2.1.1). A liberal professional with regular accrual accounts reports at case 601 with the balance sheet and P&L. [C · S61, S21]

Balance sheet and P&L: there is no prescribed ACD form; the taxpayer attaches his own presentation. [C · S25] (See lu-accounting.)

## Deadlines and payments (tax year 2025)

- Form 100 with annexes: by 31 December 2026. [C · S24]
- Income-tax advances: 10 March, 10 June, 10 September, 10 December, amounts set by the ACD's advance notice. [C · lu-income-tax-personal filing-and-assessment.md]
- ICC advances (commercial only): 10 February, 10 May, 10 August, 10 November. [C · S49]
