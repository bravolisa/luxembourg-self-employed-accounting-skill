# VAT inside income-tax figures

## Simplified (cash) method

- Receipts and operating expenses are taken **gross, VAT included**; VAT actually paid to the AED during the year is itself an operating expense, except VAT due on private withdrawals and private use. [C · S21 point 3 a)]
- Form 152 line 22: « Taxe sur la valeur ajoutée payée à l'administration de l'Enregistrement déduction faite de la T.V.A. sur prélèvements privés et utilisation privée de biens ». [C · S4]
- Example: €11,700 received including VAT, €2,000 ordinary expenses paid, €1,700 VAT paid to the AED in the same year → profit €8,000. If the VAT is paid next year, it is deducted next year (special timing rules for recurring payments exist; do not invent a number of days). [I · S27]
- Commercial simplified method (Form 110 route C): line 54 « Frais généraux payés (T.V.A. comprise) (suivant détail en annexe) »; line 55 is a separate line for deductible VAT on depreciable capital goods; there is no equivalent of 152 line 22. [C · S24]
- Fixed assets: input VAT that regular books would put on the VAT account is deductible when paid; VAT that would be part of the cost is capitalised and follows the asset (depreciation). Never deduct the same VAT twice. [C · S21 point 3 f)]
- Withdrawals in kind are added to profit **excluding VAT**. [C · S21 point 3 g)]
- So Form 152 amounts are gross, VAT included (both columns say « le cas échéant T.V.A. comprise »), with the VAT paid to the AED on line 22. [C · S21 point 3 a), S4]
- A VAT refund received from the AED is a receipt **of the year it is received**, mirroring VAT paid to the AED as an expense of the year it is paid. Example: the credit on the 2024 VAT return, refunded in 2025, is a 2025 receipt; the 2025 credit refunded in 2026 is a 2026 receipt. On the accrual method the refund stays on the balance sheet. [I]
- So receipts on this method usually differ from the VAT turnover: refunds received, Mutualité reimbursements (lu-social-security certificate-and-tax.md), invoices of another year paid in this one. A receipts breakdown makes the difference visible – lu-annual-return attachments.md. [I]

## Accrual method

- Profit is net of VAT; VAT sits on balance-sheet accounts and never touches profit. [I · S24]
- Form 112 columns: % business, amount excluding VAT, VAT invoiced. [C · S24]

## Franchise users

No VAT deduction, so VAT is part of each cost: gross = net, and 152 line 22 is nil (unless they paid reverse-charge VAT). [I · S24]

## VAT timing vs income-tax timing

VAT timing (when VAT is due) and income-tax profit timing are separate legal questions: normal-regime VAT on invoices and the cash method for income tax can coexist, but eligibility for the cash method must still be established. [I · S27]
