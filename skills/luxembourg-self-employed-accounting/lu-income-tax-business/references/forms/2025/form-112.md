# Form 112 – 2025 (operating expenses, accrual without regular accounts)

Used with Form 110 route B; total goes to Form 110 line 41. Columns: total (VAT included where applicable), % business, amount excluding VAT, VAT invoiced. [C · S5, S24]

| Line | Content |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Gross wages, salaries, occasional wages |
| 4 | Employer social contributions |
| 5 | Accident insurance |
| 6 | Commissions (footnotes 3, 4) |
| 7 | Travel and representation of staff |
| 9 | Accounting fees (footnote 3) |
| 12 | Travel and representation of the owner |
| 13–17 | Electricity, gas, water, heating, telephone |
| 18–21 | Rent of: 19 buildings with VAT, 20 buildings without VAT, 21 machines and installations |
| 22 | Fire insurance |
| 23 | Property tax |
| 26 | Municipal business tax |
| 27 | Cabaret tax and other taxes |
| 28–30 | Interest long-term, short-term, other financial costs |
| 31 | Insurance of goods and commercial installations |
| 32 | Civil and professional liability insurance |
| 33 | Office costs |
| 34 | Cleaning and maintenance of shop/workshop |
| 35 | Professional body contributions |
| 36 | Professional newspapers and periodicals |
| 37–39 | Work performed by third parties: 38 subcontracting, 39 other (footnotes 3, 4) |
| 40 | Postage and transport |
| 41 | Work clothing |
| 42 | Advertising |
| 43 | Packaging |
| 44 | Repair and maintenance of installations and machines |
| 45 | Other repairs (footnote 4) |
| 46 | New small items up to €870 (footnote 4) |
| 47–57 | Car costs: 48 fuel, 49 repairs, 50 tyres, 51 leasing/rental, 52 garage rent, 53 taxes and insurance, 54 total; km driven in 2025 and business share (km, %); 57 deductible car costs |
| 58–61 | Flat commuting deduction: units × €99 (first 4 not counted, max €2,574), place of work, home locality |
| 62 | Total general expenses → Form 110 line 41 |

Lines 8, 10, 11, 24, 25 are blank write-in rows. Footnote 4: « Prière de fournir le détail en annexe ». [C · S5]
