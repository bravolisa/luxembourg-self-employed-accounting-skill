# Form 113 (depreciation) and Form 800 (investment tax credit)

## Form 113

Depreciation schedule; its total feeds Form 110 line 42 (route B) or line 56 (route C), or Form 152 line 58. [C · S4, S20] Front page – acquisitions of the year: number, date, description, price with VAT (4), VAT (5), price without VAT (6), subsidies received (7); column 6 minus column 7 is the value to depreciate, carried to the back. Back page – depreciation table 2025: description, acquisition date, value to depreciate (3), rate (4), book value at the start of the year (5), acquisitions of the year (6), sold during the year, without VAT (7), depreciation of the year (8), book value at the end of the year; then the private and business shares. [C · S53] For a franchise user the non-deductible VAT is part of the cost: it stays in the price without VAT (column 6), so the value to depreciate is the real cost. [I] Form 113a is a different form: special depreciation under art. 32bis. [C · S53]

## Form 800

Claim form for the investment tax credit (art. 152bis LIR). The 2024 version (https://impotsdirects.public.lu/dam-assets/fr/formulaires/entreprises_annexes/2024/800f-2024.pdf) and the 2025 version (S33) were read; use the form of the tax year being filed – a 2024 form is not the one for a 2025 return. Rules: depreciation-and-investment-credit.md. [I · S22, S27]
