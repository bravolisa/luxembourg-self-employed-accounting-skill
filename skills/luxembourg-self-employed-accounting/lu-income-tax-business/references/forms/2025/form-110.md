# Form 110 – commercial profit (structure)

Three routes in section II; line numbers checked against the 2025 form (legal library F110-2025). [C · S20]

| Route | Lines | Heading | Who | Expense detail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 11–18 | Profit by comparison of net invested assets (art. 18(1)) | With regular accounts | Attach balance sheet and P&L |
| B | 19–47 | « Etablissement du bénéfice commercial » | Without regular accounts | Form 112 at line 41; depreciation Form 113 at line 42 |
| C | 48–58 | Comparison of receipts and operating expenses (art. 18(3), RGD 3.12.1969) | Without regular accounts, simplified method | Free annex: line 54 general expenses paid incl. VAT; line 55 VAT on depreciable capital goods; line 50 private withdrawals in kind added to receipts |

Route B converts cash to accrual: receipts ± client receivables (lines 20–24), work in progress (27–29), goods and stock (31–35), general expenses paid ± accruals (37–41), depreciation (42). [C · S24]

Section III « Données diverses » (heading 59–60, data lines 61–68): cash, banks, receivables, work in progress, stock, debts at both year-ends – for routes B and C, not A. [C · S20] Other lines worth knowing: 25 private withdrawals of goods (without VAT), 45 sundry gains and 46 sundry losses (e.g. bank interest, selling an asset), 73 the profit carried into section V from line 18, 47 or 58. [C · S20]

Section V (lines 72–95): declaration for municipal business tax – profit and the § 8 additions (72–80), deductions incl. donations (« libéralités », lines 83–85, with the carry-forward from the two previous years and a detail annex), the split between communes on Form 115 (line 90), loss carry-forwards (91–93), remaining profit (94) and compulsory personal social contributions (95). [C · S20]

Line 95 (compulsory personal contributions paid in the year, including dependency contributions) reduces the municipal business tax base. [C · S20]
