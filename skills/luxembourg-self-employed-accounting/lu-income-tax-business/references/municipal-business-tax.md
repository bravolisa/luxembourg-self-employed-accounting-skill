# Municipal business tax (ICC)

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

| Point | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Commercial and craft profit; declared in Form 110 section V (lines 72–95, forms/2025/form-110.md) | [C · S20] |
| Deductible for income tax | For a sole trader the municipal business tax is an operating expense (Form 112 line 26); the LIR's list of non-deductible taxes for individuals does not name it | [C · S5; lu-income-tax-personal S2 art. 12 n° 3 a)] |
| Donations | Deducted from the ICC base on Form 110 lines 83–85 « pour autant qu'elles ont augmenté le bénéfice », with a carry-forward from the two previous years. Private donations paid outside the business never entered the profit, so they do not reduce the ICC base – the deduction applies « lorsque ceux-ci ont été pris en considération lors de sa détermination » (Mémento 30.1.2) | [C · S20, S8] |
| Liberal professions | Outside ICC: « L'ICC ne concerne pas : … les professions libérales »; the ACD limits it to « uniquement les entreprises commerciales, industrielles, minières et artisanales » | [C · S49, S35] |
| Allowance | €40,000 for taxpayers not subject to corporate income tax (sole traders, partnerships); companies: lu-corporate-tax, municipal-business-tax-companies.md. Automatic, no request needed | [C · S35, S49] |
| Rate | 3% of the adjusted profit × the communal multiplier; Luxembourg City 225% → 6.75% | [C · S35, S36] |
| Zero ICC | A zero result does not remove the duty to declare | [I · S27] |
| Base | The commercial profit, with adjustments. All compulsory personal social-security contributions the owner paid in the year to a Luxembourg institution – **including** the dependency contribution – reduce the profit for ICC: Form 110 line 95, « Cotisations personnelles légalement obligatoires versées en 2025 à un établissement de sécurité sociale luxembourgeois (suivant certificat) », just after the operating profit. For income tax the same contributions (without dependency) are special expenses instead (lu-social-security) | [C · S20 line 95, S35, S47] |
| Line 95 – how | Only contributions actually paid from 1 January to 31 December count, also those a spouse pays as a co-owner or helper; they come off after the operating profit is set, so they never create or increase a loss to carry forward (circular I.C.C. n° 31). No rule splits them when the person also has liberal-profession income (IT-O-23) | [C · S66] |
| Losses carried forward | Only losses recorded in regular accounts in the year they arose (§ 9bis(2) GewStG): not losses from the receipts-and-expenses method | [C · S66] |
| Advances | 10 February, 10 May, 10 August, 10 November, set by the ACD; the balance after the assessment | [C · S49] |
| Multipliers 2026 | Published in Mémorial A n° 553 (11.12.2025); not opened | [C · S36] |

From companies onward (SARL, SA) this becomes its own skill (lu-municipal-business-tax).
