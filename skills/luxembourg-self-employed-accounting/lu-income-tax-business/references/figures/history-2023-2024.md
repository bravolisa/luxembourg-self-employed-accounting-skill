# Income tax history – 2023 and 2024

**Purpose:** only for assets bought in 2023 or 2024 (their depreciation continues today), investment credits claimed in those years, and corrections of those years. For current questions use figures/2025.md or 2026.md.

| Item | 2023 | 2024 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate write-off (art. 34) | ≤ €870 per asset | ≤ €870 per asset | [C · S29] |
| Declining-balance depreciation (art. 32) | Max 3× straight-line rate and 30% | Same (text unchanged 2023 → 2026) | [C · S3, S28] |
| Commuting deduction | €99 per unit; first 4 not counted; max €2,574 | Same | [C · S29] |
| Income tax scale | Not indexed; 0% below €11,265; a one-off "CIC indépendant" credit instead (law of 5 July 2023) | +4 index tranches; 0% below €12,438 | [C · S39] |

## Investment tax credit (art. 152bis) – the 2024 reform matters for old assets

| | Until tax year 2023 | From tax year 2024 (law of 22 Dec 2023, Mém. A n° 836) |
|---|---|---|
| General ("global") credit | 8% on the first €150,000 of investment, 2% above | 12%, no €150,000 tranche |
| Assets approved for special depreciation (art. 32bis) | 9% / 4% | 14% |
| Complementary credit | 13% | Abolished |
| Digital / ecological-energy transition credit | – | 18% (incl. operating costs); 6% on tangible assets |

[C · S31, S32]

- An asset bought in 2023 keeps the credit rules of 2023; the credit is claimed for the year of the investment. [I]
- Depreciation of an asset bought in 2023 or 2024 continues on its original cost and plan. Its cost includes VAT only where the VAT was not deductible at the time (see lu-vat figures/history-2023-2024.md for the rates and the €35,000 franchise of those years). [C · S7 for VAT in cost; I for the rest]
