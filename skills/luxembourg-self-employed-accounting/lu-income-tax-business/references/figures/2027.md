# Income tax – what is already known for 2027 and later

Never use this file for a 2025 or 2026 answer. Everything below is a **bill** [O] unless marked otherwise. Status of each bill: lu-tax-core watch list.

## 2027 – budget bill 8800 (watch list W-03) and bill 8775 (W-04)

| Change | Status |
|---|---|
| Tax scale adjusted by one index tranche (about × 1.025) from tax year 2027 – the new art. 118 text is in bill 8800; bill 8775 only announces it; the new bands: lu-income-tax-personal, figures/2027.md | [O · lu-income-tax-personal S60, lu-income-tax-personal S59] |
| Digital / ecological-energy transition investment credit: 18% → 21% (bill art. 4, 11°) | [O · S41] |
| Same credit on tangible depreciable assets: 6% → 9% (bill art. 4, 11°); the bill's item leaves the 12% general credit alone | [O · S41] |
| CO2 tax credit (self-employed included): higher amount and new phase-out from 2027 – lu-income-tax-personal, figures/2027.md | [O · lu-income-tax-personal S60] |
| Business losses carried forward **without time limit**: the bill rewrites art. 114 LIR, which governs the business losses of individuals – sole traders included – and of companies; the 17-year limit also goes for ICC | [O · lu-corporate-tax S31] |
| Corporate income tax: new rates and thresholds for companies only, not sole traders – lu-corporate-tax, figures/2027.md | [O · lu-corporate-tax S31] |
| Commuting deduction, €870 rule, ICC allowance: no change found | [I · S41, S45] |

The bill's articles on the scale, CO2 credits, corporate tax and losses were read on 2026-10-25 (lu-income-tax-personal, lu-corporate-tax); the investment-credit change was read in the bill on 2026-10-26 (art. 4, 11°). All of it stays a bill until voted. [O]

## 2028 – single tax class (bill 8676, watch list W-05)

From 1 January 2028: one tax class with a single scale, and a transitional scale for jointly taxed couples until 2052 – both scales and the other amounts: lu-income-tax-personal figures/2028.md. Voted at first reading 28 Oct 2026. [O · S43]
