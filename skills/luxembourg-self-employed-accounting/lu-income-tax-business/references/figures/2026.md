# Figures – tax year 2026

The 2026 tax forms (Form 100, 110, 112, 152, 800) are published in 2027. Until then, 2026 values rest on the law in force and on the 2026 budget law (law of 19 December 2025), which changed only the revaluation coefficients and the CO2 tax credit. [C · S34]

| Item | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate write-off of small assets (art. 34) | ≤ €870 per asset – no change found | [I · S30, S34] |
| Declining-balance depreciation (art. 32) | Max 3× the straight-line rate and 30% | [C · S28] |
| Commuting deduction | €99 per unit; first 4 units not counted; max €2,574 – no change found | [I · S34] |
| Investment tax credit | General 12% (14% for art. 32bis assets); transition 18% / 6% – no change found | [I · S34] |
| ICC | 3% × communal multiplier; allowance €40,000 for sole traders and partnerships | [C · S35, page updated 19.12.2025] |
| ICC multiplier, Luxembourg City | 225% (ACD table "from 2025"); the 2026 communal rates were published in Mémorial A n° 553 of 11.12.2025, not opened | [I · S36] |
| Income tax scale | Not indexed for 2026; 0% band below €13,230 (class 1) | [I · S34 (no indexation in the 2026 budget law); S45 secondary for the band] |
| CO2 tax credit (art. 152ter), self-employed included | Amount and phase-out: lu-income-tax-personal, figures/2026.md | [C · S34] |
| Bookkeeping duty | Turnover > €100,000, unchanged: liberal professions from the year after notification (§161 AO); commercial and craft traders from the year after a year above it (Code de commerce) | [C · S48] |
| Cessation/sale gain allowance | €10,000 (€25,000 if the gain includes a building gain); taxed at half the global rate – arts 130–132 read in the 2026 text on 20 November 2026 | [C · S3, S12] |

## Bill for 2026 (voted on 28 October 2026, not law yet)

**CIC indépendant 2026** (bill 8775): a tax credit for self-employed people for 1 June – 31 December 2026, requiring compulsory social-security affiliation; profit limits and amount: lu-income-tax-personal, figures/2026.md. Status: watch list W-04. [O · S42]

## Insurance agents' flat-rate expenses (circular L.I.R. n° 18/1, unchanged since 2003)

| Commissions | Expenses allowed without receipts | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Up to €2,000 | 30% | [C · S65] |
| €2,000 – €6,000 | €600 + 25% of the part above €2,000 | [C · S65] |
| €6,000 – €15,000 | €1,600 + 20% of the part above €6,000 | [C · S65] |
| Above €15,000 | €3,400 | [C · S65] |

The same scale applies as an optional flat rate for all operating expenses of a **side activity** carried on independently (circular L.I.R. n° 94/3 of 24 June 2008, unchanged) – rule in mixed-expenses.md. [C · S68]
