# Overview and who qualifies

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

## The legal frame – and the 2026 reform

- The general SME aids (investment, consultancy, fairs, miscellaneous investments) rest on the **law of 9 August 2018** on an aid regime for SMEs, which implements the EU General Block Exemption Regulation (GBER, Reg. 651/2014). [C · S1]
- The law was **reformed by the law of 22 July 2026**, applying from **15 September 2026**. Before that date the 2018 text (as amended in 2020) applied. [I · S7 – date from the Legilux consolidated version] Always ask **when the project started or the application was filed**: the rules differ (figures/2026.md has both sets).
- Which rules apply to a file sent before 15 September 2026 and decided after: open point SUB-O-05.
- The start-up aid (primo-création) has its own law (start-up-aid.md); SME Packages and Fit 4 are separate programmes (sme-packages.md).

## Who qualifies

- The business must hold a **business permit** (autorisation d'établissement) under the law of 2 September 2011 and not be on the exclusion list. [C · S6]
- A **sole trader** counts as an enterprise. [C · S5]
- Size (EU criteria, measured **together with partner and linked enterprises**): micro = fewer than 10 people and turnover or balance-sheet total up to €2 million; small = fewer than 50 and up to €10 million. [C · S5, S6; S1] From 15 September 2026 some aids are also open to large firms. [C · S6]
- A company **in difficulty** (e.g. more than half of its subscribed capital lost through accumulated losses, or in insolvency proceedings) gets no SME aid, with narrow exceptions. [C · S1]

## Who is excluded

- From 15 September 2026 the exclusion list is an **annex to the law**: shopping centres, cable operators, solariums, film production, show organisers, tobacco and e-cigarette shops, fuel retail, real-estate developers, agents and managers, most liberal professions, and buildings usable as housing. [C · S6]
- **Liberal professions** are excluded except **architects and engineers**; accounting firms, « experts comptables et de conseil » and fiduciaries are on the same exclusion line. [C · S6] Until 14 September 2026 the list was in a grand-ducal regulation of 2018; architects and engineers could then receive at most €200,000. [C · S9]
- **Consultants**: in everyday speech many freelancers call themselves "liberal professions", but a general consultant holds a **commercial** permit (old « conseil » permits stay valid as commercial permits). Whether « experts comptables et de conseil » also excludes such a consultant is **not settled** – open point SUB-O-01. [I · S30]
- The legal liberal professions under the permit law include architects (also interior and landscape), accountants and chartered accountants, industrial-property advisers, surveyors, construction engineers and urban planners. [C · S33]

## How to apply

- Online, on **MyGuichet.lu** (professional space), by the business or a mandated person, with LuxTrust or a Luxembourg eID [C · S2]; a professional space can also be opened with an eIDAS ID from another European country. [C · S29]
- Authority: Ministry of the Economy, Direction générale Aides d'État et financement aux entreprises. [C · S1]
- **Apply before you start** (incentive effect, *effet incitatif* / *Anreizeffekt*): a complete application must reach the ministry **before work on the project starts**. [C · S1] "Start of work" is the start of construction or the **first legally binding order** of equipment, whichever comes first – a signed quote counts; buying land, permits and feasibility studies do not. [C · S5]
- Exceptions: replacement investments can be claimed afterwards (sme-aid-regime.md); the start-up aid is not project-based.
- **From 15 September 2026**: decision within 3 months of the complete file, extendable once by 3 months; **no decision in time counts as approval**. [C · S6]
