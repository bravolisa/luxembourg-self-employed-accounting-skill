# The SME aid regime: investment, consultancy, fairs, miscellaneous investments

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

Eligibility, exclusions and "apply before you start": overview-and-eligibility.md. Amounts below are for files under the rules **from 15 September 2026** unless marked otherwise; the 2025 rules are in figures/2025.md.

## Investment aid (aide à l'investissement)

- For **creating, extending, diversifying or fundamentally changing** an establishment; **replacing** existing machines is not eligible here. [C · S2]
- **20%** of eligible costs for micro and small enterprises, **10%** for medium ones. [C · S5]
- **Minimum aid** per project: €1,000 (micro and small), €5,000 (medium) from 15 September 2026; €1,000 for every aid before. [C · S2; S5] Example: 20% of a €3,000 machine = €600 – below the minimum, no aid.
- From 15 September 2026: machines and equipment for **exclusively professional use**, unit value at least **€750**, not for rental; **vehicles excluded**. [C · S6]
- Paid **after completion** (instalments possible), on a payment claim on MyGuichet with invoices and proof of payment; from 15 September 2026 within **12 months** after the project end date in the decision (+12 months on a reasoned request). [C · S2; S6]

## Consultancy aid (aide aux services de conseil)

- Up to **50%** of the cost of external consultants. [C · S3]
- The advice must be **occasional and project-based** – not permanent or periodic, not normal operating costs such as tax, financial or legal advice or advertising; training is excluded. [C · S3]

## Fair aid

Up to **50%** of renting, setting up and running a stand; from 15 September 2026 **national fairs only** (in Luxembourg). [C · S4]

## Miscellaneous investments (aide aux investissements divers)

From 15 September 2026 this is **de minimis aid** (de-minimis-and-cumulation.md), open to SMEs and for some categories to large firms. [C · S6]

| Category (from 15 September 2026) | Micro / small | Medium | Large | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Modernisation or **replacement** of equipment | 20% | 10% | 10% | [C · S6] |
| Food hygiene; new technologies incl. digital tools improving processes and customer relations | 30% | 20% | 20% | [C · S6] |
| Environment and climate (certified energy saving ≥ 20%, renewables, water, waste); only above €25,000 of investment, aid at most €50,000 per project; no fossil-fuel assets, photovoltaics or charging points | 50% | 40% | 30% | [C · S6] |
| First investment of a newly created company (primo-création sense) | 30% | – | – | [C · S6] |

- **Replacement investments can be claimed afterwards**: at most two requests per calendar year, each for an investment of at most **€100,000**, filed up to **one year after paying** the expense. [C · S6]
- Before 15 September 2026: 20% (small) / 10% (medium) of tangible investments; requests made afterwards were capped at €20,000 / €10,000 of **aid**. [C · S8 – archived official page]
