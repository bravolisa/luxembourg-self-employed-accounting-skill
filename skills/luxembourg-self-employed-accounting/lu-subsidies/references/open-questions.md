# Open points – lu-subsidies

Answer an open point by saying it is not settled, giving the working answer with its label, and naming who can settle it.

## SUB-O-01 · Are consultants with a commercial permit excluded?
- Question: Does the exclusion of « les entreprises comptables, les experts comptables et de conseil, et les fiduciaires » (annex point 9; 2018 regulation item 24) cover a management or IT consultant who holds a commercial permit?
- Why it matters: Many BravoLisa users are consultants.
- Working answer: Ambiguous. The 2018 regulation had two separate exclusions (item 24: accounting firms, « experts comptables et de conseil », fiduciaries; item 25: liberal professions except architects and engineers); the 2026 annex merged them, and the Chambre de Commerce reads the new clause as making accounting firms, consultants and fiduciaries eligible. Neither the government nor the Council of State commented. Check with the ministry before relying on aid. [I · S6, S30]
- Who can settle: Ministry of the Economy (DG Aides d'État, info.aide.pme@eco.etat.lu).
- Status: open

## SUB-O-02 · Which de minimis ceiling when combining with the start-up aid
- Question: €200,000 over three fiscal years (Luxembourg 2019 law, referred to by the start-up aid law) or €300,000 over three rolling years (EU Regulation 2023/2831)?
- Why it matters: The skill must not state one figure as the only rule.
- Working answer: Both texts are in force; the EU figure is the outer limit and the Luxembourg law may be stricter for aid granted under it. [I · S17, S16, S10]
- Who can settle: Ministry of the Economy.
- Status: open

## SUB-O-03 · Is the €12,000 start-up aid taxable, and when?
- Question: Is the start-up aid (and other operating grants) taxable business income, and in which year?
- Why it matters: A frequent question from new business owners.
- Working answer: Taxable business income in the year granted or received, depending on the profit method – our reading; no ACD text found. [I · S27]
- Who can settle: ACD.
- Status: open

## SUB-O-04 · Booking a capital grant
- Question: Must a capital grant reduce the depreciation base, or may it be booked as a deferred investment subsidy and released over time?
- Why it matters: Depreciation and timing of profit; BravoLisa's bookings.
- Working answer: Follow Form 113 for tax: deduct it from the cost. [C · S28] The official chart allows booking it as deferred income (162x) released to income (7473) [C · lu-accounting S21]; the profit is the same when the release follows depreciation [I]. Which booking BravoLisa uses is a decision still to make. [O]
- Who can settle: ACD; BravoLisa decision log.
- Status: open

## SUB-O-05 · Files straddling the 15 September 2026 reform
- Question: Which rules apply to an application filed before 15 September 2026 and decided after?
- Why it matters: Aid files open in 2026.
- Working answer: none – no transitional article found in the law of 22 July 2026; say it is not settled. [O]
- Who can settle: Ministry of the Economy.
- Status: open

## SUB-O-07 · Start-up aid: "six months" counted to which date?
- Question: Is the six-month age of the business permit counted to the application date or the decision date?
- Why it matters: Late applicants.
- Working answer: The application date. [I · S10]
- Who can settle: Ministry of the Economy.
- Status: open

## SUB-O-09 · SME Packages for a sole trader without employees
- Question: Are sole traders without staff accepted for SME Packages in practice?
- Why it matters: Most BravoLisa users have no employees.
- Working answer: Yes on paper – the official pages set no minimum number of employees; check with the House of Entrepreneurship. [I · S11]
- Who can settle: House of Entrepreneurship (Chamber of Commerce).
- Status: open
