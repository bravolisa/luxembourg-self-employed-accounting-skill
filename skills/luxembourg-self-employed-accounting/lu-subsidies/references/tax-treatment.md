# Tax treatment of subsidies

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

## Capital grants (for an asset)

- On the depreciation form (Form 113, 2025) **subsidies received are deducted from the acquisition price** to get the depreciation base: an aided machine is depreciated on its price minus the grant. [C · S28]
- For the **investment tax credit** (Form 800) the price, cost and operating expenses are reduced by subsidies granted. [C · S25]
- For buildings the ACD likewise reduces the acquisition price by the subsidy. [C · S26]

### Worked example (invented figures)

A €25,000 machine with a €5,000 grant: depreciation base €20,000; investment tax credit computed on €20,000; keep the machine 5 years or the aid may be reclaimed (de-minimis-and-cumulation.md). [I · S28, S25, S1]

## Operating grants (start-up aid, INFPC, other cash aid)

- Business profit is the change in net invested assets over the year; a grant received increases net assets, so it falls into profit unless the law exempts it. [I · S27 art. 18]
- The list of exemptions in art. 115 LIR contains **no item for state business aid**, so SME aids and the €12,000 start-up grant are **taxable business income** in our reading. No ACD text on the start-up aid was found – open point SUB-O-03. [I · S27]
- The INFPC co-financing is expressly taxable. [C · S12]
- In the accounts, the official chart has accounts for capital investment subsidies (162x) and for releasing them to income (7473), so a grant can be shown as deferred income released over the asset's life; for tax, Form 113 deducts it from the cost. [C · lu-accounting S21; S28] Which booking BravoLisa uses: open point SUB-O-04.
