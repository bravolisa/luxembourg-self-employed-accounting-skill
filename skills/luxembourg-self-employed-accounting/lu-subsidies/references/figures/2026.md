# Subsidy figures – 2026

The SME aid law changed on **15 September 2026** (law of 22 July 2026). Use the column of the date the application was filed; for a file sent before and decided after, see open point SUB-O-05. [I · S7; C · S6]

| Item | Until 14 September 2026 | From 15 September 2026 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who | SMEs | Micro, small, medium; large firms for some aids | [C · S5, S6] |
| Exclusion list | Grand-ducal regulation of 2018 | Annex to the law | [C · S9, S6] |
| Investment aid | 20% small / 10% medium | 20% micro and small / 10% medium; equipment ≥ €750 per item, professional use only, no vehicles | [C · S5, S6] |
| Minimum aid | €1,000 for every aid | €1,000 micro and small; €5,000 medium and large | [C · S5, S2] |
| Payment claim | Deadline set in the grant decision (no deadline found in the law) | Within 12 months after the project end date (+12 on request) | [I · S5; C · S6] |
| Decision time | Not set in the law | 3 months (+3); silence = approval | [C · S6] |
| Fair aid | Any fair, 50% | National fairs only, 50% | [C · S4] |
| Miscellaneous investments | 20% / 10%; a posteriori requests capped at €20,000 / €10,000 of aid | De minimis: replacement 20%/10%; hygiene and new technologies 30%/20%; environment and climate 50%/40%/30% (projects above €25,000, aid at most €50,000); first investment of a new company 30%; replacement requests: 2 a year, each for at most €100,000 of investment, up to one year after payment | [C · S8, S6] |
| SME Packages | 70% of €3,000–25,000 excl. VAT | Same; written into the law, one request per programme (two for environmental or digital-tool programmes) per three years | [C · S11, S6] |
| Keeping the aided asset | 5 years from full payment, or the normal depreciation period | 5 years from the grant | [C · S1] |

Unchanged in 2026: the start-up aid (€2,000 × 6, at most €12,000), the de minimis ceilings, the INFPC co-financing. [C · S10, S17, S16, S12]
