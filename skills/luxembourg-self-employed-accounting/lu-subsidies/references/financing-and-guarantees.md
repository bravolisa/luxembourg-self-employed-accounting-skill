# Loans, guarantees and microcredit

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

Short on purpose: these are loans and guarantees, not grants, and the official pages are old. Always say "according to Guichet (last updated …)" and send the person to the provider.

- **SNCI** (Société nationale de Crédit et d'Investissement) is a public-law bank financing Luxembourg businesses in the medium and long term. [C · S21]
- Its **start-up or takeover loan** (prêt de création/reprise) is for newly created or taken-over SMEs with a business permit and needs a business plan with **15% own funds**; established founders and activities without a permit cannot get it. Guichet page last updated 2019. [C · S21]
- **Mutualité des P.M.E.** (set up by the Chamber of Skilled Trades) guarantees investment loans of SMEs. [C · S22]
- **Mutualité de Cautionnement** (Chamber of Commerce, at the House of Entrepreneurship) guarantees bank loans of SMEs lacking collateral – per the chamber's page; no official text found. [I · S23]
- **Microlux**, a non-profit social enterprise, offers business microcredit up to €50,000 – per the provider's page; no official text found. [I · S24]
