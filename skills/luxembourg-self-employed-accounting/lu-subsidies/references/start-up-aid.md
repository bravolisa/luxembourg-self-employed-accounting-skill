# Start-up aid (aide à la primo-création) and start-up programmes

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

## The €12,000 start-up aid

Law of 5 July 2023. For **newly created micro-enterprises in crafts and commerce only**. [C · S10]

| Point | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Amount | A flat grant paid as **€2,000 a month for at most six months**; at most **€12,000** per single undertaking | [C · S10] |
| "Newly created" | The business permit is at most **six months** old – our reading: at the date of the application | [I · S10] |
| Founders: no earlier business | No business permit (own name or as partner) in the last **10 years**, and no such activity abroad | [C · S10] |
| Founders: other companies | At most **25%** of the shares of another company, in Luxembourg or abroad | [C · S10] |
| Founders: no other income | **No pension, salary or other professional income, unemployment benefit, REVIS or other replacement income**, in Luxembourg or abroad – a side job rules the aid out | [C · S10] |
| Founders: no illegal employment | Employers convicted at least twice, in the 4 years before the judgment, for undeclared work or for employing third-country nationals staying illegally are excluded for 3 years from the judgment | [C · S10 art. 2 3°] |
| Management training | The manager has followed a business-management course organised or recognised by the competent chamber (university business degree or master craftsman certificate exempts) | [C · S10] |
| Premises | **Own premises not used as a home**, with a commercial lease or title deed – a home office does not qualify | [C · S10] |
| Excluded activities | 23 activities in the law's annex, e.g. shops over 400 m², transport and transport auxiliaries, training centres, wellness, cinemas, security, forestry, taxi/VTC, tattoo and piercing, tobacco shops, fuel retail, real-estate agents | [C · S10] |
| Decision | Within **one month** of the complete application; no decision in that month counts as acceptance | [C · S10] |
| Repayment | If the sole proprietorship is transferred, or **more than 50%** of the company's shares are sold, within **one year** of the grant decision | [C · S10] |
| Combining | Allowed with other de minimis aid within the ceiling of the 2019 de minimis law | [C · S10] |

- Common errors: the repayment trigger is **more than 50%** within one year, not 25% (25% is the limit on holdings in *other* companies); "once in a lifetime" is not in the law – the test is no permit in the last 10 years. [C · S10]
- ADEM can exempt a jobseeker receiving unemployment benefit, for up to **6 months**, from the duty to accept a suitable job while creating or taking over a business: the request is approved before the end of the 6th month of benefit, with a business plan, a financial plan and the ministry's attestation on the permit conditions; the person takes the majority of a business they held no shares in; profit above 10% of the reference wage is deducted (Labour Code art. L.521-9 §5). It is not the start-up aid: receiving unemployment benefit rules that aid out. [C · S34; S10]
- From 15 September 2026 a newly created company in this sense can also get **30%** (micro and small) on its **first investment** in equipment and intangible assets under the miscellaneous-investments aid (sme-aid-regime.md). [C · S6]
- Is it taxable? Open point SUB-O-03; see tax-treatment.md.

## Start-up programmes (public agency)

- **StartYourBusiness** (ADEM with the House of Entrepreneurship, the Chambre des Métiers and the House of Training, funded by the Ministry of Labour): training and coaching for jobseekers **registered with ADEM** who plan to create or take over a business. Two compulsory awareness workshops, then selection (questionnaire and interview), then one of three options – entrepreneurship training with group coaching, business-skills training, or the touch BASE programme; at most 6 weeks. Contact: StartYourBusiness@adem.etat.lu or the ADEM counsellor. The business-permit form asks whether the project belongs to it. [C · S35, S36; lu-business-permit S4]
- **Fit 4 Start** (Luxinnovation accelerator, funded by the Ministry of the Economy): selected tech start-ups receive up to **€150,000** without giving up equity, in three tranches of €50,000, €80,000 and €20,000, each with conditions. [C · S19]
- **Young innovative enterprises**: an RDI aid for young innovative firms (up to 70% of a three-year financing need, at most €1 million) – only for research-driven start-ups; details on Guichet. [C · S32]
