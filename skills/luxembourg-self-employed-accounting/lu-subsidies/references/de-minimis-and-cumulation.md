# De minimis, cumulation and keeping the aid

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

## De minimis ceiling

- **EU rule**: de minimis aid to one single undertaking (*entreprise unique*: the firm with the firms it controls or is controlled by) from one Member State cannot exceed **€300,000 over three years** (Reg. 2023/2831). [C · S17]
- The three years are **rolling**: for each new aid, the aid granted in the previous three years is added up. Aid counts from the date the **right to it is granted**, not the payment date. [C · S17]
- The regulation applies from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2030; the previous one had a €200,000 ceiling. [C · S17]
- **Luxembourg's own de minimis law of 20 December 2019** (not amended since) still caps its aid at **€200,000 over three fiscal years**, and the start-up aid law refers to that ceiling. [C · S16; S10] Which applies when combining: open point SUB-O-02. Both texts are in force; do not quote only one.
- Miscellaneous-investment aid is de minimis aid from 15 September 2026 (sme-aid-regime.md); the start-up aid also counts. Keep a list of de minimis aid received – applications ask for it. [C · S6, S10; I list]

## Cumulation

- Different state aids for the **same costs** may be combined only up to the highest permitted aid intensity. [C · S1]
- From 15 September 2026 miscellaneous-investment aid may be combined with other de minimis aid beyond an intensity, if all of it is de minimis and within €300,000. [C · S6]

## After the grant

- The aid is lost if aided movable assets are **sold within 5 years** of the grant, if the project is abandoned or not used as agreed, or if information was inaccurate (until 14 September 2026 the law counted 5 years from full payment, or the normal depreciation period). [C · S1]
- Repayment is due with interest within **three months** of the repayment decision; no loss if the minister approved the change in advance for force majeure. [C · S1]

## The EU framework ends in 2026

The GBER, on which the SME aid law relies, applies until **31 December 2026**; a successor was not found adopted (watch list W-11). [O · S18]
