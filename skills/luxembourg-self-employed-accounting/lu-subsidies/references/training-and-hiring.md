# Training and hiring aids

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

## Co-financing of continuing training (INFPC)

- The State pays **15%** of the annual training investment; this aid is **taxable**. [C · S12]
- The rate rises to **35%** (taxable) on the salary costs of participants who have no recognised diploma and less than 10 years' seniority, or are over 45. [C · S12]
- For firms with **1 to 9 employees**, the training investment taken into account is capped at **20% of the payroll**. [C · S12]
- The request must reach INFPC within **5 months of the financial year end** (31 May for a calendar year); no extension. [C · S12]
- Only training of **employees** counts (Luxembourg social security, employment contract), so a sole trader's own training is not co-financed. [I · S12]
- The Guichet page dates from 2019 and still describes paper filing; the INFPC now has an online application. Check the current procedure with the INFPC. [C · S12 date; I]

## Hiring a first employee

- **Older jobseeker** (45+, registered with ADEM): the employer's social-security share is refunded for 2 years (age 45–49) or until pension (50+). [C · S13]
- **Tax credit for hiring a jobseeker** registered with ADEM for 6+ months: 10% of the monthly gross pay for up to 12 months; deducted from the employer's income tax of the year the wages were paid, unused credit carried forward 10 years; claimed with the ADEM certificate and annex 805 (Form 100 page 18). [C · S14]
- **Apprenticeship aid**: the Employment Fund pays the employer 27% of the gross apprenticeship allowance (DAP/DT) or 40% (CCP); request before 1 July after the apprenticeship year. [C · S15]
