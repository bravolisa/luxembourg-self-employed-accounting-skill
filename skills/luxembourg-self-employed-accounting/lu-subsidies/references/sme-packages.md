# SME Packages and Fit 4 programmes

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `figures/` win if they differ._

Two different programmes; do not mix them up.

## SME Packages (Ministry of the Economy with the chambers)

- Packages: **Digital, AI, Cybersecurity, Service, Sustainability** (and RSE). [C · S31]
- The State pays **70%** of eligible costs. [C · S11]
- The project must cost **€3,000 to €25,000 excluding VAT** – a grant of €2,100 to €17,500. [C · S11]
- Eligible: businesses with a business permit that meet the SME criteria and have their registered office in Luxembourg. The official pages set **no minimum number of employees**. [C · S11] Whether a sole trader without staff is accepted in practice: open point SUB-O-09.
- A **pre-analysis** by the House of Entrepreneurship (Chamber of Commerce) or eHandwierk (Chamber of Skilled Trades) is **mandatory**; they help fill in the request. [C · S11]
- The business **chooses its own provider**; Guichet names no accredited list. [C · S11]
- The business pays the provider and is **reimbursed 70% after** the package is implemented and evaluated. [C · S11]
- From 15 September 2026 the law limits requests to one per programme – two for programmes with environmental impact or implementing a digital tool – over three calendar years. [C · S6]

## Fit 4 programmes (Luxinnovation)

- **Fit 4 AI, Cybersecurity, Digital, Performance, Sustainability** co-finance a diagnosis by a **consultant referenced by Luxinnovation**: aid of €1,000 to €100,000 per project. [C · S20]
- The application must be filed (by Luxinnovation, on MyGuichet) **before any binding commitment to the consultant** – do not sign the quote first. [C · S20]
- The Fit 4 rates (above the 50% cap of the consultancy aid) rest on the **de minimis** regime, not on the SME aid law: Guichet's « Références légales » name Regulation (EU) 2023/2831 and the law of 20 December 2019. [C · S20]

## Which one?

| | SME Packages | Fit 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Typical size | Small projects (€3,000–25,000) | Larger diagnoses (aid up to €100,000) |
| Provider | Free choice | Luxinnovation-referenced consultant |
| Entry point | House of Entrepreneurship / eHandwierk pre-analysis | Luxinnovation |
| Timing | Request before the project; paid after | Request before signing the quote |
