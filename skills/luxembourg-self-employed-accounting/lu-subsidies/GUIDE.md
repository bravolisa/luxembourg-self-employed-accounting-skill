# Luxembourg subsidies for small businesses

_Figures in this file: 2025–2026. The year files in `references/figures/` win if they differ._

Follow lu-tax-core for triage, labels and answer shape. **[C]** Confirmed, **[I]** Interpretation, **[P]** Practice, **[O]** Open, **[B]** BravoLisa convention; source ids in [references/sources.md](references/sources.md).

## Before answering

1. **When** was (or will) the application be filed, and has anything been ordered or signed yet? The SME aid law changed on 15 September 2026 – [2025 rules](references/figures/2025.md), [2026 both periods](references/figures/2026.md), [2027](references/figures/2027.md).
2. **Activity and permit**: commercial, craft or liberal profession; business permit held since when?
3. **Size**: staff and turnover, including linked companies.
4. **Project**: start-up, equipment (new or replacement), digital tools, advice, training, hiring.
5. For the start-up aid: other income of the founders, premises outside the home.

## Rules people get wrong most often

- **Apply before you sign**: a signed quote or order before the application kills investment, consultancy, fair and Fit 4 aid. Only replacement investments can be claimed afterwards. [C · S1, S5, S20, S6]
- The SME aid rules **changed on 15 September 2026**: the old €20,000 / €10,000 caps on later requests for miscellaneous investments no longer exist. [C · S6, S8]
- A **minimum aid** applies: 20% of a €3,000 machine is €600 – below the €1,000 minimum, so no aid. [C · S2, S5]
- The **start-up aid** needs own premises outside the home and **no other income** of the founders (no salary, pension or unemployment benefit); it is €2,000 a month for up to six months. [C · S10]
- Repayment of the start-up aid is triggered by selling **more than 50%** of the shares within one year – not 25%. [C · S10]
- Most liberal professions are excluded from SME aid (not architects and engineers); consultants usually hold a commercial permit, but whether « experts comptables et de conseil » excludes them is open. [C · S6; open point SUB-O-01]
- **SME Packages** let you choose any provider; **Fit 4** requires a Luxinnovation-referenced consultant. [C · S11, S20]
- An aided asset is depreciated on its price **minus the grant**. [C · S28]
- Two de minimis ceilings exist (EU €300,000 rolling, Luxembourg law €200,000 over fiscal years) – do not quote only one. [C · S17, S16]
